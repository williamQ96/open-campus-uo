Data and accuracy

Grey ground is interpolated from classified ground returns. Most white structures are historical LiDAR-derived candidates and may include canopy, split roofs or missed buildings.

Names use public building facts and independently curated approximate locators. Name-to-roof matches still need site verification. Post-survey or unextracted buildings use explicitly approximate envelopes, not measured reconstructions. The scene includes surrounding context; its square extent is not an official property boundary.
