# Open character sources

Kenney, [Animated Characters 3](https://kenney-assets.itch.io/animated-characters-3), **CC0-1.0**. Acquired 2026-10-31 through the creator's free itch.io download. The original ZIP, its License.txt and selected unchanged FBX/PNG inputs are retained. ZIP and exported-file checksums are in [manifest.json](manifest.json). Only the human male texture is used; other skins in the original ZIP are unused. See [the texture register](../reference-images/manifest.json) and [character build script](../../scripts/build-campus-character.py).

The rigged body, source animations and source texture remain CC0. Separately authored green/gold materials and backpack are CC BY 4.0. Original procedural vehicles/riders are also CC BY 4.0; their editable generator lives in the upstream browser template (`src/props.js`) and the pinned downstream copy. Model export metadata retains credits. No school logos or mascots are included.
