# Alder Suites and two Bushnell exteriors

The local viewer adds Alder Suites and replaces the source volumes for Morse Center and Pomajevich Hall. The 2024 OSIP aerial, City of Eugene outlines and municipal address points register all three. The outline of Alder Suites has zero overlap with every preceding building footprint. The other two keep their original scene IDs. The city outlines overlap the old Morse and Pomajevich footprints by 1,965.902 m² and 484.850 m², respectively. [City buildings](https://gis.eugene-or.gov/arcgis/rest/services/PWM/COE_Basemap_Tiled_Pro/MapServer/3) · [City address points](https://services3.arcgis.com/F7NiRLGNbA2hh7gE/ArcGIS/rest/services/EugAddress/FeatureServer/0) · [Source URLs, hashes and editable dimensions](../data/metadata/alder-bushnell-exterior-review.json).

Equipment dimensions, the threshold and openings away from the photographed west side are approximate. Some west-side openings, the northern low enclosure and the threshold are estimated. The modeled ramp and approach do not establish real accessibility.
