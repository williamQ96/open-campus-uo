# Pioneer Cemetery screened interior grave plots

The registration covers three areas within the cemetery. The first is the northern interior east of the existing grass trace, the second is between Walton and Cherry avenues east of Kelley Drive, and the third is north of Dorris Avenue. The 2023 and 2025 county images show the clearest plot outlines in different areas. The 2017 and 2021 images provide alternate sun angles and visibility under the trees. The National Register plat provides the historic grid. New geometry follows visible features and open segments on the contemporary imagery. The candidate screen removes duplicates, points within surviving curbs and features without sufficient visibility. The west end of one Walton–Cherry curb stops before a retained tree stem. The exporter checks the new details against the terrain, plot bounds and registered trunks. The grass traces retain open ends where crowns obscure the alignment. The named gravel roads carry the cemetery routes. Buried curb continuity, species, exact profiles and individual memorial identification need ground evidence. The detail package attaches to the pinned cemetery scene. It installs the three meshes and nine collision envelopes together. The cemetery exporter still reproduces the existing base asset and descriptor byte for byte.

Desktop and phone production previews each verified all 611 local exterior IDs, 384 resources, three clear grass-strip probes and nine marker collisions. Seven control routes reproduce the preceding scene's route geometry and stay at least 18.656 m from the new collidable markers. The closest new curb, strip or marker footprint is 0.266 m from a modeled tree base. The build, 199 unit tests, six browser checks, two repeat exports, 61 served-file hashes and 507 archive records pass. The detail revision was first verified in the research worktree and was published in the November 27 Pages release.

## Cherry Avenue to Rankin Lane

The southern follow-up uses twelve Lane County tiles from 2017, 2021, 2023 and 2025 between the Conger and Risdon approaches. The 2017 images reveal low, exposed edges beneath shorter shadows, and the 2021–2025 images confirm surviving edges and stone positions. The historic plat provides the plot layout. Twelve low curb groups add 103.09 m of visible trace, and 36 anonymous marker forms represent recurring features in the aerials. Seven taller modeled stones register collision. The extents of buried curbs, stone dimensions and individual identities remain estimates or unresolved. Observations that touch a registered curb, intrude on the roadway or remain ambiguous under canopy are excluded. [Lane County imagery service](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [National Register plat, PDF p. 36](https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/8377666e-d980-4494-bce2-3993f6f19669#page=36) · [Editable registration](../data/metadata/pioneer-cemetery-inner-grid.json).

The route probe exposed a gap in the network at the east end of Cherry Avenue. Two branches add eleven navigation edges over the existing gravel. They connect Kelley Drive to Risdon Lane along 93.89 m of Cherry Avenue, then follow 46.42 m of southern Risdon to the existing Rankin Lane turn. Both branches use the registered road geometry and the parent cemetery's navigation anchors. The existing cemetery surface supplies the walking grade.

Desktop and phone each load 611 exterior IDs and 390 tracked resources. The 67 sampled files match the build. The Conger approach, Kelley approach and Cherry–Risdon route measure 53.313 m, 122.601 m and 85.738 m, respectively. All three stay on the modeled pavement within the 0.13 m fit tolerance and clear the added plot geometry by at least 2.923 m. The closest added feature is 0.779 m from its modeled tree-base envelope. Both exports match the installed bytes. These results are from the research preview. The follow-up is included in the 610-model Pages release.
