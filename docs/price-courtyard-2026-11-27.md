# Price Science Commons west courtyard and overlook

The as-built courtyard has a jog in the west wall, a glass guard along the public plaza and a glazed pavilion face on the east. The university floor map confirms the west jog. Dated Lane County orthophotos and the architect’s exterior and courtyard photographs constrain the plan, the guard and the planted layout. The geographic registration puts the long west edge near east −196.65 m from north 79.03 m to 101.56 m, with an approximately 0.88 m inward jog. [University floor map](https://library.uoregon.edu/floor-map-price-science-commons-psc) · [Architect’s as-built photographs](https://www.opsisarch.com/project/university-of-oregon-allan-price-science-commons-and-research-library/) · [County orthophotos](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer).

The July 2023 DOGAMI terrain raster gives a +1.354 m median on the south terrace and −2.742 m in the central courtyard in the scene frame. A second window in the north gives −2.422 m. The model uses +1.337 m on the upper rim and a lower floor profile ranging from −2.760 m to −2.430 m. The mosaic's native grid is three feet. Sampling its exported 0.25 m cells helps align broad surfaces to the outlines. The older NOAA tile was created in 2017 from a 2013–2015 acquisition. Its older terrace returns predate the completion of Price Science Commons in 2016. [2023 DOGAMI acquisition report](https://pubs.oregon.gov/dogami/ldq/reports/OLC_WillametteValley_2023_NIR_Lidar_Report.pdf) · [Earlier NOAA acquisition record](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/57407) · [Editable controls, source and hashes](../data/metadata/price-courtyard.json).

The assembled landscape adds 72.64 m² of surrounding public paving, a 144.09 m² lower courtyard with 63.45 m² of planted beds, two representative seats, 37.61 m of framed glass guard and the glazed face visible from the courtyard. The seat positions, plant assortment, framing and sheltered floor edges use estimates. The upper navigation graph contains four links from the Onyx Green junction to the south overlook. The lower garden remains visible from the plaza and is outside the outdoor walking graph. The pavilion face supplies the garden boundary. The rest of the pavilion and its indoor approach remain open for separate exterior and interior review.

The terrain assembly replaces the complete 5 m grid cells around the cut only after verifying all three source meshes and loading the courtyard and its Onyx Green predecessor. It stages the 36 terrain faces, 36 foundation faces and 30 lawn faces before replacing any index array. Loss of the courtyard asset or a required source leaves the earlier terrain in use.
