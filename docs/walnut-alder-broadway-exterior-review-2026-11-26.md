# Walnut, Alder and Broadway exterior review

The local viewer adopts three L2 exteriors under their original scene IDs. City addresses, building outlines and 2024 OSIP imagery connect each reference to its scene candidate. The city outlines overlap the previous candidates by 200.906 m² at Walnut, 521.219 m² at Alder and 146.324 m² at Broadway. Broadway uses the dated roof perimeter visible in the aerial, which extends west of the city outline. - **1401 Walnut St, Transportation Services** keeps the deep brown hip and restores the three gridded west windows, south office doors and bike hoops. UO confirms the office address. The south entry threshold, rack dimensions and openings away from the observed frontage use estimates. The office and columns have separate collision. Dimensions, openings and threshold are approximate. The freestanding motel sign and the later redevelopment remain outside this dated model. The city's Aspire decision confirms approval in November 2024. Construction or demolition since the observation still needs verification. ## Roof and scene verification

The comparison ray-casts both geometries at the same historical surface cells. It reports median absolute height differences in meters on cells that hit both. The revised roofs hit 2,655 of 2,682 selected cells across the three surfaces. The nine Broadway misses per surface fall beyond the revised perimeter. The other two models hit all selected cells. The replacement removes 412 source triangles and preserves all surviving indices, vertex attributes and 84 peer IDs in three batches. It replaces the Alder and Broadway context roof treatments. The 7,710-edge scan finds no added route conflict. The nearest edge is 4.2304 m from the new collision footprints.

Desktop and phone production previews verify three searches, three estimated walkable approaches and eight collision probes. Three of the probes cover open space beneath the canopy. Corrupting the third source anchor restores all three previous models and both prior roof treatments. Staged, adopted and served bytes match. Formatting, 195 unit tests, seven focused browser tests, production build, 339 open archive hashes and 272 viewer pins pass. The assets are original editable geometry. Image references stay external.
