# Vivian Olum Child Development Center exterior review

The default scene registers the main building as UO 165 (`roof-1c631d7c`) and the annex as UO 164 (`roof-1d1be960`). The exterior models cover 832.24 m² and 173.39 m² respectively. The main building has stepped gray gables, vertical siding, framed windows, roof vents and a narrow west walk. The annex has a pale shallow gable and a timber-framed covered approach on its south side. Each building keeps its own selectable ID and modeled entrance. The clear passage and open yards between the two remain visible in the rendered comparison. [University map](https://map.uoregon.edu/) · [UO building inventory](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-09/02_buildings_by_number_fy2025-1.pdf) · [University architecture archive](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/vivian-olum-child-development-center).

The university outlines were adjusted to align with the 2024 aerial and 2017 building-edge returns. The main building shifts 1.72 m west and 1.36 m north. The annex shifts 2.45 m west and 1.62 m north. The aerial shows the roof steps, annex canopy and west-side paved edge. The UO exterior photo supports the general vertical siding, dark skirt and light window frames. Its limited coverage leaves portions of window rhythm, porch framing, door positions and exterior materials approximate. The source street width in the scene is also approximate.
