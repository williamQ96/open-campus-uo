# Pilot-area globe lamp family — 2026-11-04

Eight existing inferred globe lamps now have simple stepped bases, globe supports and top caps. Components use a reusable low-poly column primitive, merge into the verified existing metal furniture batch, and preserve the source pole and globe geometry, positions and original GLB. The remaining three lamps were not altered: a five-point vertical screen hit a modeled building at one, and two sit on modeled paving where current placement and clearance are uncertain. A lamp on paving is not necessarily erroneous; these locations require evidence.

The forms are generic interpretations informed by the previously archived [2019 Anstett photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anstett_Hall,_University_of_Oregon,_Eugene_-_DPLA_-_7f05aff2c79e236edd0bed0104b6f145.jpg) (Gary Halvorson/Oregon State Archives, CC BY 4.0), not a survey of the eight lamps. Source positions and heights were read from the original mesh. Limited five-point samples at each candidate were compared with building, ground and vegetation meshes; these are not a full 3D clearance, ground-contact, navigation or accessibility assessment. Existing scene ground is generally 0.02–0.07 m above the original pole-base height at the sampled centers, and the stepped base intersects it. Styles, actual positions, dimensions and current condition remain unverified. PROP-01 remains level 1. No source image or third-party geometry is embedded. Original model output is CC BY 4.0 and procedural code Apache-2.0.
