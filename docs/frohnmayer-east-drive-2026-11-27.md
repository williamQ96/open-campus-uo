# Frohnmayer Music east service drive and Pioneer Cemetery southwest walk

Four Lane County orthophotos from 2017, 2021, 2023 and 2025 show a service drive along the cemetery's west side. The drive connects the parking court beside Southwest Campus Green to East 18th Avenue. It is separated from Conger Lane by the cemetery verge. The southwest corner has a curved concrete walk and an access aisle, while a second sidewalk bends along the building toward a small apron. The July 2024 ground view confirms the curved walk next to the planted cemetery verge. The campus plan documents Douglas firs along the cemetery edge. This package retains the existing cemetery trees. [Lane County aerials](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [July 2024 ground view](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=dyJn7khw4HS8-qqi744JDQ&heading=19.16) · [Campus plan, printed pp. 95–96](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/principle_12_design_area_special_conditions_4th_edition_1.pdf) · [UO parking map](https://transportation.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-07/ParkingZonesEspanol2025%20%281%29.pdf).

The reconstruction includes 1,533.84 m² of asphalt and 131.62 m² of concrete walks. Two raised parking islands and the curved entrance bed provide 206.44 m² of planted surfaces. The markings include 11 stall dividers, an angled striped access aisle and two accessible symbols. Red curbs follow the drive edges and open at the southern walk transition. The shade-obscured edges, curb heights, planting clusters and transition grades use image-based estimates. [Editable measurements and imagery receipts](../data/metadata/frohnmayer-east-drive.json) · [Exporter](../scripts/build-frohnmayer-east-drive.py).

Two sidewalk branches add 23 pedestrian edges from existing East 18th sidewalk nodes. The western branch follows the building walk and the eastern branch ends at the access aisle. A third branch adds 23 edges on the shared asphalt driveway and ties into the existing Lot 20 walk. Southwest Campus Green and the street keep their pinned meshes and route coverage. The revised grass verge restores 676.35 m² of ground cover based on the installed road triangles and their visible shoulder. When the new site paths install, the viewer retires 30 inferred road edges and uses the registered asphalt connection.
