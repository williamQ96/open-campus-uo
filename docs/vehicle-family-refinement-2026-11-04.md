# Generic vehicle family refinement — 2026-11-04

The procedural car, SUV, van and pickup now share readable grille, windscreen wipers, door seams, handles and lower trim; the van also has a sliding-door track. The pickup windshield had been inside its cabin and is now just outside the front wall. Four types are refined through the same runtime generator, so instances inherit the improvement without changing their placements, routes, collision shapes, rider geometry or campus layout. Existing visible bounding boxes are unchanged. They remain generic, unbranded interpretations, not representations of specific vehicles or a surveyed UO fleet. PROP-02 remains level 1.

UO [Motor Pool](https://transportation.uoregon.edu/uo-motor-pool) and its [2024–25 annual report](https://transportation.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-11/Annual%20Report%202025%20Draft_7_7_2025.pdf) supply context for vehicle categories only; no university photograph, logo, model or texture was copied. Original generated models are CC BY 4.0 and code is Apache-2.0. [Before](images/vehicle-family-before.png) / [after](images/vehicle-family-after.png) show the four generic models in a neutral review scene, not actual campus locations.

The export now includes the previously runtime-only SUV, for six total kinds: car, SUV, van, pickup, bicycle and scooter. The new `assets/props/campus-props-vehicles-2026-11-04.blend` is an editable snapshot of that GLB, with its source hash recorded inside; the procedural JavaScript is still the source of truth. Prior snapshots remain intact. The versioned Blender importer accepts both five-mesh legacy and six-mesh current exports and refuses to overwrite outputs.

Verification: 66 Node tests, production build, 4 targeted browser tests including activity density and navigation; four-type visual comparison; all visible model bounds retained; bicycle and scooter positions/colors compared unchanged; Blender reopen and fresh GLB import matched all six mesh coordinates, face counts and transforms. Vertex counts rose from 1,536 to 2,112 for car/SUV, 1,536 to 2,184 for van and 1,680 to 2,184 for pickup; no hardware performance claim is made. Vehicle placement and styles remain illustrative.
