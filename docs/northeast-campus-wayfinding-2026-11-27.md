# Agate–Franklin gateway and Onyx map station

The university identifies both sites in its northeast campus landscape inventory. Four Lane County orthophoto dates and July 2024 ground views register the gateway and its planting border. The 2026 CPFM photograph shows the arched panel, paired ornamental posts, pale lettering and gold medallion. The junction apron and western marked Franklin crossing follow the same registered ground imagery. [Northeast Campus Diagnosis, printed p. 15](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/nediagnosis_sept_17_2012rdcd2.pdf#page=19) · [CPFM gateway photograph](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/exterior-features#signage) · [July 2024 gateway view](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=gr9iuLx6ccFYByGgTg6X5w&heading=254.31) · [Lane County aerials](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer).

The June 2024 and July 2019 views show the Onyx set on a low stone planter beside two entrance bollards. Independent sight lines meet within the registered island near east −196.70 m and north 155.60 m. The convex post caps, spanning light hood, cabinet width and 54-inch post spacing follow the outdoor sign plan. The display contains a schematic made from the project's building and route geometry. The gateway uses a simplified relief disc and original lettering geometry. [June 2024 Onyx view](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=pBjyKwL7OgQt-yhSi5SHow&heading=202.53) · [July 2019 Onyx view](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=hU4UzfMBIU4lNRqpd5M-Ug&heading=191.38) · [Campus map station standard, p. 5](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2024-10/uo-sign-plan-2024.pdf#page=5).

The campus GIS records a horse chestnut and maidenhair tree on the map side, and three black tupelos east of the approach. The model uses their registered trunk positions and inventory dimensions. The crowns are representative. The apron, driveway and low planting edges follow four aerial dates and both ground views. [Campus tree inventory](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/trees).

Hedge profiles, sign height, post ornament, planter stone and partly obscured curb edges use estimates. The Oregon Hall map station, Campus Heart kiosk and East 13th Avenue information point remain in the evidence register pending stronger current placement views.
