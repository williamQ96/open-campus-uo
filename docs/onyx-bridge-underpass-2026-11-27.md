# Onyx Bridge ground passage and north garden walks

The campus accessibility map shows the north–south walk passing under Onyx Bridge. The north garden and approach to the existing Franklin-side map station are legible in the 2017, 2021, 2023 and 2025 Lane County orthophotos. The 2025 campus survey and the Campus Plan identify the bridge and the connection through Onyx Green. The accessibility map registration has a 0.151 m RMS residual across ten controls. [Campus accessibility map](https://mapapps.uoregon.edu/campusMaps/accessibility.pdf) · [2025 campus survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-01/onyxbridge_2025.pdf) · [Campus Plan](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/principle_12_design_area_special_conditions_4th_edition_1.pdf) · [County orthophotos](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer).

The model connects the existing Onyx Green west walk to the apron of the existing map station and restores both walks around the north garden. It adds 203.28 m² of paving, 130.25 m² of terrain transitions, three planted beds totaling 108.94 m² and 27 walking links. The bed outline uses the 2023 orthophoto where shadows are lighter. Two low trees follow campus inventory entries 2896, Fernleaf Full-Moon Maple, and 4087, Fragrant Snowbell. Their crowns, the low planting and the curb sections use estimates. The northern endpoint stays on the map apron at east −191.60 m, north 156.24 m. The bridge deck, mapped ground footprints, piers and map station retain their existing meshes. [Tree inventory](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/trees) · [Editable controls and source hashes](../data/metadata/onyx-bridge-underpass.json).

The July 2023 DOGAMI raster helps fit exposed approaches. Airborne returns under the bridge are screened. The approximately 15 m covered passage uses interpolation between the exposed approach controls and matches the retained north and south surfaces. The graph requires the Onyx Green and northeast wayfinding packages. The new ground and navigation fall back together when either dependency or the passage mesh fails. [DOGAMI acquisition report](https://pubs.oregon.gov/dogami/ldq/reports/OLC_WillametteValley_2023_NIR_Lidar_Report.pdf) · [Runtime descriptor](../apps/web-preview/public/scene/onyx-bridge-underpass-review/site.json).

## Verification

Desktop and phone production previews each match 611 exterior IDs, 410 successful tracked responses and 89 file hashes. The bridge route is 54.025 m, the west garden leg to the map apron is 33.426 m, and the longer Onyx Green approach is 75.213 m. All three stay within 0.13 m of registered paving, with at least 0.857 m clearance to the modeled collision envelopes. Each profile passed 1,133 standing-height and collision samples. The five opening probes give a minimum modeled headroom of 3.804 m. The bridge soffit and screened pavement grades still need field confirmation.

Three focused browser cases, 201 unit tests, format checks, production build, 533 archive checks and viewer pins pass. The GLB, descriptor and route coverage reproduce byte for byte. [Browser tests](../apps/web-preview/tests/browser/onyx-bridge-underpass.spec.js).
