# Regional fern form refinement — 2026-11-04

The shared procedural fern now has ten curved fronds and denser paired, pointed leaflets, a stylized interpretation of regional sword-fern form. Oregon State University's [Northwestern forest understory study](https://andrewsforest.oregonstate.edu/pubs/pdf/pub4949.pdf) describes western sword fern as having arching fronds with many pinnae; [UO's Willamette River natural-area list](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/wrna-plants-and-animals) documents the species in that natural area. Neither source identifies the plants at these three illustrative model positions. The model does not reproduce a photograph or imply measured species, morphology or current planting.

The same reusable procedural source is used for all three previously adopted fern clusters; locations, scale, material, source-pinned suppression and the scene GLB remain unchanged. The other ambiguous clusters remain untouched. The procedural source `apps/web-preview/src/fern-geometry.js` generates runtime geometry directly, so no separate exported GLB is used for this detail; the earlier implementation is retained at `assets/vegetation-refinement/fern-geometry-before-arch-2026-11-04.js`. Original code is Apache-2.0. No source imagery is bundled. ENV-03 remains level 1.

The generated radius at the adopted 1.08 scale is approximately 1.0801 m, inside the earlier 1.35 m five-point screen; that screen is not a full physical clearance, route or accessibility assessment. The new component has 680 triangles versus 396 before, an increase of 852 triangles across three clusters. The existing material batch absorbs the geometry without a new draw object. Dimensions, species, terrain contact and actual current locations remain unverified. Three scene views, unit tests, build, browser scene and activity/navigation checks, and the existing activity-disabled draw-call budget were used; no hardware benchmark is claimed. No building interior or layout changed.
