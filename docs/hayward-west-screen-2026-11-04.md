# Hayward Field west-stand screening — 2026-11-04, not integrated

[UO's venue description](https://hayward.uoregon.edu/about) describes separate east and west stands, an open structure and a wood roof; [UO's overview](https://news.uoregon.edu/hayward) notes timber beams and a transparent roof. The existing `context-oc-hayward` is explicitly a 28 × 150 m approximate solid rectangular west-stand placeholder, not the entire stadium, track, tower or a surveyed roof. Its dimensions, height and exact placement are unverified. No reference imagery or third-party geometry is redistributed.

An isolated 126-box study of a pale shallow roof and wood-colored ribs was tested over the existing placeholder. The first proposed roof exceeded the existing source-bound guard and was rejected; the guard was not changed. A shallower variant loaded but the candidate overview still reads as a long solid warehouse rather than the open and asymmetric Hayward grandstand described by UO. For comparison, [existing overview](images/hayward-overview-before-2026-11-04.png) and [street view](images/hayward-west-before-2026-11-04.png) are retained. The candidate is archived as isolated JSON; it is not referenced by the runtime and is not an approved reconstruction. No building, collision, campus layout or grade was changed. Hayward stays level 1.

A more useful next step is to establish the registered extent of the actual west stand and roof, evaluate a replaceable exterior shell rather than merely topping the solid placeholder, and check selection, navigation, performance and independent export. The candidate does not reconstruct the stadium bowl, stands, track, tower, entrance or true roof profile. No interior work was undertaken.
