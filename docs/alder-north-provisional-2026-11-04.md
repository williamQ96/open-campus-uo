The UO [Creative Writing page](https://humanities.uoregon.edu/creative-writing/graduate-program) shows an 818 entrance with white masonry, a projecting columned porch and windows; its photo was consulted visually, not copied or redistributed. The broad visual vocabulary is supported; exact dimensions, glazing, entrance, columns, masonry, present-day conditions and other facades remain unmeasured. In particular the photo shows oval windows and an arched entry that the simplified overlay does not reproduce. The generic runtime entry remains an estimated marker, not a depiction of the real entrance.
