# Prince Lucien Campbell Hall east courtyard

Four Lane County orthophotos from 2017, 2021, 2023 and 2025 show the upper court, the east stair, a square western bed and the planting around the art setting. The 2023 tile has the clearest contrast across the stair and the round feature in the east bed. The university's site plan shows the earlier sculpture in a planted setting. Its updated survey identifies James Lee Hansen's *Vigil* as the sculpture installed in that courtyard. The first pass inferred a low circular footing from the aerials. The December 2023 ground views support a darker, raised support, stair handrails and the south guardrail. [Lane County imagery](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [UO site plan and historical survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-01/plccampbell_2025.pdf) · [Artist catalogue](https://jamesleehansen.com/artwork/g26-01-falconer/).

The package models 497.51 m² of paving including a 24.79 m² inset, 66.55 m² of planting in three edged beds, four stair treads and a short retaining return. It uses the university's tree records for Japanese snowbell 607 and Kousa dogwood 605. The shrub forms, tread profiles and outer edges beneath trees are fitted. The deck keeps the existing entrance and bench support datums and blends back to the Quad walk. Within 1,192 historical NOAA ground returns, the paired upper and lower windows have 74 and 28 points and their median elevations differ by 0.58 m. The modeled drop along the stair center is about 0.44 m in the viewer datum. [NOAA point source](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/laz/geoid18/8866/44123A1309.copc.laz) · [Campus tree inventory](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/trees).

Three branches add 14 navigation edges from the registered Quad walk to both existing approach points and around the planting. The site uses the original PLC exterior package and pins the Quad route recipe. If the court geometry fails its hash check, its layers are skipped together. If the Quad does not load, the court geometry stays visible and the viewer retains the previous outdoor routing. ## Vigil catalog evidence

The artist's catalogue identifies the work installed at UO as G30. The museum's fall 2015 member magazine records the PLC garden installation and identifies the work as a 1985 bronze measuring 95½ × 25¾ × 16 in (2.426 × 0.654 × 0.406 m). The Maryhill Museum retrospective shows Hansen alongside the same 1985 work before the PLC installation. The December 9, 2023 photograph shows the same arrangement of the bronze forms as the Maryhill Museum catalogue and locates the work against the PLC window bays. The photographer captioned it as The Falconer. The university survey, artist catalogue and visual match identify it as Vigil. A geometry source suitable for redistribution is still needed. [Artist catalogue](https://jamesleehansen.com/artwork/g26-01-falconer/) · [JSMA member magazine, fall 2015, back cover, archived copy](https://doczz.net/doc/2168478/fall-2015---jordan-schnitzer-museum-of-art) · [Maryhill Museum exhibition catalogue, printed p. 12](https://www.maryhillmuseum.org/wp-content/uploads/2014/03/JamesLeeHansen_Booklet_F.pdf#page=14).

## Plinth, railing and rose bed refit

Randy Nishimura's close photograph and his wide view from the Quad carry timestamps 27 seconds apart. The close view shows the dark support above the rose bed. The model fits a gently tapered section approximately 0.81 m above the planting surface, with a roughly 0.68 × 0.66 m footprint. Rose canes obscure portions of the lower perimeter, and the side profile and mount hardware remain estimates. The 2025 university survey plan identifies roses in the same bed. Seven modeled groups use representative winter growth, with cluster centers and spread estimated from the photographs. The wide photograph shows rails at both ends of the stair and a picket guard along the south retaining return. The rails are fitted to the existing four treads and both landings. The south guard runs 5.72 m. [December 2023 ground photographs](https://sworegonarchitect.blogspot.com/2023/12/eugenearchitecturealphabet-p.html) · [UO survey site plan, p. 4](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-01/plccampbell_2025.pdf#page=4).

## Verification and status

Desktop and phone production previews each load all 611 local exterior IDs, 378 resources and all 13 new waypoints. The four courtyard routes measure 7.22 m, 32.63 m, 23.80 m and 19.12 m. Each stays within the 0.13 m tolerance of modeled pavement and clears the new raised beds and retaining return. Two asset exports and two coverage exports reproduce byte for byte, and 54 sampled preview files match the build. The courtyard is included in the Memorial Quadrangle Pages release with the 610-model inventory.

For the plinth and rail refit, desktop and phone each matched 611 exterior IDs, 390 resources and 67 served-file hashes. Both stair-side collision probes close and the center probe stays clear. All four route samples stay within the 0.13 m paved tolerance and clear modeled obstacles by at least 0.895 m. The paving mesh and route coverage rings match the pre-refit data. Both repeat exports and the updated coverage file match the installed bytes. These results are from the research preview. The refit is included in the 610-model Pages release.
