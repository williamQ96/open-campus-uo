# HEDCO courtyard landscape

Four Lane County orthophotos from 2017, 2021, 2023 and 2025 register the HEDCO courtyard lawn, paving and planter edges. A 2014 University of Oregon courtyard study documents the layout and furnishings. A campus plan photograph provides an earlier view. The campus tree inventory identifies Prairie Fire Crab 4597 in the south garden. [Lane County orthophotos](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [Courtyard field study, PDF p. 108](https://scholarsbank.uoregon.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/a054136d-556e-4939-a611-daed2b31686a/content#page=108) · [Campus plan photograph](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-05/table-of-contents_2025.pdf#page=2) · [Campus tree inventory](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/trees).

The overlay adds 350.62 m² of lawn, 623.67 m² of concrete paving with joints, two raised east planters covering 28.81 m², a 192.85 m² south garden and a 32.61 m west parapet. Three benches, three green pedestrian lamps, the registered crabapple and representative low plantings complete the grounds. Nine bounds register collision for the raised planters, parapet and furnishings. Two sampled walks stay within 0.13 m of modeled paving and at least 0.75 m from the collision bounds. [Editable registration](../data/metadata/hedco-courtyard-landscape.json) · [Deterministic exporter](../scripts/build-hedco-courtyard-landscape.py).

The historical NOAA lawn sample has a median scene elevation of 7.767 m. The west parking sample has a median of 4.397 m. The landscape uses the existing modeled courtyard datum, approximately 0.55 m below the historical lawn sample, to meet the building and existing walking surfaces. The west entrance threshold, exact grades and small fixture dimensions remain estimates. The field study records art in the east courtyard. The artwork geometry still needs a reusable source. ## Verification

The verified HEDCO descriptor pins the original exterior and courtyard. The landscape falls back independently or with its HEDCO predecessor when either asset fails to load. The staging export regenerates the same mesh and descriptor bytes. The package targets the 610-model GitHub Pages scene.
