# University Street from East 15th to Southgate

The campus landscape survey and campus plan describe University Street as a pedestrian axis with curbside parking and street trees between East 15th and East 18th. Lane County aerials from 2017, 2021 and 2025, along with street views from 2021, July 2024 and June 2026, establish the two sidewalks, parking islands, East 15th paired-line crossings and the fence by the Outdoor Program Barn. The Pioneer Cemetery tour and stadium venue records identify the adjoining landmarks. [University Street Axis survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/university_street_axis_06_12_07.pdf) · [Campus Plan Principle 12](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/principle_12_design_area_special_conditions_4th_edition_1.pdf) · [Lane County orthophotos](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [Pioneer Cemetery tour](https://eugenepioneercemetery.org/self-guided-tour/) · [Jane Sanders Stadium](https://goducks.com/sports/2011/6/21/210828087).

The package includes 7,010.17 m² of roadway and 2,273.73 m² of sidewalks and approaches. It adds parking and bicycle paint, two paired-line crossings, eight roadside fixtures and 59.44 m of fencing. It places 24 live trees from the campus inventory, including eleven Pyramidal English oaks. The parking layout, tree crowns, fixture dimensions and pavement elevations use estimates. The photo and aerial references inform original geometry. [Campus tree inventory](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/trees) · [Editable registration and dated source references](../data/metadata/university-south-axis.json).

Five branches contribute 76 navigation edges. The west sidewalk runs below the existing Dorris stair and connects around the cemetery wall to Rankin Lane. The east sidewalk reaches the Jane Sanders and Howe Field approach. The south end uses the brick Southgate crossing. Two independent exports match byte for byte. Both desktop and phone production previews matched all 610 exterior IDs to mesh geometry and loaded 362 app and scene resources. The two sidewalk routes measure 340.81 m and 341.01 m. All 74 waypoints registered, ten paving probes were clear and four fixture probes reported collision. Seven paired production views cover both ends of the corridor, the parking islands, the stadium and court frontages, the cemetery stair and the north tree row.
