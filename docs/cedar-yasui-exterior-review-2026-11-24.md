# Cedar and Yasui Hall exterior review

The paired exterior replaces the earlier Cedar and Yasui placeholders under their existing scene IDs `context-oc-cedar` and `context-oc-yasui`. The editable models follow the UO 205 and 206 outlines, the 2024 aerial and the completed-building photographs. Both are L2 under the project's [Knight Library comparison](knight-library-registration-2026-11-23.md). [University map](https://map.uoregon.edu/) · [Cedar Hall](https://housing.uoregon.edu/cedar) · [Yasui Hall](https://housing.uoregon.edu/yasui) · [Rowell Brokaw and Mithun project](https://rowellbrokaw.com/uo-hamilton-walton-phase-2).

| Model | Registered wall footprint | Main exterior elements |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Cedar | 2,620.504 m² | North and south housing wings, a perpendicular cross wing and a lower projection, glazed entrances and living floors, and the adjacent low glazed courtyard pavilion |
| Yasui | 2,650.102 m² | Long west wing and north and south arms, light and striped metal panels, brick piers, a glazed lounge bridge and a ground-level breezeway |

The campus outlines were shifted by 2.48 m west and 0.96 m north for Cedar, and 2.32 m west and 1.28 m north for Yasui. Cedar's four approach anchors use doorway observations from the university first-floor map. Yasui's open portal is confirmed by the east-side photo and the mapped recesses. Its two doorway anchors use visual estimates. The courtyard pavilion's roof was traced in plan and its walls were inferred under the overhang. Roof elevations, individual windows, paths, pavilion wall lines and doorstep dimensions remain approximate. These halls opened after the 2017 height raster. [2024 OSIP aerial service](https://imagery.oregonexplorer.info/arcgis/rest/services/OSIP_2024/OSIP_2024_SL/ImageServer) · [Yasui breezeway photograph](https://housing.uoregon.edu/yasui) · [University opening and naming record](https://news.uoregon.edu/content/uo-board-names-residence-hall-receives-strategic-plan-update).

The north and south lawns, approximately 1,258 m² of traced paths and approaches, and 23 trees located using the campus inventory restore the shared outdoor rooms. The aligned 2018 and 2024 imagery identifies three old surface models as Walton Complex roofs. Their three pinned triangle ranges are omitted together with adoption of the replacement. The ledger retains their old IDs as historical records. [2018 OSIP aerial service](https://imagery.oregonexplorer.info/arcgis/rest/services/OSIP_2018/OSIP_2018_SL/ImageServer) · [University demolition notice](https://blogs.uoregon.edu/cpfmnotifications/2021/06/).

Seventeen paired views cover the wing profiles, openings, courtyard pavilion and walks. The preserved triangles in both affected context batches match in position, normal and color. All 7,710 active route edges clear the new ground footprints. A 40-point collision sweep across the former obstruction is clear, with the flanking wall and the pavilion blocked as modeled. Rays through the opening pass at walking height and hit the glazed bridge above. Six approaches were walkable in desktop and mobile production checks. Invalid model data, a wrong triangle anchor and an invalid ground footprint each restored the previous group.

Yasui carries two explicit ground footprints under one complete roof footprint. This retains the building selection and its full minimap outline while the ground-level collision geometry excludes the walkway. Run `python scripts/build-cedar-yasui-site-review.py --audit <audit.json>` from the project root.
