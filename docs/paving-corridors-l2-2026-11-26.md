# Lawns, paving, roads and paths at L2

The local ENV-05 package replaces the straight link through Memorial Quadrangle with the northern X, the southern ring and their side walks. It fits 1,905 m² of concrete and 4,534 m² of lawn to the 2024 aerial and the university landscape survey. The source mesh is clipped at the work boundary. The adjacent scene, including the Knight Library approaches, uses the retained source geometry and authored site meshes. Six circular tree pits fit the existing modeled stems. The material uses the scene's existing joint and lawn treatment. [Editable site layout](../data/metadata/paving-corridors-l2.json) · [University of Oregon landscape survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/memorial_quad_.pdf) · [Oregon imagery service](https://imagery.oregonexplorer.info/arcgis/rest/services/OSIP_2024/OSIP_2024_SL/ImageServer).

The two campus corridor batches cover all 139 active source features. Of these, 96 have pavement widths matched to City of Eugene records over part of the centerline. The match accepts 5,391 short spans with 42.8 km of combined source centerline lengths, including aliases. Unmatched spans retain the source width. Adjacent widths taper to shared joints. Asphalt, asphalt overlay, concrete and gravel use distinct matte colors. Ground support follows the L2 terrain grid and the existing Ford/Arena grade. The existing closure remains active. The City polygons serve as reference checks for the resulting original buffers along the Census centerlines. ## Memorial Quadrangle south walks and Knight Library apron

The 2017, 2021 and 2025 Lane County orthophotos show the two diagonals crossing below the ring. The university plan locates the straight side walks and shows both diagonals returning to those walks. The revision follows those branches and connects them to the east–west apron in front of Knight Library. Canopy obscures the ends of the diagonals, so the last few metres and exact pavement widths are fitted estimates. The central lawn remains open between the crossing and the apron. [University survey, p. 2](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/memorial_quad_.pdf#page=2) · [Lane County orthophotos](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer).

The updated Quad contains 2,201.49 m² of paving and 4,590.51 m² of lawn within its extended boundary. The route patch has 234 nodes and 242 edges. It uses the three existing network anchors and meets the Knight forecourt across 105.08 m² of modeled paving. The same fitted pavement rings govern free-pin connectors. Endpoint checks sample every 0.05 m to catch short gaps around pavement corners. The eight lawn activity paths and the west activity walk use the revised surfaces.

Two repeat exports match byte for byte. Both campus corridor meshes retain their index, position, normal and color buffers. Desktop and phone each load 611 exterior IDs and 376 tracked resources. Fifty-two preview responses match the production build. All six sampled routes stay within 0.13 m of the combined Quad and Knight apron paving. The six routes measure 31.75–59.89 m and cover the diagonals, both side walks and both library approaches. The south-edge audit checks 66 graph edges. The Memorial Quadrangle Pages release includes the revision and the subsequent Bailey oak and route refit. [Latest Quad review](memorial-quad-bailey-oaks-2026-11-27.md).

## PLC east courtyard sculpture

The 2025 PLC survey and the campus art guide identify James Lee Hansen's *Vigil* in the east entry courtyard. The 2007 quadrangle survey records Hansen's earlier *The Falconer*, stolen in 2008. The pedestal footprint and access stair are registered from recorded aerials. A close view is still needed for the pedestal section and sculpture geometry. [East courtyard reconstruction](plc-east-court-review-2026-11-27.md). Two UO records differ on whether the Kincaid Field plaque is near the museum's northeast or northwest corner. The landscape survey maps it at the Quad's east edge. The design uses the mapped east-walk symbol as an approximate position. Exact field coordinates and inscription layout await a close view. [Plaque reconstruction](memorial-quad-plaques-2026-11-27.md).
