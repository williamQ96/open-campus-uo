# Lawns, paving, roads and paths at L2

The local ENV-05 package replaces the straight link through Memorial Quadrangle with the northern X, the southern ring and their side walks. It fits 1,905 m² of concrete and 4,534 m² of lawn to the 2024 aerial and the university landscape survey. The source mesh is clipped at the work boundary. The adjacent scene, including the Knight Library approaches, uses the retained source geometry and authored site meshes. Six circular tree pits fit the existing modeled stems. The material uses the scene's existing joint and lawn treatment. [Editable site layout](../data/metadata/paving-corridors-l2.json) · [University of Oregon landscape survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/memorial_quad_.pdf) · [Oregon imagery service](https://imagery.oregonexplorer.info/arcgis/rest/services/OSIP_2024/OSIP_2024_SL/ImageServer).

The two campus corridor batches cover all 139 active source features. Of these, 96 have pavement widths matched to City of Eugene records over part of the centerline. The match accepts 5,391 short spans with 42.8 km of combined source centerline lengths, including aliases. Unmatched spans retain the source width. Adjacent widths taper to shared joints. Asphalt, asphalt overlay, concrete and gravel use distinct matte colors. Ground support follows the L2 terrain grid and the existing Ford/Arena grade. The existing closure remains active. The City polygons serve as reference checks for the resulting original buffers along the Census centerlines. [City pavement layers](https://gis.eugene-or.gov/arcgis/rest/services/PWE/Transportation/MapServer) · [City data notes](https://www.eugene-or.gov/1352/Maps-and-GIS-Disclaimer).

## Integration

- Replaces 26 edges in the old straight Quad chain with 211 fitted edges. The updated graph reconnects the north and south approach and Johnson Lane. It locates the old junctions by projected coordinates so that it can also use the graph compacted by the University District review.
- The viewer checks the ground, pavement and navigation sources before adopting the meshes and routes together. Missing or changed assets return to the earlier pavement and graph. Use `/?exterior-review=paving-corridors-baseline` on the development preview to see the earlier version with the same site context.
- The road and path GLB polygons improve estimated pavement overlap from 0.7271 to 0.8091 IoU in the examined buffers around matched spans. Within those buffers the projected area outside the City reference falls from 65,702 to 43,650 m². The check uses the pinned legacy GLB before the University District strip change. The replacement coordinates agree with the L2 grid and modeled lift within 0.023 mm before the separate Ford/Arena grade is applied.
- Seven matched render pairs keep the same draw counts. The new mesh adds about 17,000 to 19,000 triangles in the captured frames. Desktop and phone production loads match both staged exports and each produces a 32-point Condon Hall to Knight Library route. A separate end-to-end Quad probe follows the west walk with the viewer's existing approximate endpoint connections.
- Coordinates and widths under tree cover remain approximate. Unmatched roads and paths retain their estimated source widths. Bridge deck heights, contemporary access and painted markings remain outside the evidence for this model.

Run `scripts/build-paving-corridors-l2.py` with the corridor candidate table, grade tie capture, pinned Pipeline `corridors.py` path and output directory. The two exported files and the local viewer changes are uncommitted.
