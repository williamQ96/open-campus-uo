# Kendall Plaza and the south Education promenade

Kendall Plaza occupies the lower court at the HEDCO atrium. The 2014 courtyard study photographs the brick court, three broad east tiers, railings, raised planter and benches. The 2017, 2021 and 2025 county orthophotos show the persistent outlines and the two paths from Education South and Southwest Campus Green. The campus describes the sloping approach that connects the upper and lower grounds.
