# Kendall Plaza and the south Education promenade

Kendall Plaza occupies the lower court at the HEDCO atrium. The 2014 courtyard study photographs the brick court, three broad east tiers, railings, raised planter and benches. The 2017, 2021 and 2025 county orthophotos show the persistent outlines and the two paths from Education South and Southwest Campus Green. The campus describes the sloping approach that connects the upper and lower grounds. ## Unity and Harmony

The college locates Masayuki Nagase's 2009 carved granite installation in Kendall Plaza, on the lawn east of HEDCO and at Clinical Services. The artist lists four sculptural seats about 1.22–1.83 m long and a 9.75 m by 4.88 m freestanding group. The installation photograph from the college looks north toward the plaza and Education Annex. Its gravel foreground, two basins and upright opening match the layout of three persistent features on the south lawn in the 2017, 2021 and 2025 county images. The 2009 college newsletter shows the opening and one basin from the facing view. [College art catalogue](https://education.uoregon.edu/art-college-education-complex) · [Artist's project record](https://www.mnagase.com/portfolio/unity/) · [2009 Education Matters, p. 7](https://scholarsbank.uoregon.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/b05b5a2e-2ff0-4910-ac61-f883b211c5c5/content#page=7).

The 2025 aerial registration places the north basin near `[-622.6, -466.65]`, the opening near `[-624.2, -469.1]` and the south basin near `[-625.55, -472.0]` in campus ENU metres. Two lower-court seat candidates recur near `[-625.9, -403.75]` and `[-625.9, -419.1]`. The February 2026 field report measured one seat at about 1.28 m by 1.10 m by 0.58 m. Its reported coordinates fall about 5 m from the northern aerial candidate. Individual bench identities, exact profiles, reusable artwork geometry and the Clinical Services placement still need evidence. ## South lawn paths and gravel setting

The 2017, 2021 and 2025 orthophotos show the west walk continuing past the art to the sidewalk bend, a separate east walk and the planted strip between them. The college photograph confirms the gray gravel setting and the globe lamps along the west walk. The modeled extension adds 279.98 m² of concrete, 51.98 m² of gravel, 579.48 m² of lawn and 258.65 m² of planting beds. Seven trees use the university tree register.
