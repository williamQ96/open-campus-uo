# Cascade Charley and the west end of Science Walk

University records place Alice Wingwall’s 1991 Cascade Charley fountain and Scott Wylie’s 1991 Science Walk at the courtyard between Cascade, Pacific and Volcanology. The Volcanology resource survey shows the fountain immediately west of the 1964 and 1990 bunker additions. The 2012 northeast campus survey separately locates the fountain, inlaid paving, and Volcanology turnaround. [Fountain exhibit](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/cascade-charley) · [Science Walk exhibit](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/science-walk) · [Volcanology site plan, PDF p. 4](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/volcanlogy_05_31_07.pdf#page=4) · [2012 landscape map, PDF p. 19](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/nediagnosis_sept_17_2012rdcd2.pdf#page=19).

The aerial screen places the basin near scene east −245.93 m and north 62.97 m. Four visual centers from 2017, 2021, 2023 and 2025 fall within 0.32 m of their mean. This spread measures repeatability of the visible feature under changing shadows and branches. The clearest year, 2023, gives an estimated 5.1 × 2.8 m visible rectangle. The stair landing candidate lies to the northeast near east −241.93 m and north 71.73 m. Oblique views and obscured edges still affect the rim and stair dimensions. [Lane County orthophotos](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [Registered picks and source hashes](../data/metadata/cascade-charley-science-walk-screen-2026-11-27.json).

The 2026 campus geology group photographed a dry tiled basin with a roughly 0.20 m waterline, a central spout, raised concrete elements and rock and brick inlay around the rim. A person of known height provides a visual scale check. The report’s printed GPS resolves 3.47 km from the courtyard. The university library photograph documents the cascade with water flowing, and the art tour locates the blue listening markers. The current water schedule and exact positions of the listening markers remain unverified.
