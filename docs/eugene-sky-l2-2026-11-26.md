# Eugene daytime sky and cloud banks

ENV-07 uses a procedural fair-weather sky across the campus. The atmosphere grades from a pale horizon to a cooler zenith, with subtle warmth toward the scene's light direction. A lower band of 12 shallow cumulus banks uses irregular contiguous silhouettes and flatter bases. Six higher and thinner banks add a second visible height range. Each bank uses a seeded shape and vertex-color shading so its contour and color remain stable. This is an authored daytime setting. Eugene's summer climate and published descriptions of shallow cumulus and layered clouds inform the design. Cloud position, altitude and color are approximate scene values. [City of Eugene climate context](https://www.eugene-or.gov/DocumentCenter/View/79698) · [NWS cloud development](https://www.weather.gov/source/zhu/ZHU_Training_Page/clouds/cloud_development/clouds.htm) · [NWS cloud classification](https://www.weather.gov/lmk/cloud_classification).

The runtime follows the camera and drifts the cloud mesh slowly around the viewer. Reduced motion freezes drift without interrupting camera tracking. When motion resumes, the next frame continues from the frozen angle. Static and moving views use the same atmosphere and cloud geometry. The viewer has two sky meshes sharing an unlit vertex-color material.

## Model and verification

The standalone 319,308-byte GLB contains the inward-facing atmosphere and all 18 cloud banks. Reimport matches 5,893 vertex positions, normals, colors, triangle indices, local transforms and the unlit vertex-color material. It represents the camera-centered sky at rest. In a separate renderer set the background order, disable depth writing, fog and tone mapping for these meshes, and center the group around the camera as needed. The two earlier clouds-only exports remain available.
