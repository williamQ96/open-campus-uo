# Education Annex and its outdoor approach

Education Annex is University of Oregon building B0048 at 922 East 16th Avenue. The 2025 campus inventory lists the one-story building, and the updated historic survey documents its wood shingles, center porch gable and later raised approach. The campus map identifies the west entrance and shows the present red siding, pale trim, rail and green name board. [Building inventory](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-09/02_buildings_by_number_fy2025-1.pdf) · [Historic survey, pp. 1, 4 and 5](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-01/educationannex_2025.pdf) · [Campus map photograph](https://map.uoregon.edu/resources/aws/popup-images/education-annex.jpg).

The university outline is 142.07 m² including the west porch projection. The existing scene footprints have zero overlap with this outline. The Annex is registered under `uo-education-annex` as its own selectable exterior. County aerials show the same gabled form. On 789 first returns within the 0.40 m roof screening band, the median absolute height residual is 0.105 m. The representative six-over-six windows, roof vents, porch columns and siding use original modeled geometry. [Lane County orthophotos](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [NOAA point cloud](https://noaa-nos-coastal-lidar-pds.s3.amazonaws.com/laz/geoid18/8866/44123A1309.copc.laz).

The 7.39 m approach ramp rises 0.37 m in the model. A separate four-tread stair reaches the north side of the porch. Eight navigation edges link the west approach to the east bypass of Kendall Plaza. The name board and two beds sit outside the pathway. The thresholds, tread count, railing profiles and some window positions use estimates. The modeled ramp is 1.35 m wide and rises 0.37 m over 7.39 m. The rail clears the registered maple's modeled trunk by 0.055 m. The authored centerline clears the nearest modeled obstacle by 0.558 m and all route collision boxes expanded by the viewer's 0.50 m margin. All three stayed within 0.13 m of the registered paving in both profiles. Five ramp heights and four stair heights rose in sequence.
