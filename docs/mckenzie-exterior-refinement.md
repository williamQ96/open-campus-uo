# McKenzie Hall exterior study, 2026-11-04

The [UO cultural resource survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/mckenziehall2025-0) describes a four-story asymmetrical building with yellow brick, exposed concrete bands, flat roof, Kincaid Street to the west and an east courtyard. The [UO Global Engagement photograph](https://global.uoregon.edu/contact-us) and the [National Trust preservation feature](https://savingplaces.org/stories/actionfund2025) (courtyard photograph credited to Brian Davies) were consulted for facade vocabulary only, not redistributed or used as textures. Original procedural overlay geometry is CC BY 4.0.

The existing `roof-f61e9b20` identity is an approximate nearest-roof match. Reversible source-pinned panels cover generic windows on two west spans and the central east-facing section. Concrete bands, paired narrow west windows and east glazing and piers are stylistic approximations, not surveyed measurements. The existing flat roof and surrounding wings remain; no new measured roof reconstruction is claimed. [West before](images/mckenzie-west-before-next.png) / [after](images/mckenzie-west-after-next.png); [east before](images/mckenzie-east-before-next.png) / [after](images/mckenzie-east-after-next.png).

The east source shows a sunken courtyard and raised circulation; the current ground and mesh do not represent these, and the overlay does not add a courtyard void, stairs or elevated walkways. Other elevations, window spacing, entrances, dimensions, materials and present-day conditions remain unverified. Terrain and existing trees were retained; the terrain is an approximate exported surface, not a field survey. The overlay projects beyond the original mesh and adds no collision. A conservative rectangle screening found no existing navigation graph edges within 0.5 m of the modeled spans; this does not establish pedestrian clearance or accessible routes. Interior, base GLB, paths and vegetation were not edited. Keep level 1 pending further evidence and user-defined standards.
