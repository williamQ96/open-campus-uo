# Deschutes Hall source identity review — 2026-11-04

The [UO architecture guide](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/deschutes-hall) locates Deschutes Hall at 1477 E. 13th Avenue and dates it to 1990. The [2009 Oregon State Archives photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Deschutes_Hall_(Lane_County,_Oregon_scenic_images)_(lanDA0048a).jpg) by Gary Halvorson depicts a broad brick building; the low-resolution source permits reuse with attribution. The image is referenced only, not redistributed.

The project assigns the name by `approximate-nearest-roof` to `roof-0ca67b60`, a fragmented 365.5 m² LiDAR candidate whose scene mass is much smaller and less regular than the depicted facade. Historic LiDAR may contain canopy or miss roof/walls; this comparison does not prove that the model is a tree or identify a replacement mesh. [South view](images/deschutes-south-before.png) and [north view](images/deschutes-north-before.png) document existing context. No geometry was changed and level remains 1. A registered campus footprint or other spatially anchored evidence is needed before transferring facade details or correcting the identity.
