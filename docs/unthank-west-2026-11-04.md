# Unthank Hall west facade — local partial refinement, 2026-11-04

The [design-team description](https://aiaoregon.awardsplatform.com/gallery/nGGBxPzM/QJMYLzpb?search=2907fc90a64f58b5-8) describes seven stories, brick, metal-clad volumes and ground-floor glass; the architect's [west-facade vignettes](https://rowellbrokaw.com/blog/2021/9/27/unthank-hall-vignettes) were consulted for broad character. Referenced photos are not copied or redistributed. Identity is associated with the existing approximate campus-map envelope; this is not a surveyed building match.

The source-pinned overlay adds a schematic brick west wall, ground-floor glass bays, six rows of framed upper windows, two light metal panels and horizontal trim to `context-oc-unthank`. These elements provide a coherent facade treatment across the current model's west side. Their exact sizes, locations, materials, elevations and current condition are inferred; opaque glass is illustrative, no real entrance, canopy, courtyard, floor plan or roof is reconstructed. The source is an approximate 90 by 50 m solid rectangle, not the actual multi-wing building. Its east end overlaps the separate Bean candidate by 201.22 m² in the project's 2D footprint screen; the overlay does not touch the east side, and this flag does not prove an actual building intersection. The west face and orientation are relative to the placeholder, not a registered architectural survey. The building remains level 1.
