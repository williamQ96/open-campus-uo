# Susan Campbell Hall exterior

A source-pinned runtime replacement retains the building identity `roof-fd061857` and original campus GLB. Its approximate L-shaped body, gambrel main roof and shed dormers are informed by the [UO historic survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/susancampbellhall2025) and an archived [2011 photograph by Gary Halvorson / Oregon State Archives, CC BY 4.0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Susan_Campbell_Hall,_University_Of_Oregon,_Eugene_-_DPLA_-_4e7906177b35545a22a8ec321bd92da7.jpg). The survey is linked only; the photograph is not a runtime texture.

A first roof-only overlay was rejected because it intersected the existing flat-roof geometry; this replacement is reversible and does not rewrite the base asset. Photo orientation, window count and placement, wing roof, entrances, dimensions, terrain junctions and current conditions remain unverified approximations. Existing tree crowns obscure part of the view. No navigation, collision or accessibility claim is made. The asset remains level 1 and its interior is untouched. [Before](images/susan-campbell-before.png) / [after](images/susan-campbell-after.png).

## Multi-elevation follow-up, 2026-11-04

The [UO historic survey, updated 2025](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/susancampbellhall2025), describes L-shaped brick massing, multi-pane wood windows, a gambrel roof and shed dormers. It includes two photographs and a site diagram. Photographs are used as reference only, not redistributed or used as textures. Their precise alignment and present conditions remain unverified.

This reversible layer adds multi-pane detailing to existing windows on several elevations and six illustrative wing-roof dormers, and removes window and dormer objects buried where the building wings overlap. The original L-shaped massing and existing landscape are retained. Window counts, positioning, the wing roof and dormer junction, entrances, grade and western or courtyard details remain approximations, and the asset remains level 1. [Outside before](images/susan-outer-before-next.png), [outside after](images/susan-outer-after-next.png), [courtyard isolated for inspection](images/susan-court-clear-after.png). The [actual contextual view](images/susan-court-after-next.png) is heavily obscured by existing tree crowns; this geometry-only inspection hides other meshes temporarily and does not alter them in the application.

Two conservative ENU collision rectangles cover the modeled L-shaped volumes while retaining the original footprint. No graph edges intersect these boxes at a 0.5 m margin. Entrance locations, walkable approaches and accessibility have not been established, and this is not a full vegetation or clearance survey. The previous exterior is preserved at `assets/exterior-refinement/susan-before-multiface-2026-11-04.json`; the underlying campus GLB and interiors remain unchanged.

The photograph is used as a reference, not a runtime texture; its exact current appearance is not established.

Two stylized brick parapet volumes replace two existing schematic dormer caps on the long south-facing model elevation, with their window frames and glazing moved to the new visible face. Placement and proportions are interpreted from the historical view, not surveyed; the real roof profiles and exact orientation need confirmation. Existing grounds, model anchor, interior and conservative collision rectangles remain unchanged. The courtyard entrance shown in the UO survey photograph, the wing roof, other facades, route accessibility and present conditions remain incomplete or unverified; the asset remains level 1.

## Courtyard entry study, 2026-11-04

The [UO cultural survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/susancampbellhall2025), PDF page 4, includes a courtyard photograph showing a columned porch near the junction of the two wings. Based on that image and the approximate L-shaped model, this study places an original stylized porch and glazed door on the north-facing inner elevation, removing one conflicting schematic window. Its coordinates, dimensions, precise orientation and present condition are inferred, not surveyed; the source photo is linked, not redistributed as a texture.

Two small conservative collision boxes cover the modeled post bases, in addition to the two prior building envelopes. No existing navigation graph edges intersect the added boxes with a 0.5 m margin, but this is not an accessibility or full clearance survey. The existing interactive entrance remains separately estimated on the south side at `(-445, 168.53)` and was not moved; the new courtyard door is visual geometry and is not a verified interactive or accessible route. The base GLB, other building geometry, vegetation, interiors and route graph are preserved. The photograph, modeled floor height and local ground are not a measured match; the building remains level 1.
