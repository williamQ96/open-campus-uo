# McArthur Court, Esslinger Hall and Student Recreation Center exterior review

The local default replaces three adjacent building meshes and their collision in one installation, using their existing scene IDs. The November 23 development review initially recorded all three at L1. Open the [local preview](http://127.0.0.1:5195/) or the [development baseline](http://127.0.0.1:5191/?exterior-review=athletic-baseline).

## Evidence and boundaries

- UO campus map footprints 020, 023 and 051 provide the starting partition. The 2024 orthophoto shows the connecting strip between McArthur Court and Esslinger, the neighboring roof outlines, and the full eastern recreation wing. The university's surveys document the original arena, its 1954 Esslinger connection and the later SRC connection. The model places the shared physical seams without assigning shared interior spaces. [UO campus map](https://map.uoregon.edu/), [Oregon 2024 orthophoto service](https://imagery.oregonexplorer.info/arcgis/rest/services/OSIP_2024/OSIP_2024_SL/ImageServer), [McArthur 2025 survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-01/mcarthur-court_2025.pdf), [Esslinger 2025 survey with site plan](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-01/esslingerhall_2025.pdf).
- The 521,087-return local clip from NOAA tile `44123A1310` was projected into scene-local EPSG:3740 coordinates. Earlier returns resolve the arena's central ridge and taller exterior trusses, separate height bands at Esslinger and the north gym's raised roof at SRC. The eastern and southern SRC additions are absent in the clip. Their outlines and visible forms come from the 2024 orthophoto and completed-project photographs. The buildings' current roof equipment, door thresholds and dimensions of the east volume are approximate. UO announced replacement of Esslinger's northern roof for 2026. The model uses the earlier roof evidence there and omits uncertain small roof elements. [NOAA Lane County collection](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/57407), [UO McArthur assessment, PDF pages 8–17 and 49–50](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/mac_ct_preliminary_hp_assessment.pdf), [RDG 2015 SRC project](https://rdgusa.com/work/project/university-of-oregon-student-recreation-center-expansion-and-renovation), [DCI completed-project photographs](https://www.dci-engineers.com/work/university-of-oregon-student-recreation-center), [UO Esslinger roof notice](https://blogs.uoregon.edu/cpfmnotifications/2026/06/05/).
- The preview includes original arena corner cladding, buttresses, glazing and entry canopy, Esslinger's stepped roof and west portal, and the recreation center's two-part north gym roof, clock, glazed east wing and colonnade. The arcade stays open under the upper volume. Its eight columns and the three building ground plans are collidable. The three entrances have modeled approaches and terrain-draped paving. One inferred context tree, index 70, stood inside the SRC east extension and protruded through its roof. The preview removes the 80 triangles for that tree after checking its source location. The tree correction is scoped to the local athletic review. This is a correction of a model overlap based on the orthophoto and finished exterior photographs.

The old McArthur and Esslinger plans intersected by 3,229.85 m². The new plans meet at the documented junction with zero overlap area. No new plan intersects a fourth building's scene footprint by more than 1 m². The modeled arcade's collision footprint excludes 453.25 m² of overhead structure. Compared the old manifest height with the new surface at common 0.5 m roof cells. On 4,600 arena roof cells away from truss crowns, the median absolute elevation difference fell from 2.095 m to 0.045 m. On 4,929 SRC north gym cells, formerly covered by the old Esslinger mesh, it fell from 2.227 m to 0.061 m. Eight Esslinger comparison bands have new median differences between 0.063 m and 0.288 m. These comparisons use the same historical inputs that guided the fits.

The 2,536 perimeter samples found at least 0.39 m of footing cover. Compared the replacement and former collision envelopes against the existing navigation graph with a 0.42 m margin. No edge gained a collision. Chromium confirmed the three distinct entrance points, the passage beside the arena, the recreation arcade and column collision, and 0.085 m pavement lift above the original walking mesh. The student recreation entry opens the project's shared generic classroom and returns to the selected site. Corrupt replacement bytes restore the previous geometry and collision. Site behavior verifies the model and its runtime placement. Current entry and exterior construction measurements still need field or as-built sources.

## Default adoption and exterior review, 2026-11-24

- **McArthur Court** gains diamond panels near the upper corners, secondary window bays, paired east stairs, steel doors, braced concrete landings and railings. The west composition and the surveyed metal roof and external trusses establish the building's silhouette. Stair locations and rise are approximate. The east stairs have collision envelopes and are architectural context. The modeled entry is on University Street. [UO historic assessment, west and east elevations and photographs](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/mac_ct_preliminary_hp_assessment.pdf).
- **Esslinger Hall** gains the twelve octagonal grilles across its west door and transom, the entry cornice and projecting window sills. The 2026 university notice identifies the southern 2024 roof work and shows the light finish over the gym and part of the connecting roof. Those two modeled portions now use a light membrane. The north work area and September skylight installations are documented in the notice. The model uses historical heights and approximate finishes for the northern portion, pending images or dimensions that show its current roof and skylight details. [UO survey and entrance photo](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-01/esslingerhall_2025.pdf), [UO roof notices and marked aerial](https://blogs.uoregon.edu/cpfmnotifications/2026/06/05/).
- **Student Recreation Center** gains the glazed wing's blue horizontal band, projecting east sunshades, deeper metal roof edge and two upper window bands above the east lobby's doors. The completed-project photographs support the brick, glass and concrete arcade composition. Exact louver spacing, cladding profiles and the newer wing's absolute heights remain estimates. [RDG project description](https://rdgusa.com/work/project/university-of-oregon-student-recreation-center-expansion-and-renovation) and [DCI project photographs](https://www.dci-engineers.com/work/university-of-oregon-student-recreation-center).

Compared individually with the Knight Library example, McArthur's external structure and historic façades, Esslinger's stepped massing and distinctive west portal, and the recreation center's old-to-new massing and detailed east frontage support L2 for all three exterior records. The 2024–2026 roof documentation, before/after views in both material modes and desktop and mobile smoke evidence are held in `D:/github/opencampus-interior-research-20261116/exterior-l2/athletic-default-20261124/`.

The local default applies the footprints, stair and column collision, paving and tree correction together. It leaves 531 selectable buildings and 571 visible trees. All 7,705 route edges were checked at the 0.42 m radius with zero new conflicts. The passage next to the modeled stair, the open SRC arcade and three entrance approaches passed browser checks. A corrupt athletic asset restores the old meshes, collision and one context tree while the other default packages remain available.
