# Gerlinger Hall east facade refinement

2026-11-03; local and unpublished. Building `roof-9ac93ab7`, planting `ENV-03`.

UO's [2016 historic assessment](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/gerlinger_hall_historic_assessment_2016-01-20.pdf), pages 4 and 7, identifies the five-bay pedimented entrance as the east elevation. Its floor plan shows the short end of the long east–west hall. The current source-mesh footprint's eastern end is approximately east -359 m, north -198 to -222 m; runtime ray samples at selected heights show a faceted eastern wall from about -359.1 to -360 m. This source, geographic frame and mesh evidence resolve the previous north/east placement error; the historical roof footprint and image alignment still are approximate, not measured field control.

A new independently pinned layer adds a pale triangular pediment, simplified five-bay sash pattern, burgundy awnings, central entrance, two shallow balcony rails and a pair of decorative shrubs. Ground placement samples the existing heightfield. The design interprets the archived [2013 photograph](../data/reference-images/photos/gerlinger-hall/exterior-2013.jpg) by Rick Obst, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), and UO's link-only official assessment; see [image metadata](../data/reference-images/manifest.json). No reference pixels are embedded. Dimensions, exact proportions, hidden sides, accessibility, shrub species and 2026 condition remain unverified. The original GLB and stable ID are unchanged; the earlier north-facing Hall candidate remains disabled and retained. This is an increment, not an approved level-2 standard or promotion; Hall and ENV-03 remain level 1.

[Before](images/hall-east-before.png) and [after](images/hall-east-after.png) are the same controlled browser view with existing procedural trees hidden for legibility; [the context view](images/hall-east-after-context.png) retains the original trees. These are diagnostic renders, not photographs. The added geometry groups materials by building or vegetation with vertex colors to lower draw calls, retaining the independent building identity and existing toon/PBR support. The existing Johnson and Annex layers remain active. The source-pinned rebuild guard includes the new layer.

Validation: local production build, 49 JS tests, eight rebuild-protection tests, and browser checks for Annex selection, Hall/landmark search, material modes and the existing draw-call ceiling; an initial ceiling failure was resolved by batching. Visual reviews use Chrome/SwiftShader and are not hardware performance measurements. No interior or terrain is modified.

## North and south elevation follow-up, 2026-11-04

The [UO 2016 historic assessment](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/gerlinger_hall_historic_assessment_2016-01-20.pdf), PDF pages 10–15, includes 2013 north and south photographs and records the addition of south lower-window louvers and changes to roof dormers. These historical references are consulted only, not redistributed or used as textures. Its recommendations are not treated as present conditions; local PDF SHA-256 `b1a78611394e20302882426b5baa58894a0fd0d57c4e29fb0af262bb58017168`.

The existing reversible layer now adds schematic tall south sash windows, lower louvers, eight representative south roof dormers and north-wing windows. The original east layer and all vegetation objects remain; no building, terrain, routing or collision data are replaced. Window number and dimensions, dormer geometry, vents, south entrances and present conditions remain approximate. Roof and wall positions were checked by limited local rays against the existing source mesh, not surveyed.
