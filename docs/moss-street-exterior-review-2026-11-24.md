# Moss Street Children’s Center exterior review

The default preview uses one registered exterior for UO 168. The north candidate `roof-72c79877` keeps its scene ID and receives the published building name. The south candidate `roof-7b325cb8` remains in the ledger as a historical mapping. The two meshes overlapped the UO outline by 474.36 m² and 458.40 m² respectively. The new outline covers 1,412.75 m². It follows the university wall envelope, while roof edges and canopy overhangs come from the aerial. [University map](https://map.uoregon.edu/) · [University architecture archive](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/moss-street-children-s-center) · [2024 OSIP aerial](https://imagery.oregonexplorer.info/arcgis/rest/services/OSIP_2024/OSIP_2024_SL/ImageServer).

The five volumes have six pitched roof plates, clerestories, brick and sage cladding, and timber-framed glazing around the open court. The exterior includes the public Moss Street approach, a simple replica of the timber shelters, and courtyard decks. The water oak uses UO tree 5299 for its stem location. Its stylized 21.4 m height and 23.6 by 22.1 m crown envelope come from the 2017 surface and 2024 aerial. The university record gives the earlier size as 50 ft tall and 50 ft wide. The tree is a separate vegetation mesh in the site package. Threshold, porch and roof trim dimensions, planting edges and tree branch form remain approximate. [Mahlum project and courtyard photographs](https://www.mahlum.com/projects/moss-street-childrens-center/) · [UO exterior photograph](https://pages.uoregon.edu/mscc/contact.html) · [UO facilities description](https://moss.uoregon.edu/facilities) · [University tree map](https://map.uoregon.edu/trees/main).

The 2017 roof comparison used 496 alternating cells inside buffered roof polygons, with unmapped heights and probable vegetation outside the roof elevation range excluded. The new mesh hit all 496 positions. The 403 paired hits had a median absolute difference of 0.566 m before and 0.018 m after, with a new median of 0.021 m across the full set. The same historical surface informed the modeled roof planes. Twelve paired renders cover the elevations, court and roof. The 7,710 active route edges clear the building, posts and oak stem by at least 5.64 m. Forecourt paving stops at the existing road edge. [2017 surface evidence](campus-surface-2017-2026-11-24.md).

Both legacy ranges are checked and removed together. The retained shared-batch positions, normals and colors match triangle for triangle with the neighboring Kalapuya and Longhouse replacements active. A bad model pin or a mismatched second source anchor restores both historical mesh members. The retirement reduces selectable scene entries by one while preserving the registered facility and its modeled exterior approach. The ledger records L2 after review against the [Knight Library exterior](knight-library-registration-2026-11-23.md).
