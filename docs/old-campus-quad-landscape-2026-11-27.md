# Old Campus Quad cross-paths and Class of 1893 stone

The 2007 landscape survey and 2022 Villard Hall assessment record the Class of 1893 stone north and east of Villard. The survey identifies the stone as coming from Spencer Butte. The 2022 close view shows a rough, tapered stone with a squared broad face and the carved class identification. The model uses an original beveled mesh and linework for the inscription. The estimated position is east −425.92 m and north 218.12 m, with approximately two metres of uncertainty from the old diagram and the screened aerial views. Dimensions, face bearing and finish are estimates from the photograph. [Old Campus Quad survey, PDF pp. 2 and 7](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/old_campus_quadrangle_06_12_07.pdf#page=2) · [Villard Hall assessment, PDF pp. 35 and 37](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/villard_historicassessment_2022_0.pdf#page=37).

The 2017, 2021, 2023 and 2025 county aerials register the east-west walk by Allen Hall, the intersecting diagonals toward Lawrence and the short eastern link. The 2021 views show the main junction clearly. The newer photographs show construction around Villard and University Hall while the eastern alignments remain legible. The package adds 441.27 m² of paving, 330.82 m² of shoulder transitions and 37 walking edges. The branches tie into the retained Hello Walk. The compiled paving footprint includes the retained approaches so navigation can follow them through the junctions. Curve positions, grades and widths beneath tree cover use estimates. [Lane County orthophotos](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [2022 Friendly Hall assessment, PDF p. 46](https://scholarsbank.uoregon.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/f44cd7de-4ca5-48b1-981f-6cee0f225b7d/content#page=46).

Six trees follow the campus inventory, including the white oak, Scotch elm and incense cedar on the north lawn and three registered trees near the Allen-side end. Two inferred trees conflict with the new paving and return if the overlay or Hello Walk fails to load. The July 2023 terrain mosaic informs relative grades. The new surface blends into the retained Hello Walk elevations. ## Verification

Four sampled routes stay within 0.13 m of the registered paving on desktop and phone. The east-west route to the Allen-side approach measures 61.714 m. Both profiles pass 1,670 terrain and collision probes. The minimum clearance from the four route centerlines to the modeled solid envelopes is 1.364 m.

Each local production preview loads 611 exterior IDs, with 414 successful responses and 93 matched file hashes. Five focused browser cases cover the new monument, paving, tree decisions, the retained historic landscape and corrupted-package fallback. The 201 unit tests, build, formatting, 537 archive checks and viewer hashes pass. The asset, descriptor and compiled paving coverage reproduce byte for byte.
