# Onyx Bridge candidate screen — 2026-11-04, local only

The [UO 2006 historic resource survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/onyxbridge05_30_07.pdf) describes Onyx Bridge as a four-story rectangular building with exposed diagonal steel trusses, straddling the Cascade Annexes and attached to Pacific and Klamath Halls. The [UO architectural guide](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/onyx-bridge) independently describes its exterior trusses. The 2006 document is historic reference, not a present-day survey; no reference image or geometry is included.

The project manifest labels `roof-a5a51275` by approximate nearest roof, but that candidate covers about 166 m² with recorded height 4.14 m. It is not sufficient evidence for the four-story bridge. A project-coordinate 2D screen placed the Pacific Hall candidate 1.4 m away, the large irregular Willamette candidate 16.5 m away and an approximate Klamath context envelope 22.7 m away. These are distances between project polygons, not measurements of actual buildings, and cannot identify the missing bridge or prove mesh collisions. No trusses, geometry, identity or collision was changed. The existing name association and level 1 remain as before, with this limitation recorded in the inventory. A registered plan, independently anchored roof sections and a vertical/selection/navigation review are needed before a source-supported reconstruction.
