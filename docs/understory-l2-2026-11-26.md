# Shrubs, understory and planted beds at L2

ENV-03 covers the pilot and campus planting layer. The new default scene draws 75 rebuilt shrubs, five rebuilt fern clusters and three previously detailed fern clusters. Eight low shrubs sit in two strips north of Knight Library, and five mark one bed west of the Johnson Hall north walk. These 13 shapes replace the 40 shrubs in the old continuous band and ring. The other 62 displayed shrubs keep their inferred source anchors. One crown is shortened eastward to clear the modeled Johnson paving. Independently authored site planting follows the existing site packages. [Editable planting register](../data/metadata/understory-l2.json).

The 2024 Oregon imagery informs bed outlines and walk gaps. The planting count, individual shapes and exact positions are representative. The campus landscape survey provides historical context, and the Oregon State University Extension guide provides references for regional foliage forms. The project uses original geometry and materials. Plant locations, species and seasonal appearance still need field confirmation. [Oregon imagery service](https://imagery.oregonexplorer.info/arcgis/rest/services/OSIP_2024/OSIP_2024_SL/ImageServer) · [UO campus landscape survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/old_campus_quadrangle_06_12_07.pdf) · [OSU Extension planting guide](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/catalog/em-9697-incorporating-pacific-northwest-native-plants-your-water-wise-landscapes).

## Scene integration and verification

- The editable register keys all 122 source spans to the original planting and understory layers. Four shrub forms vary in canopy shape and branch arrangement. The new fern clusters use separate pinnae and tapering frond ribs. The three previously detailed groups stay visible through their original 6,120 vertices. The new plant batch draws 34,020 indices, and the three beds share a second mesh. Bed and shrub bases follow the highest of the local terrain, lawn and paving. The revised plants and bed interiors sit visibly above those surfaces.
- The runtime validates the asset and source spans before swapping the batches. Existing site masks determine which spans still draw. A failed replacement leaves the preceding vegetation visible. The existing source-mask failure path can restore the three associated shrub instances in the replacement. Open `/?exterior-review=understory-model-baseline` to compare the previous planted band, ring and understory with the same site packages.
- The surface screen compares 107 preceding plant spans and 80 replacement spans against 235 nearby rendered building, paving and approach triangles. The preceding spans touched the PLC facade, its site approach and the old generic paving. The revised plant meshes have no contacts with the sampled triangles. The three soil meshes intentionally meet the underlying landscape surfaces. Thirteen relocated shrub centers pass the runtime solid-collision probes.
- The footprints of the 80 revised shrubs and ferns and three beds were compared with 7,705 route edges and 608 walkable entrance segments. The closest bed is 2.96 m from the modeled route graph, and the closest plant envelope is 3.1813 m away. These checks cover the modeled pathways and entrances.
- Six matched WebGL views verify the two Knight beds, the Johnson walk and the separate fern fronds. Each camera kept the same draw count. Twelve thousand sampled bed points clear the modeled surfaces by at least 1.29 cm. The Johnson bed has a visible seam where the underlying paving meets the lawn. Desktop and phone production previews drew the same counts of shrubs, fern clusters and trees with byte-identical staged, installed, built and served assets.
