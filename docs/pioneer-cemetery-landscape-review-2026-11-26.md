# Eugene Pioneer Cemetery landscape review

This package reconstructs the main outdoor circulation and several visible landmarks on county parcel 1703320000600. The 63,742.88 m² parcel aligns the cemetery between East 18th Avenue, University Street and the campus. The cemetery association map supplies the names and historic layout. The Lane County 2017, 2021 and 2025 orthophotos constrain the visible alignments. [Cemetery self-guided map](https://eugenepioneercemetery.org/self-guided-tour/) · [Lane County imagery service](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [National Register nomination](https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/8377666e-d980-4494-bce2-3993f6f19669).

- Eight named ways follow the registered path grid, with narrow gravel shoulders and about 1,824 m of centerline. The 6,668 m² modeled travel surface follows the current terrain. Widths and portions screened by the tree canopy are approximate.
- The University Street stair uses 11 concrete treads, paired handrails and open landings. A modeled route connects the street to Memorial Square via Dorris Avenue. Low masonry retaining walls follow visible portions of the East 18th edge. [East stair in July 2024](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=-xUdMd56U5OekBMXryjUOQ&heading=277.27).
- The southeast stone identification sign has individually modeled lettering, stone strips, a cap and planted ground around the base. The GAR landmark uses a stepped base, blue-gray shaft and a simplified representative figure. The reported overall height is about 7.62 m. Exact stone, letter and figure dimensions are estimates. [Sign photograph by Randy Fletcher](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eugene_Pioneer_Cemetery_sign_2007.jpg) · [Cemetery association GAR account](https://eugenepioneercemetery.org/gar-plot/).
- The export includes 121 canopy estimates screened against two surface models and the 2025 aerial, and replaces 15 conflicting procedural background trees. Crown centers, tree forms and colors are approximate. Six small rose plantings correspond to the tour map and association descriptions. Twenty-three small stone forms correspond to recurring aerial features across all three dates. Their profiles and heights are approximate and the models carry no burial names. [Cemetery heritage roses](https://eugenepioneercemetery.org/heritage-roses/).

Two independent exports matched byte for byte. Five before-and-after views verify the four landscape meshes. The 113.61 m model route crosses the stair and reaches Memorial Square. Desktop and phone production previews check all 11 increasing standing heights, both rail and centerline behavior, the sign and monument obstacle footprints, and four paved sampling points. A changed GLB restores the previous landscaping, terrain behavior and all 15 original tree ranges. Both devices retain the same 610 selectable exteriors, 457 entrances and 139 corridor records. The build, formatting, 197 unit tests, eight related browser cases, 459 archive checks and 402 viewer hash checks passed. The package ships with the default GitHub Pages scene. [Deployment history](https://github.com/williamQ96/open-campus-uo/actions/workflows/pages.yml).

## November 27 inner plots and support structures

Four open grass alleys and 13 surviving curb groups now follow visible alignments checked against the 2021 and 2025 imagery. Patches with partial evidence retain open ends. Widths are the estimated visible strips, and their modern surfaces are mown grass. The historic nomination records 8-foot platted alleys and illustrates the curb profiles. [Lane County orthophotos](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [National Register nomination, PDF pp. 6–7 and 30](https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/8377666e-d980-4494-bce2-3993f6f19669).

The hip-roof masonry maintenance building, neighboring post-and-gable shelter and about 112 m² of approach surface follow the two recurring aerial roof outlines and the historic façade images. Elevations and openings are approximate. The shelter's supports are solid and its covered bay is open in the preview. One procedural tree that passed through its roof is suppressed. Both structures are part of the landscape bundle. [National Register nomination, PDF pp. 9 and 37](https://npgallery.nps.gov/GetAsset/8377666e-d980-4494-bce2-3993f6f19669).

The second package builds reproducibly and keeps the published 113.61 m route from University Street to Memorial Square. The additions are included in the November 27 release. Screened grave rows and further curb segments are still pending. [Landscape inventory](landscape-l2-inventory.md).

## Memorial Square kiosk and sign

The association’s fall 2026 newsletter photographs the interpretive kiosk installed in 2016 and the green sign beside it. The 2017 and 2021 aerials expose the kiosk roof, and the 2025 image shows canopy over the area. The model uses the observed timber posts, planked notice board, corrugated roof and green sign. Roof dimensions and sign placement are estimates. The paper inserts use representative graphics. Both fixtures have local collision, and the nearby walk remains open. [Association newsletter, PDF p. 3](https://eugenepioneercemetery.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/EPC-e-Newsletter-FALL-2026_High-res.pdf) · [Cemetery self-guided map](https://eugenepioneercemetery.org/self-guided-tour/).

## Smith Vault and adjacent plot edges

Tour stop 24 and the November 2026 restoration newsletter identify the vault just north of Cherry Avenue. The 2017, 2021 and 2025 county images show the same east–west hip. The scene uses a 2.80 × 1.38 m footprint, a shallow hipped plaster cap and a plaque on the east end. The height of 1.25 m and the plaque placement and lettering are estimates. Two additional concrete curb traces follow visible sections across the three images, bringing the modeled curb count to 15 groups. [Association tour](https://eugenepioneercemetery.org/self-guided-tour/) · [Association newsletter, PDF p. 3](https://eugenepioneercemetery.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/EPC-e-Newsletter-FALL-2026_High-res.pdf) · [Lane County orthophotos](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer).

Two exports matched byte for byte. The foreground plot edge and roof profile were checked in paired views. Desktop and phone production previews detected the vault footprint and verified clear ground to its east and on Cherry Avenue. The paving, stair, canopy, shelter and kiosk meshes retain their published vertices, faces and colors. The vault update is local.
