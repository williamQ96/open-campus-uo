# East 13th Avenue from Lillis Plaza to University Street

The 2017, 2021 and 2025 Lane County orthophotos tie the Lillis seam, crosswalks, sidewalk edges and University Street square to the campus coordinate grid. The modeled corridor adds 2,608.06 m² of road and 3,610.44 m² of sidewalks and paved plazas. The two parallel-line crossings are near Fenton and the Allen approach. The model includes curb openings, bicycle symbols and selected service-space markings. Paving colors, curb elevations and portions under tree cover use estimates. [Lane County orthophotos](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [UO 13th Avenue project](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/13th-avenue-axis-conceptual-design).

At University Street, the 552.81 m² Heart of Campus apron has transverse paver bands, a contrasting central commemorative brick field, planter pairs and six bollards. The visible grid guides the original model. Donor inscriptions remain unmodeled. The 2025 orthophoto registers the north shelter footprint and the two bike shelters east of Anstett, which UO documents as a 2018 installation. Shelter profiles, post placement and rack counts are approximate. The geometry includes covered and open bicycle racks. [UO Heart of Campus history](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/heart-of-campus) · [UO shelter placement notice](https://blogs.uoregon.edu/cpfmnotifications/2018/07/16/bike-rack-placement-718-824/).

Seven roadside and junction planters and six UO-registered trees use the aerial and campus inventory. Three tree pits interrupt the south paving edge. A source-pinned removal clears the procedural stem inside the Lawrence approach. Its absence matches the three aerials. Crown dimensions and understory composition are estimates. [UO tree inventory](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/trees).

Six path branches add 43 navigation edges. The east–west pedestrian branch runs along the south side past Friendly Hall, and the north branch ends west of the construction frontage. The Fenton crossing links these two branches. August 2026 campus guidance reports the north sidewalk by Friendly fenced through fall term 2027. The model depicts permanent paving for that block. The exact temporary fence and trailer footprint still require field registration. [UO 2026 closure update](https://news.uoregon.edu/content/get-update-construction-activity-around-university-0) · [Friendly Hall site logistics advisory](https://blogs.uoregon.edu/cpfmnotifications/2025/06/12/6-30-25-12-31-27-friendly-hall-renovation-project-and-access-advisory/).

The two independent exports are byte-identical. Six paired production views cover the seam with the west package, two bicycle shelters, Fenton crossing, the south sidewalk and the University Street square. Desktop and phone viewport sessions both loaded mesh geometry for each of the 610 active exterior IDs and returned the same 246.63 m south-side route and 16.90 m Fenton crossing route. The traced south route stays on the modeled south sidewalk alongside Friendly. Six paving samples remained passable, six furniture positions registered collision and the removed tree's indexed stem triangles were absent. Nine related browser checks passed, including corrupt-bundle recovery and restoration of the source tree. The west, central and east segments are included together in the East 13th Avenue release.
