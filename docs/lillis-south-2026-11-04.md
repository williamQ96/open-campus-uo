# Lillis Hall south entrance — 2026-11-04

The [UO architecture guide](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/lillis-business-complex) describes the four-story glass entrance with photovoltaic cells and the north–south axis; [UO's Lundquist College](https://business.uoregon.edu/about/lillis-complex) identifies the four buildings comprising the complex. A [UO exterior photo](https://news.uoregon.edu/ducks-hatch-business-start-ups-help-getting-ground) was consulted for the glass and light portal; the photo is copyrighted, linked only and not bundled or used as a texture. No university logo or lettering is reproduced. Original JSON geometry is CC BY 4.0.

The source asset `roof-86754ad7` is an approximate-nearest-roof match, not a surveyed footprint. The candidate sits between the project's Peterson and Anstett models. Seven horizontal ray samples at scene height 8 m along the proposed south face struck this building's original GLB mesh at glTF z approximately -46 m (one also hit a projecting source window at -45.92 m). This is evidence of a modeled wall, not proof of actual building boundaries or the exact entrance position. A reversible, hash-pinned layer adds a schematic opaque glass wall, light framing, a portal and small dark panels suggesting photovoltaic cells. It replaces no source geometry; the detail has 39 boxes in one merged selectable building mesh. The cells, portal dimensions and exact glass layout are illustrative and do not simulate energy generation or reproduce the transparent atrium interior.
