# Reusable fern variation — 2026-11-04, local only

The existing three stylized fern clusters use the same reusable component. Its ten fronds now have fixed, bounded differences in length, height and azimuth rather than identical radial profiles. The design remains deliberately low-poly and deterministic; it does not add instances or expand beyond the existing 1.35 m clearance screen at the adopted scale. The visual change is modest: [isolated comparison](images/fern-variation-comparison-2026-11-04.png), previous version left and new version right. This review is not a campus-context close-up. The three scene positions, source-pinned suppression, existing material batch, 680 triangles per cluster, original GLB, surrounding layout and interiors are unchanged. The prior generator is retained at `assets/vegetation-refinement/fern-geometry-before-variation-2026-11-04.js`.

[Oregon State University Extension](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/catalog/pub/ec-1577-gardening-oregon-native-plants-west-cascades) describes western sword fern as an evergreen shade plant, and [UO](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/wrna-plants-and-animals) lists it in the Willamette River natural area. Neither identifies plants at these model positions; leaf shapes, dimensions and present conditions are illustrative, and the former five-point screen does not establish physical clearance or accessibility. ENV-03 remains level 1. No source image or third-party geometry is redistributed.

The new [normalized standalone GLB](../assets/vegetation-refinement/fern-varied-2026-11-04.glb) contains one fern in glTF coordinates with a green standard material; position, scale, placement, scene suppression and batch integration are separate. It is a single mesh, not a per-frond Blender scene. Its 2,040 positions and normals were compared against the runtime generator with zero maximum error, with matching color, identity transform and embedded source SHA-256. Reproduce with `node scripts/export-fern.mjs ../../assets/vegetation-refinement/<new-name>.glb` from `apps/web-preview` while local Vite runs; the exporter rejects an existing output and verifies source stability and reimport. `src/fern-geometry.js` is authoritative. Blender was unavailable; no new `.blend` snapshot was created. The generator is Apache-2.0 and original geometry CC BY 4.0.

Validation: 124 Node tests, production build, formatter and viewer hashes passed; five targeted browser checks included standalone equivalence, full-scene loading, activity/navigation and the unchanged <180 activity-disabled draw-call limit. Visual comparison was isolated; a new campus-context close-up and a full scene rebuild were not performed. Existing bundle-size warning remains. Work stays local.

This is a model-footprint conflict, not proof of actual overlap or an identity correction.
