# East 18th Avenue west sidewalks and crossings

The registration covers 203.5 m of East 18th Avenue from the Alder approach to the west end of Pioneer Cemetery's Rankin frontage. Lane County's 2017, 2021, 2023 and 2025 orthophotos establish the road edges, both sidewalk alignments, the Kincaid crossings and the Clinical Services south parking court. The west Kincaid markings are clearest in 2021 and 2023. Canopy covers parts of the south sidewalk and the west crossing in the later views. The campus building survey and clinic parking map corroborate the south frontage. [Lane County imagery](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [Clinical Services survey, plan on page 4](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/clinicalservice05_30_07.pdf#page=4) · [Clinic parking map](https://hedcoclinic.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-12/Parking%20Map%20-%20New%20with%20CSB.pdf).

The north sidewalk meets the registered Rankin frontage at its west end. The existing cemetery route network serves the internal named paths. This registration ends at the frontage seam. The clinic branches end on the south apron beside the previously modeled entrance. The contemporary imagery supports the plan positions. Six route probes stayed within the 0.13 m coverage tolerance and clear the planting envelopes by at least 0.673 m. The two Kincaid routes and the clinic parking route cross at the modeled markings. The visible surfaces on either side of the cemetery seam differ by 8 mm across a 10 cm sample. The west-only preview exposed a 0.106 m standing-height difference on the cemetery side. The subsequent Rankin frontage registration aligns that standing height with the sidewalk. The west mesh, descriptor and coverage reproduce byte for byte.
