# Memorial Quadrangle pedestrian lamps

The 2007 landscape survey marks standard pedestrian lamps along the Quad and Knight Library apron. The 2017, 2021, 2023 and 2025 Lane County orthophotos show six corresponding pale globes or narrow pole shadows across at least two dates per location. The 1990 National Register nomination records seven historic cast-iron poles with tapered fluting and frosted globes. The historic count and the original hardware inventory remain separate evidence. The model uses the campus lighting plan's dark-green fluted post, stepped footing and frosted acorn globe. The 3.048 m pole and 3.433 m overall height represent the shorter standard.
