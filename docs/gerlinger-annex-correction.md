# Gerlinger Annex identity and exterior correction

2026-11-03. Local and unpublished. Building ID `roof-f0b575d6` remains unchanged.

The project's approximate locator is longitude -123.0765252, latitude 44.0432758, and the historical roof candidate spans east -499 to -433 m and north -270 to -242 m. It sits east of Knight and south-west of Gerlinger Hall. The UO [2025 Gerlinger Annex survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/gerlingerannex2025), pages 1–4, identifies the Annex at 1484 University Street as the 1969 modern, flat-roofed brick and concrete building next to the field and connected to Hall. Its site plan and photograph support the existing location relationship, not the previous arched, dormered facade. This is a reasoned coordinate/site-plan match, not a surveyed exact footprint. The Commons photo previously labelled Annex remains archived with its own provenance; the photographed building or exact elevation should be independently confirmed, and it must not be used as proof of the Annex facade.

An independently reversible source-pinned replacement layer presents an approximate flat-roof brick mass, a concrete upper panel, glazed lower bays and a simplified planted concrete frontage. Size and position follow the existing historic footprint envelope; photograph-based form is interpreted, not traced or measured. The frontage's exact orientation, structural layout, dimensions, entrance, hidden elevations, actual planting and current condition remain unverified. The planter segments sample the existing historical heightfield; they are illustrative and do not imply a verified path or accessibility. Official survey photographs are link-only; no source image pixels are included. Original procedural geometry is CC BY 4.0, code Apache-2.0.

## Hall east/north correction

UO's [Gerlinger Hall historic assessment](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/gerlinger_hall_historic_assessment_2016-01-20.pdf), pages 7–9, identifies the entrance and awning facade as the east elevation. The previous project generator and last detail layer placed its interpretation on the north-facing side. The new Hall overlay has therefore been deactivated, preserved as an editable candidate, and its inventory records the unresolved alignment. Its earlier screenshots document the candidate, not the current scene. The older base GLB retains its prior inaccurate Hall approximation pending a broader, source-supported remodelling; we have not moved it speculatively. Johnson's separate layer remains active.
