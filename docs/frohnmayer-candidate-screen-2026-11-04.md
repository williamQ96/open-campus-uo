# Frohnmayer candidate screening — 2026-11-04

The [UO School of Music and Dance](https://musicanddance.uoregon.edu/about/facilities) identifies the Frohnmayer Music Building and describes its central courtyard and multiple wings. The [UO architecture guide](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/frohnmayer-music-building) documents several building phases. The project assigns `roof-e4a5429f` by approximate nearest roof; its footprint is about 505 m². Comparing its project coordinates with the archived [2024 USDA NAIP image](../data/metadata/naip-2024-main-campus-reference.json) places that outline over one rectangular roof within the wider music-building area. This is a coarse position screen, not registration of the candidate to a particular wing or facade, nor proof of present-day condition. The official exterior photograph has no granted project redistribution rights and is linked only. No source photograph or third-party geometry is included.

Because the image and existing mesh do not establish which facade belongs to this roof section or show its whole elevation, no detailed facade, entry, label, interior, footprint or collision was changed. Its existing level 1 remains. Registered source plans and views of the specific wing are needed before site-specific reconstruction.
