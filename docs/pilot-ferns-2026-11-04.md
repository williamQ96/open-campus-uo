# Regional fern-like planting — 2026-11-04

Three existing illustrative planting clusters were refined with a reusable faceted fern form, without moving their centers. A source-pinned patch selects 192 unique positions / 720 existing vertices: 648 from three original shrub forms and 72 from their old triangular fern fans. The new three-cluster detail is merged into the existing green understory material batch; the source GLB and other understory meshes remain unchanged. The source GLB hash, target material/mesh and rebuild preservation guard must match before application.

The shape is an original stylized interpretation informed by [OSU Extension's western Oregon native plant guide](https://extension.oregonstate.edu/catalog/pub/ec-1577-gardening-oregon-native-plants-west-cascades), which describes sword fern as an evergreen plant suited to shade, and [UO's natural-area species list](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/wrna-plants-and-animals), which includes western sword fern in that natural area. Neither source identifies the species at these three model locations. No source photographs or third-party geometry are included; original output is CC BY 4.0 and procedural code Apache-2.0. Species, location, height and current conditions remain unverified; ENV-03 remains level 1.

A center plus four cardinal points at 1.35 m were ray-tested against visible authored building and paving meshes. Only three of eleven existing cluster locations passed this limited screen; five others had hits on building details and three had hits on paving (some overlap). Those eight original clusters remain unchanged, pending better evidence. The five-point screen is not a full geometric clearance, field, navigation or accessibility survey; inferred paths and ground heights can differ from reality. The component's outer radius is below the screen radius.
