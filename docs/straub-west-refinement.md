# Straub Hall west exterior

A source-pinned, reversible west-facing overlay adds a simplified brick facade, repeated framed windows and a three-arch entrance to the existing `roof-5ded2bf0` building. The [UO cultural resource survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/straubhall2025) describes brick, four stories and a triple-arched entrance, and its site plan places the main frontage on the west, Onyx Street side. Its 2006 photo and plan were consulted but are not redistributed or used as textures. The linked survey was accessed 2026-11-04.

The position follows the existing approximate LiDAR footprint; exact dimensions, window count and spacing, entrance location, materials, grade and present conditions are inferred. The existing source model merges roof sections and does not reproduce the survey's gabled roof or full building plan; the other elevations and attached buildings were not reconstructed. Trees obscure portions of the view, and no route, collision or accessibility correctness is claimed. [Before](images/straub-west-before.png) / [after](images/straub-west-after.png). The original GLB, placement and interior are unchanged. This partial improvement remains level 1.

The existing building card identifies the Department of Psychology from a separately linked current UO department contact page; the historic survey was not used to infer additional current occupants. Runtime overlay is original CC BY 4.0 geometry; no third-party model or image is bundled.

## West wing roof and attic study — 2026-11-04

The same UO survey describes a medium-pitch gable roof and a stucco attic half-story. An additive, estimated gable over the western long wing and a light-colored attic band bring the existing west overlay closer to that description. The pre-existing 323 objects remain unchanged; the original building mesh, entrance and collision data are retained. [Street before](images/straub-roof-west-before-2026-11-04.png) / [after](images/straub-roof-west-after-2026-11-04.png); [aerial before and after](images/straub-roof-aerial-comparison-2026-11-04.png). The 2006 survey imagery is consulted only, not distributed.

The ridge height, exact footprint, attic band and junctions are approximations against the existing merged scene; the rear wings, courtyard roofs, actual window spacing, present condition and physical entrance relationships require further source or on-site checks. Trees obscure portions of ground views. This is a partial level 1 study, not a complete or verified level 2 building.

The [prior pinned specification](../assets/exterior-refinement/straub-before-roof-2026-11-04.json) is preserved. The [standalone GLB](../assets/exterior-refinement/straub-west-roof-overlay-2026-11-04.glb) contains the additive overlay only, not the source building, terrain, navigation or collisions; do not load it alongside the runtime overlay. Export/reimport matched position, normal and color attributes exactly for 12,768 vertices. The JSON remains the editable procedural source; no new Blender snapshot was made. The screenshots and browser checks cover selected viewpoints, not an as-built survey.
