# Global Scholars Hall exterior review

The local default replaces the rectangular placeholder with the registered UO 158 exterior under the existing scene ID `context-b0158`. A 4,276.25 m² footprint defines three five-story towers, two lower connecting volumes with planted roofs, and two open east courtyards. The roofs have slope breaks, dormers and the central tower's solar panel banks. The façades distinguish brick, pale panels, glazed corners and the lower commons. Four modeled approaches serve the north lobby, western dining frontage, south tower and south courtyard. [University architecture archive](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/global-scholars-hall) · [University Housing](https://housing.uoregon.edu/global%20scholars) · [Ground-level exterior archive photo](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/catalog/44881ef63a314d57144a20e4ca5d2a91).

The ground outline comes from the UO map with small bends simplified and an approximately 1.65 m west adjustment based on the 2024 imagery and 2017 surface transition. Entrances use the public map's first-floor markers projected to the modeled boundary. The old block covered 56.2% of the registered outline. Figure 3.13 in the 2015 UO dissertation documents the typical tower outlines. The 2024 aerial and 2017 surface distinguish the north and south roof profiles, planted connector heights and courtyard shelter locations. [University map](https://map.uoregon.edu/) · [2015 dissertation, fig. 3.13](https://scholarsbank.uoregon.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/3cf8362c-b20b-4bad-b854-67f30a7185fd/content) · [OSIP imagery](https://imagery.oregonexplorer.info/arcgis/rest/services/OSIP_2024/OSIP_2024_SL/ImageServer) · [2017 surface](campus-surface-2017-2026-11-24.md).

Of 1,954 sampled raster positions, 1,116 hit both exterior models. Their median absolute difference fell from 4.173 m to 0.236 m. The full new footprint's median is 0.188 m. These are comparisons against the same 2017 surface used to place the roof profiles. Ten pairs of rendered views cover all sides, the north court and both planted roof decks, with one added draw call in each matched view. The building and shelter supports clear all 7,710 active route edges, with a minimum clearance of 6.39 m. Two adjacent context batches retain their original vertex attributes in the matched scene comparisons.

The local ledger records L2 following comparison with the [Knight Library exterior](knight-library-registration-2026-11-23.md). Dormer sizes and spacing, portions of the window rhythm, canopy dimensions, planted bed edges and threshold levels remain approximate. Doors use the existing shared classroom preview. The GLB contains original geometry and material colors. The source imagery and measured comparisons are in `exterior-l2/east-campus-20261124/` in the research worktree.

The editable [parameters](../data/metadata/global-scholars-site-review.json) and [generator](../scripts/build-global-scholars-site-review.py) reproduce the GLB and descriptor byte for byte. Run `python scripts/build-global-scholars-site-review.py --audit <audit.json>` from the project root. The output contains 63,922 building triangles and 304 terrain-following paving triangles. It stores 191,784 triangle corners using 127,313 indexed vertices while retaining their positions, normals and colors.
