# Science Walk east of the Cascade arch

The campus museum guide distinguishes the raised bridge from the lower route. It sends visitors down the courtyard stair and east along a paved path to benches at the Dawn redwood. The 2007 Volcanology survey places the tree east of the bunker. The Campus Plan identifies the lower east–west Science Walk along the southern edge of Onyx Green. The lower path was registered against the 2026 access map, the 1991 circulation diagram and four Lane County orthophotos from 2017 through 2025. The east end flares into the visible paving at the west side of Onyx Green.
