# Allen Hall west frontage, 2026-11-04

The [UO historic survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/allenhall2025) identifies the 1953–54 west wing, its brick and limestone exterior, nine relief plaques, three stories and flat roof. The [UO architecture history](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/allen-hall) documents the 2012–13 expansion. A [Docomomo Oregon photograph](https://www.docomomo-oregon.org/resources/allen-hall/) was consulted to check the renovated west elevation. Reference photographs are linked only, not redistributed or used as textures. Original overlay geometry is CC BY 4.0.

On `roof-7b7893d6` (a curated approximate locator inside the LiDAR-derived footprint), a reversible, source-pinned overlay adds an illustrative limestone-colored panel, two horizontal bands of glazing, simplified plaques and roofline louvers to the western wall. The panel is aligned to sampled existing mesh points; its precise extent, sizes, color, fenestration and plaque details have not been surveyed. [Before](images/allen-west-before.png) / [after](images/allen-west-after.png). Existing flat roof and original mesh remain; the surveyed flat roof shape is supported but exact roof features are unverified.

The existing footprint can include tree canopy and omit courtyards. The UO survey predates or schematically omits parts of the later addition, and the east and south wings cannot be mapped confidently to the irregular model; they remain generic. The sunken west courtyard, entrance, planting and accessible routes were not reconstructed. No terrain, trees, interiors, base GLB or navigation data were changed. The overlay has no separate collision; no route graph edge was found within 0.5 m of a conservative rectangle enclosing the panel. This is not a clearance or accessibility survey. Keep level 1 pending evidence and user-defined criteria.

## Local draw-call consolidation

The Anstett south and lamps layers are kept as independently source-pinned JSON, but their static vegetation meshes and their static prop meshes are merged by kind at load time, only for these two layers. Building meshes and identities remain separate. Identity transforms and compatible materials are checked before merging. Automated coverage verifies every position and color value is preserved; the matching Anstett camera capture was pixel-identical before and after. The original less-than-180 draw-call check passed with the Allen overlay enabled. Local merging trades some per-mesh frustum granularity for fewer draws within this one site.
