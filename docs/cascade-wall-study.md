# Cascade Hall exterior wall study - 2026-11-04

The [UO Department of Earth Sciences](https://naturalsciences.uoregon.edu/earth-sciences/contact-us) places Cascade Hall north of the EMU and describes its south entrance and courtyard. The [UO Libraries photograph by Rick Gersbach, 2008](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/catalog/3b89276e18452b0a1d8c0ad5c9034409) shows dark brick and courtyard arches. It is CC BY-NC-ND 4.0 and is linked for reference only; no photograph is copied or redistributed. The [UO architecture guide](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/cascade-hall) documents the newer Cascade Hall in the science complex, distinct from the earlier Cascade Annex.

Source building `roof-f42d2ea3` has an anchor-within-footprint identity in the campus manifest. A reversible, source-hash-pinned wall patch recolors 348 matched vertical source vertices to an illustrative dark brick tone; roofs and nonmatching surfaces remain unchanged. [South before](images/cascade-south-before.png) / [after](images/cascade-south-candidate.png), [east before](images/cascade-east-before.png) / [after](images/cascade-east-candidate.png). The patch does not add geometry, affect navigation or alter the existing terrain, vegetation or building interiors. It introduces no additional draw object. The target is an artistic approximation, not a color measurement.

The source roof and walls are simplified. Courtyard, arched opening positions, entrance, precise material variation, all elevations and existing conditions remain unverified; no arch, fountain or art has been recreated. This is a material-only partial improvement and remains level 1. The JSON patch is the editable runtime input: the standalone GLB and Blender snapshot remain unchanged and do not show the recolor without the overlay. Reference image rights do not extend to redistribution. Original patch parameters are CC BY 4.0. Source matching checks reject changes in the expected asset and vertices; visual review and existing browser and performance regression tests check the running scene.
