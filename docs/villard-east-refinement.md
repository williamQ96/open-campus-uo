# Villard Hall east facade

The source-pinned procedural replacement retains `roof-399c648e`, the original campus GLB, and the approximate campus footprint. It interprets the east facade using [Daderot's 2017 CC0 photograph](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Villard_Hall_-_University_of_Oregon_-_Eugene,_Oregon_-_DSC09617.jpg) and the [UO 2017 assessment, east elevation](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/171103_uo_villard_hall_robinson_assessment_final_reduced.pdf) (page 34, PDF page 40). The original CC0 photograph is archived with metadata and hash; the UO document is linked, not redistributed. No photo texture is used.

The simplified mass, tower roofs, windows, columns, entry porch, rail and ornamental details are interpretations, not a dimensional reconstruction. Eight shrubs represent a garden shown in the photograph; placement and species are illustrative. Non-east elevations are placeholders; current conditions, dimensions, collision clearance and accessibility are unverified. Building remains level 1. Interior is untouched.

The east scene was reviewed before and after; regression checks cover selection, rendering modes and the campus landmark scene. [Before](images/villard-east-before.png) / [after](images/villard-east-after.png).

## West/theater junction screen, 2026-11-04

The [UO 2025 survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/villardhall2025) identifies Robinson Theatre as attached on the west, while the [2017 assessment](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/171103_uo_villard_hall_robinson_assessment_final_reduced.pdf), PDF page 41, shows existing construction and marks a proposed removal/new connection; the proposal is not evidence of the post-2025 as-built junction. The approximate LiDAR-derived project footprints and replacement geometry show a gap, which should not be treated as a real passage. Additional current exterior imagery or measured plans are needed before reconstructing or blocking the junction. No geometry, collision, navigation or grade was changed.

The prior requested camera at `(-495, -185.5)` was inside the existing theater collision and the viewer's return logic correctly fell back to its start. Collision-free test positions `(-465, -205)`, `(-465, -165)`, and `(-465, -185)` produced the requested coordinates in first person, providing [four repeatable views](images/villard-west-seam-screen-2026-11-04.png); the two close lateral views are dominated by existing walls. The test `tests/browser/villard-west-view.spec.js` checks the blocked original coordinate and restored camera positions rather than mistaking fallback for the requested view. This is a scene diagnostic, not evidence that the model's gap, collision or accessibility matches reality. Local only; unpublished.
