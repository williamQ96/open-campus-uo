# Road vehicle prop review, 2026-11-25

The outdoor inventory group `PROP-02` covers four generic road vehicle bodies used as moving and parked campus props. The editable procedural source is [props.js](../apps/web-preview/src/props.js). The [standalone GLB](../apps/web-preview/public/props/campus-props.glb) contains these bodies and the bicycle and scooter. The archives referenced by the inventory retain earlier versions.

The [UO Motor Pool](https://transportation.uoregon.edu/uo-motor-pool) documents campus vehicle types. Manufacturer dimensions give a representative envelope for each generic model. The scene uses original geometry and materials. Its type selection and parking positions are authored for the visualization.

The sedan has a low cabin and trunk. The SUV has a taller roof and full-height tailgate. The van has solid cargo sides and split rear door glazing. The pickup has a separate open bed. Twelve front, side and rear render views were reviewed for silhouette, glazing, wheel arch clearance, lights and mirrors. Each tire reaches the model ground plane. All four shapes fit their exported bounds. Each stays within 3,030 position vertices and uses a single mesh.

The runtime derives scaled collision capsules from the body width, length and height. The rounded silhouette approximates corners and excludes mirrors. All 20 model envelopes fit their authored slot footprints at runtime scale and yaw. Comparing the horizontal model contact patches to the source heightfield gives a maximum absolute difference of 0.164 m at the four tire centers. Parking permission, terrain and paving heights remain approximate. The source review rates the generic external prop group L2.

The browser checks compare all six exported position arrays against the procedural source. They also compare the two cycle meshes and all six node transforms against the older archive. `npm run export:props` regenerates the GLB using the local viewer.
