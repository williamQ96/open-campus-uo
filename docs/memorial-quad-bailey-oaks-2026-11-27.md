# Bailey oak rows and Memorial Quad walks

The eight campus inventory positions form two rows of four around the ring. IDs 616–622 are registered as Pyramidal English oak, and ID 623 is registered as English oak. The 2007 landscape survey identifies this layout as the Robert Chase Bailey memorial planting. The model uses the current inventory positions and reported tree heights and crown spreads. Trunk diameters and branch profiles use visual estimates. The age and replacement history of individual trees remain open. Five procedural placements in the work area are retired after checks against the four dated orthophotos. [Campus tree inventory](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/trees) · [UO landscape survey, p. 2](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/memorial_quad_.pdf#page=2) · [Lane County aerials](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer).

The 2021 and 2023 bare-canopy views show the east walk outside the tree row and the X between Johnson Lane and the ring. The model refits the ring, restores the two crossings north of it and keeps the southern diagonals connected to the east walk. Eight root openings cover 26.45 m². The total Quad package covers 2,462.42 m² of paving and 4,440.47 m² of lawn. Pavement widths beneath crowns use fitted dimensions. [Tree input and aerial receipts](../data/metadata/memorial-quad-bailey-oaks.json) · [Quad paving and route input](../data/metadata/paving-corridors-l2.json).

The 259-node and 269-edge route patch uses the same paving as the visual layer. The nearest authored edge is 2.013 m from a trunk collision envelope. The tree batches, their collision boxes, the retired tree ranges and the Quad surface install together. Retiring the earlier placements works with the refined and legacy pilot meshes. [Oak mesh generator](../scripts/build-memorial-quad-bailey-oaks.py) · [Surface exporter](../scripts/build-paving-corridors-l2.py).

## Verification and publication

Both production browser profiles load all 611 local exterior IDs, 380 resources and eight registered oaks. All six sampled routes stay within 0.13 m of the modeled pavement. The two diagonals measure 36.00 m and 34.94 m, and the east walk test measures 75.32 m. The shortest gap between a sampled route and a trunk envelope is 2.158 m. The existing PLC, library and plaque checks pass. Both road and path meshes match the earlier index, position, normal and color buffers. Two repeat surface exports, 56 preview hashes, 199 unit tests, 18 focused browser checks and 503 asset archive records pass. The update is included in the Memorial Quadrangle Pages release with the 610-model inventory.
