# 360 and 362 Sunset Drive exteriors

One legacy roof covered two street addresses and filled the space between them. The two municipal outlines measure 225.609 m² for 360 and 212.246 m² for 362, with about 6.166 m of separation. The local scene keeps `roof-ceb2cf04` for 360 and gives 362 the new `sunset-362` ID. The address points are on separate Lane County parcels 1803042100700 and 1703333402902. [City outlines](https://gis.eugene-or.gov/arcgis/rest/services/PWM/COE_Basemap_Tiled_Pro/MapServer/3) · [Lane County parcels](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/PlanMaps/AddressParcel/MapServer/1) · [County aerials](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer).

The 360 model has light siding, a double garage beneath a separate street gable, two green awnings and a raised terrace with pale rails. The 362 model uses a shallow gable, brown siding, twin garage doors and two balconies with open bays beneath them. The landing elevations, balcony depths and street-side grades are approximate. Both models keep explicit empty entrance lists because pedestrian entrances were screened or could not be placed confidently.
