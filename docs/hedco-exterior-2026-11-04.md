The [architect](https://www.hackerarchitects.com/education/college-of-education-complex) and [UO architecture guide](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/hedco-education-building) describe the education complex and its courtyard. A [UO aerial photograph](https://provost.uoregon.edu/college-education-dean-search) was consulted as reference, not copied or redistributed. These references support the broad materials and identity, not the exact modeled window locations.

The existing `context-b0010` is an approximate context envelope: its rectangular, flat-roofed body does **not** reproduce the real building's complex footprint, courtyard and pitched roofs. The new source-pinned layer adds two schematic brick faces with a regular pattern of framed vertical windows; the wall color patch recolors 16 matched vertical source vertices. The pattern, entrances, other facades, roof, footprint, height and current site conditions are not surveyed. The building remains **level 1**, with substantial work before comparison to the Knight example. [Before](images/hedco-before-2026-11-04.png) / [after](images/hedco-after-2026-11-04.png).

The additions merge into the existing building mesh after its identity and 1,976 source vertices are checked. The original GLB remains unchanged. Six thin collision strips border the existing modeled walls and no route-graph edge intersects them with a 0.5 m buffer; this is not a real-world survey or accessibility validation. Existing surrounding models, terrain, vegetation, layout and interiors are retained.
