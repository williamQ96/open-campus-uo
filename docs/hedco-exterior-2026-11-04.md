# HEDCO Education exterior, local partial refinement — 2026-11-04

[UO College of Education](https://education.uoregon.edu/coe-campus-and-community) describes the 2009 HEDCO Education Building and its red brick and tall vertical windows. The [architect](https://www.hackerarchitects.com/education/college-of-education-complex) and [UO architecture guide](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/hedco-education-building) describe the education complex and its courtyard. A [UO aerial photograph](https://provost.uoregon.edu/college-education-dean-search) was consulted as reference, not copied or redistributed. These references support the broad materials and identity, not the exact modeled window locations.

The existing `context-b0010` is an approximate context envelope: its rectangular, flat-roofed body does **not** reproduce the real building's complex footprint, courtyard and pitched roofs. The new source-pinned layer adds two schematic brick faces with a regular pattern of framed vertical windows; the wall color patch recolors 16 matched vertical source vertices. The pattern, entrances, other facades, roof, footprint, height and current site conditions are not surveyed. The building remains **level 1**, with substantial work before comparison to the Knight example. [Before](images/hedco-before-2026-11-04.png) / [after](images/hedco-after-2026-11-04.png).

The additions merge into the existing building mesh after its identity and 1,976 source vertices are checked. The original GLB remains unchanged. In the matched local overview both screenshots recorded 20 draw calls; this is not a hardware benchmark. Six thin collision strips border the existing modeled walls and no route-graph edge intersects them with a 0.5 m buffer; this is not a real-world survey or accessibility validation. Existing surrounding models, terrain, vegetation, layout and interiors are retained.

Editable source consists of `apps/web-preview/public/scene/hedco-exterior-detail.json` and `hedco-wall-color.json`, pinned in `presentation.json`. [Independent GLB](../assets/exterior-refinement/hedco-exterior-detail-2026-11-04.glb) contains only the generated facade overlay, not the base building, wall recolor or collision data. Its 6,984 positions, normals and colors match the generated runtime overlay exactly; it is one mesh, not a per-component Blender scene. Blender was unavailable; no new `.blend` snapshot was created. Original illustrative geometry is CC BY 4.0; source images are reference-only under their respective rights. No software was installed and no deployment was made.

Validation: 124 Node tests, 14 preservation tests, production build, formatting, 119 archived asset checks, viewer override hashes and 10 targeted browser tests passed, including selection in both material modes, merge attributes, standalone exports, navigation and the unchanged <180 draw-call budget. A metadata-only simulation carried 42 detail layers; it is not a full scene rebuild. The build still reports its existing chunk-size warning.
