# Pilot-area bench seats — 2026-11-04

Nine pre-existing inferred pilot benches now use three separated wooden seat slats each, complementing the earlier slatted backs. The general slatted furniture vocabulary is informed by the archived [2019 Anstett photo](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anstett_Hall,_University_of_Oregon,_Eugene_-_DPLA_-_7f05aff2c79e236edd0bed0104b6f145.jpg) (Gary Halvorson / Oregon State Archives, CC BY 4.0). The photograph does not establish the exact styles or positions of these nine modeled benches. No photograph or third-party geometry is embedded; original detail and source-selection data are CC BY 4.0. PROP-01 stays level 1.

The reversible source-pinned selection collapses 72 distinct original seat-corner positions / 216 source vertices. The replacement adds 27 boxes to the existing bench detail layer, preserving the original seat center, height and outer bounds. The detail is merged into the verified existing furniture wood mesh, preserving its material and avoiding a separate draw object. The source GLB and Blender are not modified.
