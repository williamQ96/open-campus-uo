# Dads’ Gates and the North Campus approach

Dads’ Gates stand south of East 11th Avenue between McKenzie Hall and the Miller Theatre Complex. The National Register nomination documents the two ten-foot main leaves, the two seven-foot pedestrian openings with paired leaves, the iron fence, tall lantern pylons and the inward opening direction. The University’s landscape survey supplies the left and right fence alignments and the relationship between the gates, sidewalks and theater drive. [National Register nomination, PDF pp. 5–7](https://npgallery.nps.gov/NRHP/GetAsset/NRHP/04000829_text) · [UO Dads’ Gates Axis survey, PDF p. 2](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/dads_gates_axis_04_30_07.pdf) · [UO cloverleaf survey, PDF p. 3](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/04_dads_gates_cloverleaf-rfs.pdf).

The model keeps all six leaves open. It builds the lattice pylons, scrollwork, upright iron, glass lanterns, bronze medallion forms and the pedestrian gate headers. The main opening is 6.10 m between hinge centers, the two pedestrian openings are 2.14 m between their centers, and the tallest pylons reach about 3.21 m. Fine ironwork, paint color and the medallion relief are estimates. Outer fence endpoints follow the landscape plan and photographed approaches. The nomination’s 120-foot description gives a historical scale reference and the overall model span remains approximate. The model uses original geometric components and simplified medallion faces. [Campus-side view, July 2019](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=V8efquUl9ynvY3eNnV1uOA&heading=359.86) · [East side, July 2024](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=weXS_5YP34AiuLO-FE859A&heading=209.09) · [West side, July 2024](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=h0-9BUiVhiK72i9OQOzesw&heading=141.58).

The initial north registration used approximately 807.11 m² of asphalt and 304.96 m² of concrete. These surfaces cover the arc around the traffic island, service drive, paired walks, the west crossing and the approach to the foot of the theater terrace. The red curb traces follow the dated street views. Four planted areas, two beeches and two small pines use the survey and tree inventory. The model suppresses a procedural tree inside the west walk. Heights, crown sizes and planted ground outlines use estimates. [Lane County aerial service](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [UO tree inventory](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/trees).

The initial north export included 32 navigation edges. From the East 11th Avenue anchor, both production previews returned 93.60 m to the south end of the drive, 62.81 m to the west walk and 78.59 m to the approach below the theater terrace. The viewer can shortcut between graph endpoints over clear ground. All three gate openings and five paved standing points are passable in the model. Four fence and leaf probes hit collision. The street and route reconstruction reflects the dated evidence and uses approximate surface geometry.
