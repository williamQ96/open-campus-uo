# Kincaid Green and Lokey Education courtyard

The 2007 survey identifies the historic horseshoe walk and five Douglas-firs marking the end of Kincaid Street. Their trunk positions follow the campus tree inventory. Lane County's 2017, 2021 and 2025 orthophotos establish the visible concrete walks, lawn edges and the roof of Scholar's Walk. The campus design-area plan identifies the green as the historic entry landscape for the Education buildings. [Landscape survey, pp. 2–3](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/kincaid_green_06_12_07.pdf) · [Campus design-area plan](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2023-12/principle-12-design-area-special-conditions_2022_23_.pdf) · [Lane County orthophotos](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer).

The courtyard study photographs the curved brick paving, brick and timber seating and covered walk. Its field description establishes the southwest exit and the courtyard's relation to the education wings and garden. The model follows the visible paving and connects around the east end of Education East. The north garden route stops beside the existing exterior wall envelope. [Courtyard study, printed pp. 97–98](https://scholarsbank.uoregon.edu//bitstreams/a054136d-556e-4939-a611-daed2b31686a/download) · [College of Education site description](https://education.uoregon.edu/coe-campus-and-community) · [Existing exterior review](plc-lokey-tykeson-exterior-review-2026-11-24.md).

## South pavilion and staircase

The 2021 and 2025 aerials show the diagonal pavilion roof and the stair approach on the north side of Education South. NOAA point returns establish an intermediate platform and a higher approach near the building. The university's 2006 photograph shows the stair and the 2014 field study describes the covered intermediate landing. The model adds a hipped roof, four posts, a landing and 19 representative treads in two flights. Tread count, rail details and the upper threshold are estimates. The stair geometry is separate from the campus route graph pending a verified second-floor threshold. [Education East survey, p. 5](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/educationeast05_30_07.pdf) · [Courtyard study, printed p. 98](https://scholarsbank.uoregon.edu//bitstreams/a054136d-556e-4939-a611-daed2b31686a/download).

The historic resource survey distinguishes the original Scholar's Walk between Education East and West from the 1980 covered walk leading south to Education South. The 31.40 m structure registered above is the covered south walk. [Education West survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/educationwest05_30_07.pdf).
