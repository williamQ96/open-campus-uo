# Terrain and land cover at L2

ENV-04 covers the complete 2 km × 2 km foundation. The default local viewer adopts one indexed mesh with 160,801 vertices and 320,000 triangles. It uses the same horizontal grid as the original scene. The water colors follow nine USGS NHD areas and waterbodies over 0.311 km². The 2024 NAIP comparison shows the course of the Willamette, the Millrace and nearby ponds. The ground material uses an original palette with aerial estimates of vegetation and exposed surfaces. The reduced haze on this mesh keeps the river visible in the overview. [Editable ground recipe](../scripts/build-landcover-l2.py) · [Source records and statistics](../data/metadata/landcover-l2.json) · [U.S. Geological Survey hydrography](https://hydro.nationalmap.gov/arcgis/rest/services/nhd/MapServer) · [USDA NAIP archive record](../data/metadata/naip-2024-main-campus-reference.json).

Published Oregon Lidar Consortium bare-earth data supplies the 2023 ground profile. The 2014–2015 Lane County survey covers gaps, tied to the 2023 profile by the 0.070 m median on common dry ground. Source transitions are feathered. The fitting uses 524 footprints, 608 walkable entrance segments, 139 corridor features, the route graph and active landscape meshes to retain existing contact levels. It preserves the Ford/Arena grade and the scene border. A total of 62,339 vertices move, including 51,521 by at least 0.10 m. At 2023-sampled vertices eligible for change, the median absolute difference falls from 0.4181 m to 0.0662 m. This is a fit to the reference data. Independent field validation remains open. [DOGAMI elevation service](https://gis.dogami.oregon.gov/arcgis/rest/services/lidar/DIGITAL_TERRAIN_MODEL_MOSAIC/ImageServer) · [DOGAMI data distribution policy](https://www.oregon.gov/dogami/lidar/Documents/DOGAMI_Lidar_Data_Distribution_Policy_20170411.pdf).

## Scene and verification

- The default navigation heightfield and terrain ray mesh derive their grade from the same pinned GLB used to draw the foundation. The previous asset and field reappear if validation fails. The existing 0.018 m render offset keeps surface layers separated. The original foundation is available for comparison with `/?exterior-review=landcover-baseline` in the development viewer.
- The source route graph and 608 walkable entrance segments show zero height change at 112,524 samples spaced 0.5 m or closer. Exact browser comparisons also preserve all 608 gate and approach coordinates and their sampled heights. The eight park, river, campus and hardscape probes match the expected visible and collision geometry within 0.002 mm.
- Seven matched WebGL views use equal triangle and draw counts. Desktop and phone production load the same repeated-stage, installed, built and served bytes. Browser tests exercise toon, PBR, damaged-file fallback, the east Arena door and the Knight, Johnson and EMU hardscape.

The shoreline follows the reference on a 5 m grid and appears softened at that scale. The map does not provide a water-level, river-depth or current access survey. Existing grass, paving and road models remain tracked under ENV-05. The original Blender foundation is the L1 snapshot. The L2 ground has a reproducible GLB builder, with original elevation and hydrography files kept outside the runtime.

Run `python scripts/build-landcover-l2.py --inputs INPUTS --terrain TERRAIN --stage OUTPUT` using the archived grade ties and NHD extracts in `terrain-landcover-238-l2` and the adjacent `dogami-terrain-20261123` terrain archive. The package is local and uncommitted.
