# Johnson Hall rear exterior and planting

2026-11-03. Local, unpublished. Asset `roof-906c030e`; landscape inventory `ENV-03`.

The existing Johnson rear elevation had windows and green awnings but no central entrance. An independent source-pinned, data-driven detail layer adds a simplified double door, stone surround, transom, projecting canopy with corbels and short decorative stair, plus two flanking shrub masses and smaller groundcover. The source scene, building geometry, terrain and interiors are not regenerated. [Before](images/johnson-rear-before.png) and [after](images/johnson-rear-refinement.png) use the same fixed camera and the actual campus GLB with the detail layer; nearby inferred trees obscure parts of the view. They are controlled browser renders, not photographs.

The source is the approved archived 2012-07-25 [rear photograph](../data/reference-images/photos/johnson-hall/rear-2012.jpg), Gary Halvorson / Oregon State Archives, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); source and exact attribution are in the [reference manifest](../data/reference-images/manifest.json). Original geometry is CC BY 4.0 and source code Apache-2.0. No image pixels are embedded. The doorway, canopy, stairs and planting are stylized interpretations; dimensions, current conditions, rear access, hidden sides and plant species are not verified. The entry and stairs are decorative and do not establish collision or accessible routing. The photo dates the reference, not the present state.

The scene layer [data](../apps/web-preview/public/scene/johnson-rear-detail.json) is pinned to the current campus-expanded GLB SHA-256, and [generic geometry construction](../apps/web-preview/src/exterior-detail.js) groups primitives by kind and material while retaining Johnson's selection ID. The rebuild guard carries the layer only when both source and layer hashes match; a changed source stops for review instead of silently placing details on different geometry. The original GLB remains authoritative and untouched.

Validation: production build; 46 JS unit tests, including source mismatch, geometry validation and semantic identity; seven rebuild-preservation tests; six targeted browser tests covering landmarks, material modes, building cards and phone sizes; the fixed-camera visual comparison above. Browser uses Chrome/SwiftShader; this is not a device performance or dimensional accuracy claim. The overall building and ENV-03 remain level 1 pending broader reference-supported refinement and comparison with the Knight example; no level-2 standard is claimed.
