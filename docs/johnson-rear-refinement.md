# Johnson Hall rear exterior and planting

2026-11-03. Local, unpublished. Asset `roof-906c030e`; landscape inventory `ENV-03`.

The existing Johnson rear elevation had windows and green awnings but no central entrance. An independent source-pinned, data-driven detail layer adds a simplified double door, stone surround, transom, projecting canopy with corbels and short decorative stair, plus two flanking shrub masses and smaller groundcover. The source scene, building geometry, terrain and interiors are not regenerated. [Before](images/johnson-rear-before.png) and [after](images/johnson-rear-refinement.png) use the same fixed camera and the actual campus GLB with the detail layer; nearby inferred trees obscure parts of the view. They are controlled browser renders, not photographs.

The source is the approved archived 2012-07-25 [rear photograph](../data/reference-images/photos/johnson-hall/rear-2012.jpg), Gary Halvorson / Oregon State Archives, [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); source and exact attribution are in the [reference manifest](../data/reference-images/manifest.json). Original geometry is CC BY 4.0 and source code Apache-2.0. No image pixels are embedded. The doorway, canopy, stairs and planting are stylized interpretations; dimensions, current conditions, rear access, hidden sides and plant species are not verified. The entry and stairs are decorative and do not establish collision or accessible routing. The photo dates the reference, not the present state.

The scene layer [data](../apps/web-preview/public/scene/johnson-rear-detail.json) is pinned to the current campus-expanded GLB SHA-256, and [generic geometry construction](../apps/web-preview/src/exterior-detail.js) groups primitives by kind and material while retaining Johnson's selection ID. The rebuild guard carries the layer only when both source and layer hashes match; a changed source stops for review instead of silently placing details on different geometry. The original GLB remains authoritative and untouched.

Validation: production build; 46 JS unit tests, including source mismatch, geometry validation and semantic identity; seven rebuild-preservation tests; six targeted browser tests covering landmarks, material modes, building cards and phone sizes; the fixed-camera visual comparison above. Browser uses Chrome/SwiftShader; this is not a device performance or dimensional accuracy claim. The overall building and ENV-03 remain level 1 pending broader reference-supported refinement and comparison with the Knight example; no level-2 standard is claimed.

## Shallow roof and continuous side/rear cornices, 2026-11-04

The [UO 2025 Johnson survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/johnsonhall2025) describes a metal gable-on-hip roof and terra-cotta cornice. The archived [2024 USDA NAIP pilot aerial](../data/reference-images/aerial/naip-2024-pilot-rgb.png), US public domain, shows a light roof with broad slopes; the [2012 Gary Halvorson / Oregon State Archives rear photograph](../data/reference-images/photos/johnson-hall/rear-2012.jpg), CC BY 4.0, shows the rear cornice and entry. Sources are used as visual references, not runtime textures. The aerial is 0.6 m/pixel and does not establish a surveyed roof profile or current conditions.

A shallow, light hipped roof sits above the original flat roof, with simplified cornice bands and brackets along the rear and both sides. It approximates the general sloping roof; the surveyed gable-on-hip profile and exact eave details are not yet reconstructed. Three existing small source roof markers are visually covered by the new roof; simplified replacements keep their original horizontal centers at a higher elevation. Their identities, dimensions and actual existence are unverified. The previous 36 rear-layer objects, north portico, corrected frieze, entrances, original source mesh, vegetation, campus layout and interior are retained. Added geometry does not change navigation or collision, and does not certify entry accessibility.

[Isolated rear/end comparisons](images/johnson-roof-isolated-2026-11-04.png) show before left and after right; [north before](images/johnson-roof-north-before-2026-11-04.png) / [after](images/johnson-roof-north-after-2026-11-04.png) and [south before](images/johnson-roof-south-before-2026-11-04.png) / [after](images/johnson-roof-south-after-2026-11-04.png) retain the actual surrounding scene. Trees partly obscure the rear. Roof slope, accurate gable ends, rooftop equipment, east and west elevations, entrance dimensions and post-2024 condition remain unresolved; the building remains **level 1**, not certified equivalent to Knight.

## Gable-on-hip roof correction, 2026-11-04

The shallow truncated hip above was an interim approximation. The [UO historic survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/johnsonhall2025) calls the roof gable-on-hip, and the dated [2024 NAIP reference](../data/metadata/naip-2024-reference.json) supplies broad roof context. This follow-up replaces the truncated plateau with an approximate shallow roof having a central ridge and hipped ends. The three illustrative roof markers keep the same horizontal centers; the outer two are lowered and tilted to follow the new roof planes. Their actual identities and dimensions remain unverified. The existing roof overlay, front and rear facades, entrance, vegetation, collision and interior otherwise remain unchanged.

[Isolated rear and end comparison](images/johnson-gable-hip-isolated-2026-11-04.png) shows the earlier hip at left and the new form at right. [Campus north before](images/johnson-gable-hip-north-before-2026-11-04.png) / [after](images/johnson-gable-hip-north-after-2026-11-04.png), [south before](images/johnson-gable-hip-south-before-2026-11-04.png) / [after](images/johnson-gable-hip-south-after-2026-11-04.png) retain the existing scene; the low roof is partly hidden by the front parapet, and trees obscure part of the rear. Ridge length, pitch, metal finish, roof equipment, corner details and the precise as-built gable-on-hip construction remain approximate; this is not a measured reconstruction. Johnson remains **level 1**, not a claim of Knight equivalence.

The former [layer](../assets/exterior-refinement/johnson-before-gable-hip-2026-11-04.json) and [generator](../assets/exterior-refinement/exterior-detail-before-gable-hip-2026-11-04.js) are preserved, as is the previous overlay GLB.
