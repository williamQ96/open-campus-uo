# Longhouse-area south facade — provisional local refinement, 2026-11-04

The existing unidentified `roof-f736712f` exterior has an illustrative 23-box south facade with dark timber-like panels and a large glazed opening. A bounded source-pinned palette also changes the target's existing wall and roof colors. This is a provisional visual study, not an identification or measured reconstruction; the inventory retains its unidentified name and level 1. The other elevations, old gabled roof geometry, window layout, dimensions and existing estimated entrance are not verified. In particular, changing roof color does not establish an accurate roof shape. Campus placement, building interior and official identity are unchanged.

The [UO Longhouse history and photographs](https://longhouse.uoregon.edu/history-many-nations-longhouse), [UO architectural history](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/many-nations-longhouse), [UO photograph](https://news.uoregon.edu/content/geography-conference-set-many-nations-longhouse), and [2011 UO conceptual plan](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/mnlh_conceptualplanreport02-07-2011_1.pdf) informed broad wood appearance and the existing large south window. The conceptual plan also describes an existing living roof; its proposed future expansion was not treated as built. Approximate geographic coordinates, source footprint and the archived 2024 NAIP overview support a plausible location only; they are not a registered current survey. Facade divisions and colors are interpretive. Cultural motifs were not recreated. Linked third-party photos are reference only and are not bundled.
