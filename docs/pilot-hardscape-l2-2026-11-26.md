# Pilot stairs, terraces and raised planters at L2

ENV-06 covers the pilot hardscape around Johnson Hall, Knight Library and the Erb Memorial Union, with the earlier raised-planter record at Oregon Hall. The default scene now carries the reconstructed stairs, terraces and planter. The earlier Condon, Fenton and Chapman frontage records remain linked in the inventory.

## Evidence and modeled details

- Johnson Hall's north entrance has ten stone treads, an upper landing and two inset iron rails. The archived front photo establishes the stair-and-rail arrangement. The new flight meets the current heightfield and modeled doors, which leave about 0.42 m of total rise. The physical risers are visibly steeper in the photo and the current scene elevations are estimates. [UO building survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-01/johnsonhall_2025.pdf) · [Archived front photo](../data/reference-images/photos/johnson-hall/front-pd.jpg) · [Earlier rail review](johnson-north-rail-audit-2026-11-10.md).
- Knight Library keeps both modeled north portals and their lamps. The replacement uses a 15-tread west approach and an eight-tread east approach with handrails, stepped stone sides and a short paved apron at the east foot. The survey and photographs establish the two approaches. The modeled tread counts, dimensions, apron limits and levels fit the existing building and terrain. [UO building survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-01/knightlibrary_2025.pdf) · [Archived north photo](../data/reference-images/photos/knight-library/north-oblique-b-2018.jpg) · [Building registration](knight-library-registration-2026-11-23.md).
- The northwest EMU amphitheater is a separate feature from the south Green. Landscape sheets L200, L300 and L400, 2024 Oregon imagery and dated public street views locate the curved paving, three tiers, raised stage and three surrounding low wall runs. The new heights and paving divisions are fitted to the campus terrain. The courtyard retains the representative furniture from the pilot furnishing layer. The low walls have rendered geometry, and collision is defined for the six stair rails. [UO landscape drawings](https://pages.uoregon.edu/moxoby/EMU/Documents/EMU_CD/EMU100CD/EMU100CDs-PKG4-VOLUME%201-Landscape.pdf) · [2024 Oregon orthophoto service](https://imagery.oregonexplorer.info/arcgis/rest/services/OSIP_2024/OSIP_2024_SL/ImageServer) · [Landscape architect's project record](https://www.cameronmccarthy.com/project/university-of-oregon-erb-memorial-union-emu/).
- Oregon Hall's south planter has masonry faces, a continuous coping with joints and a separate lawn surface. The survey and imagery identify the planter. The registered footprint and existing collision are retained. The coping section, material colors and levels remain approximate. [UO building survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/oregonhall2025) · [Earlier south frontage study](oregon-south-refinement.md#raised-lawn-candidate).

## Runtime and verification

- The builder exports original geometry with 3,788 walkable triangles and 5,244 detail triangles. The loader checks the asset bytes and three dependent site hashes, then applies four exact source-index cuts together. The five matched views and geometry comparison verified the removal of 576 source triangles and exact retention of the surviving indices and vertex attributes. Invalid source anchors restore the preceding site.
- All 33 straight tread centers, three curved tread centers and the east apron resolved to the model surface above the heightfield. Six probes hit the rail envelopes. The browser walked up the west approach and stopped below its tall side. Checked the six rail boxes against all 7,705 edges in the pinned source graph. The smallest separation was 3.725 m.
- Desktop and phone production previews mounted all six rail envelopes and retained 524 selectable buildings, 294 batch members and two separate roof refinements. Five sample centers per viewport returned the model as player support. Both Knight entrance interaction volumes now follow their new landings at 7.076 and 7.138 m in scene coordinates. The repeat export, installed assets, production bundle files and bytes served from the local preview matched by SHA-256.
- All 197 unit tests and 13 targeted browser tests passed. The browser run includes pointer entry and return at Knight west, keyboard entry and return at Knight east, and the earlier EMU and Oregon-Jaqua site cases.
- The model, editable placement and source register and runtime descriptor are local and uncommitted. To reproduce, run `python scripts/build-pilot-hardscape.py --stage PATH --audit PATH` with separate output and audit paths. Use `/?exterior-review=hardscape-baseline` for the scene comparison.
