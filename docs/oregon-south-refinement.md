# Oregon Hall west-wing frontage

A reversible source-pinned overlay adds a brick facade with horizontal window bands on the inferred south side of the western wing of `roof-11d15952`. The [UO cultural resource survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/oregonhall2025) describes the three-story west wing, brick and horizontal windows and includes a historical photograph and site plan. The source image was consulted 2026-11-04, linked only and not redistributed or used as a texture. Original procedural geometry is CC BY 4.0.

The named building is an approximate nearest-roof match, and the south-facing section is inferred by comparing the survey plan with the existing LiDAR-derived outline. The thick backing panel masks old generic windows and is not a measured construction detail. Exact building boundaries, window spacing, material colors, height, entrances, raised planter and present conditions remain unverified. The other elevations and east wing remain generic, and an existing tree obscures part of this view. [Before](images/oregon-south-before.png) / [after](images/oregon-south-after.png). No landscape, collision, route, interior or original GLB change is implied. The building remains level 1.

## Raised lawn candidate

A separate reversible layer adds an illustrative rectangular lawn and low retaining edge in that area. Its extent, material, height and present condition are not surveyed; the plan was not georeferenced. An input-data screening found the closest generated tree stem outside the proposed rectangle, but does not constitute a current tree survey or prove full canopy clearance. No tree or route is moved; collision and accessible passage are not modeled by this layer. The retaining edge has prop semantics and the lawn vegetation semantics, so neither identifies the building. [Before](images/oregon-planter-before.png) / [after](images/oregon-planter-after.png). ENV-05 and ENV-06 remain level 1.

## East-wing multi-elevation follow-up, 2026-11-04

The [UO cultural resource survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/oregonhall2025) describes a four-story east wing, three-story west wing, flat roofs, horizontal metal-frame windows and a south raised planter. Its photograph and site plan were consulted only, not redistributed or used as textures. The surveyed photograph primarily supports the south view; the east side applies the documented window vocabulary by inference, not from a verified east-elevation image.

This reversible overlay adds illustrative four-level window bands to the south and east sides of the inferred east wing, brick-colored backing panels and flat roof-edge trim. The matching panels are thick enough to cover generic placeholder windows and are not measured construction details. Existing west facade, raised-lawn layer, base mesh and vegetation remain. [South before](images/oregon-south-before-multiface.png) / [after](images/oregon-south-after-multiface.png), [east before](images/oregon-east-before-multiface.png) / [after](images/oregon-east-after-multiface.png), [north context](images/oregon-north-after-multiface.png).

Limited scene ray samples fit the existing east wall, and nearby ground-height samples range roughly from -0.55 to 0.10 m; this does not constitute a site survey. The named asset remains an approximate nearest-roof match. Exact wing boundaries, window counts and dimensions, east facade, roof, recessed entrances and balconies, raised-planter grading and present conditions need verification. The added panel strips are not included in collision, though conservative test rectangles did not intersect existing navigation graph edges with a 0.5 m margin. Existing planter and entrance accessibility are not verified. The original GLB, campus layout and interiors remain unchanged. The asset stays level 1; previous overlay: `assets/exterior-refinement/oregon-before-multiface-2026-11-04.json`.
