# 2017 post-expansion surface elevation screen

The Willamette River US Army Corps of Engineers highest-hit raster is available in the DOGAMI surface service as raster 500 with catalog name `WILLAMETTE_RIVER_BATHY_2017_USACE_HIGHEST_HIT_UTM10N_VERTICAL_FEET`. The query locks that raster before exporting the 2 km campus region. A point at the Straub classroom addition returned observations from 2017 and earlier layers, so using the raster ID matters for changes after 2015. [DOGAMI surface service](https://gis.dogami.oregon.gov/arcgis/rest/services/lidar/DIGITAL_SURFACE_MODEL_MOSAIC/ImageServer) · [2017 survey report](https://pubs.oregon.gov/dogami/ldq/reports/Willamette_River_Topobathymetric_LiDAR_Technical_Data_Report_SIGNED.pdf).

The export uses the project's EPSG:3740 bounds and a 1 m nearest-neighbor grid. Service heights are NAVD88 US survey feet, converted to metres before subtracting the scene origin elevation. Coverage is 3,152,673 of 4,000,000 cells. Of the 534 original scene footprints, 372 have a valid interior sample and 344 have coverage of at least 90%. Coverage uses points at least 1 m inside the footprint where the inset is nonempty. The surface captures trees and equipment and dates to 2017. Additional footprint and imagery checks are needed for newer construction and for samples near roof edges.

The TIFF, derived ENU grid, HTTP export URL, extent, source raster record, hashes and per-footprint counts are held in the research worktree under `exterior-l2/campus-surface-2017-20261124/`. The 2023 Autzen point-cloud clip was checked separately against its LAS extent. Its southern edge is about 509 m north of the scene origin, so it does not cover the Straub or Earl footprints. The 2017 screen made it possible to compare both new models with post-expansion roof heights. [Straub and Earl exterior review](straub-earl-exterior-review-2026-11-24.md).

A same-cell comparison of the two epochs retains 319 independent L1 exteriors with at least 20 paired cells and at least 60% paired coverage. Nine candidates have median differences of at least 2 m in magnitude. The 2024 aerial montage shows roof fragments, sites now occupied by parking or paths, and vegetation near some older mesh boundaries. For Kalapuya Ilihi, 90.01% of the old candidate lies in the mapped hall but it covers only 13.96% of the hall footprint. The 2017 roof surface is 6.95 m higher in the median of 356 paired cells over the interior of that candidate. These measurements prioritize further registration work. The comparison table, imagery hashes and mapped overlaps are in `remaining-exterior-changes.csv` and `remaining-exterior-aerial-screen.json` in the research directory.

The full Kalapuya Ilihi outline covers 2,946.721 m². The footprint screen isolated seven conflicts with edges 2510–2516. The 2024 aerial overlay shows why the historical road and replacement roof needed a coordinated review. The local model is described in the [Kalapuya exterior review](kalapuya-exterior-review-2026-11-24.md). The earlier evidence is in `exterior-l2/kalapuya-20261124/registration-audit.json` and `roof-and-stale-road.png` in the research worktree.
