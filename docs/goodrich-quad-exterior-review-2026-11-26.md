# Goodrich Hall and the Alder Street exteriors

The local viewer replaces two source meshes and adds a building omitted from the source campus. City of Eugene outlines 171531, 171532 and 171533 and the Alder address points register the sites. The existing Goodrich Hall and 1145 Alder meshes overlap their city outlines by 513.704 m² and 174.733 m². The 1125 Alder outline overlaps none of the 534 source building footprints. [City buildings](https://gis.eugene-or.gov/arcgis/rest/services/PWM/COE_Basemap_Tiled_Pro/MapServer/3) · [City address points](https://services3.arcgis.com/F7NiRLGNbA2hh7gE/ArcGIS/rest/services/EugAddress/FeatureServer/0) · [Source pins and editable dimensions](../data/metadata/goodrich-quad-exterior-review.json).

- **Goodrich Hall, 828 E 11th Ave**, retains its original scene ID. The north face has masonry courses, sash windows, pilasters, a fanlight and the diamond transom over the stepped entry. The west addition joins the historic hall and includes an open bell frame, four bells and the cross dedicated in August 2025. The 2026 campus aerial establishes the renovated silhouette. Tower height, links, obscured windows and threshold dimensions are approximate. [Bushnell dedication notice](https://bushnell.edu/news/bushnell-marks-130-years-with-grand-reopening-of-goodrich-hall-and-bell-tower-dedication-august-26/) · [Campus aerial](https://bushnell.edu/the-beacon-magazine/brief-spring-2026/) · [North entrance photograph](https://bushnell.edu/give/legacy/) · [July 2024 construction view](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=HdGyFYG2JNGQu8NRJ10UVg&heading=90).
- **Bushnell Facilities & Maintenance, 1125 Alder St**, has a separate scene ID. Its street elevation has white lap siding, a dark gable truss, paired upper windows and an open porch with two masonry supports. Porch depth, threshold and screened elevations are approximate. [Bushnell campus directory](https://bushnell.edu/student-life/campus-safety/) · [July 2024 street view](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=1sjDqsuBa-5u9O9fERrjFA&heading=100).
- **1145 Alder Building** keeps its former scene ID and uses an address-based label. The model restores the low stepped roof, red brick walls, tall chimney and lower west vent. The south access point and chimney height are inferred. The building's institutional name and use remain unverified.
