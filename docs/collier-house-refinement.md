# Collier House exterior

A reversible, source-pinned runtime replacement for `roof-e5accf83` adds an approximate two-storey light wood-sided house, low hipped roof, north porch, southwest addition, windows and small shrubs. The [UO cultural resource survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/collierhouse2025-0) describes the original as two-storey, wood-sided and hipped, and identifies the north porch and later southwest addition. Its historic photo and site plan were consulted on 2026-11-04, but are linked only, not redistributed or used as textures. The procedural reconstruction is original CC BY 4.0 geometry.

The original source GLB and building ID are retained; the runtime layer replaces only the matched semantic building. The inferred footprint, siding, roof proportions, porch, chimney positions and planting are simplified. Rear and addition windows are illustrative because the surveyed photograph does not show those faces. Placement, exact dimensions and present conditions require additional references. The original collision footprint is retained and conservatively supplemented as described below; navigation and accessibility remain approximate. No existing trees or interior assets were modified. ## Collision and routing

A geometry audit found approximately 114 square metres of the visual body, addition, porch and steps outside the old estimated polygon; roughly 6 square metres of the old polygon lie outside those simplified shapes. The original footprint is preserved, and the source-pinned replacement now supplies four conservative rectangular movement envelopes. They block the body, addition, porch and stairs rather than attempting to model walkable steps; users cannot walk onto this illustrative porch. This does not establish a real entrance or accessibility route.

The viewer checks these supplemental obstacles for player movement, route connectors and campus-life detours; route graph edges intersecting an expanded obstacle are excluded. The existing graph has zero edges intersecting the current boxes with a 0.5 m margin. The minimap and old footprint remain approximate, and the conservative union may block areas that are physically open. Collision boxes are bound to the exterior layer and validated before use; the unchanged base GLB and geographic manifest remain intact.

Validation: 59 JS tests, including movement across the previously unblocked porch and a synthetic route graph; local browser checks for the actual Collier collision, selection, building cards and navigation; production build. These are model-consistency checks, not a measured survey.
