# Kalapuya Ilihi Hall exterior review

The local default retains scene ID `roof-b36ba733` and gives it the registered UO 159 identity. The old candidate covered 411.36 m², or 13.96% of the campus-map outline. The rebuilt envelope covers 2,945.46 m² and models both residential wings, the open west court, the lower northeast commons, stepped roofs, brick façades and dark glazed joints. Three modeled approaches serve the north, west court and south doors. Visits use the shared classroom preview. [University map](https://map.uoregon.edu/) · [Mahlum design and exterior photos](https://www.mahlum.com/projects/kalapuya-ilihi-residence-hall/) · [University Housing](https://housing.uoregon.edu/kalapuya%20ilihi).

Compared 2,545 eligible 2017 roof cells. At the 334 locations with hits in both models, the median absolute height difference dropped from 8.882 m to 0.148 m. The new median across all eligible cells is 0.138 m. Nine paired render views cover the offset wings, upper window bays, low glazed commons and entrance canopy. The exterior reaches L2 after comparison with Knight Library. Canopy and doorstep dimensions, several rear openings and some rooftop equipment remain approximate. [2017 roof evidence](campus-surface-2017-2026-11-24.md) · [Knight Library example](knight-library-registration-2026-11-23.md).

## Road and navigation correction

The 2024 aerial shows the unnamed TIGER corridor `tiger-1104264146938-0-0-0` crossing the completed hall. The site retires all 12 graph edges on that corridor, including the seven intersecting the new footprint. Seventeen modeled path edges tie the entrances to East 17th Avenue. All 7,710 active edges clear the replacement footprint and canopy supports. The active minimap hides the obsolete road and the road mesh removes 123 source triangles. Eight reconstructed surface fragments retain 3.643 m² of the shared pavement on East 17th Avenue and Moss Alley. [OSIP 2024 imagery](https://imagery.oregonexplorer.info/arcgis/rest/services/OSIP_2024/OSIP_2024_SL/ImageServer).

The source pins cover the building package, compatible terrain and route graph. Only the intended slices were removed from the road and building index buffers. The surviving index sequences and vertex attributes match the baseline, including the adjacent Longhouse overlay. Failed model or graph verification restores the prior site, corridor and directions together. Browser directions from Global Scholars Hall reach the west court with no collision in the sampled path.
