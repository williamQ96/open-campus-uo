# Clinical Services Building roof study — 2026-11-04

The [UO 2006 historic resource survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/clinicalservice05_30_07.pdf) identifies Clinical Services at 901 East 18th Avenue and describes irregular broad sloping metal roofs and exposed concrete rafter ends. Its site plan places East 18th Avenue to the south; the photograph appears to show the street-side facade, but its exact camera direction and present-day condition are not confirmed. The image is linked only and not redistributed. The project locator falls within `roof-c1ff8b83`, whose historical LiDAR-derived footprint remains an approximation and can include canopy or omit wall details.

A reversible source-pinned operation inclines the existing roof and upper wall vertices as a single shallow plane, rising toward the west by an illustrative 3.5% gradient. It selects 2,218 of 3,968 source vertices by height and a source-matched rooftop-feature color and limits each vertical movement to 0.9 m. Horizontal coordinates, unselected lower vertices and their normals, vertex colors, triangle indices, ground, navigation and building selection remain unchanged. This keeps the source roof outline continuous and avoids the floating slabs observed in the discarded candidate. It is a stylized approximation: the survey describes irregular sloping surfaces, and neither their exact number, orientation, dimensions nor the rafter positions are reconstructed. [South before](images/clinical-study-south-before.png) / [after](images/clinical-study-south-final.png); [west before](images/clinical-study-west-before.png) / [after](images/clinical-study-west-final.png).

The human-editable `apps/web-preview/public/scene/clinical-roof-slope.json` controls the runtime patch. `src/building-roof-slope.js` validates the source hash, target identity, material, exact vertex and selection counts and displacement limit before changing a cloned mesh. `presentation.json` pins the parameter file, and the staged rebuild guard preserves it only for matching GLB and parameter hashes. The original Blender and standalone GLB remain untouched and do not contain the change; the runtime loader and parameter layer are required for this appearance. Previous loader and rebuild guard source are preserved. Original code is Apache-2.0 and parameter data CC BY 4.0.

The change is partial, not a verified exterior reconstruction or a level-2 claim. Roof pitch, footprint, exact eaves, exposed rafters, materials, facade openings, terrain contact, entrances and current conditions remain unverified; keep level 1. No building interior or tree assets were changed. Checks include actual-source vertex-by-vertex comparison, four views, selection in toon and PBR, the existing activity-disabled draw-call budget, unit tests, production build and source-pinned rebuild tests. No physical access or hardware performance claim is made.
