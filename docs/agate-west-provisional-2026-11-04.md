# Agate Hall west frontage — provisional local refinement, 2026-11-04

A source-pinned, 77-box west facade has been integrated on the existing unnamed `roof-b068940e` candidate: light wall panels, two levels of window bays and green awnings, and a simplified central parapet. The [UO Agate Hall survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/agatehall2025) identifies the west Agate Street frontage, two-story stucco form and awnings; the [2016 UO photograph](https://news.uoregon.edu/content/american-english-institute-makes-move-renovated-agate-hall) shows green awnings. The survey photograph shows red awnings at an earlier time. The facade, number and proportions of bays, parapet, color, entry and present conditions are interpretive, not measured. Reference imagery and third-party geometry are not bundled. The underlying roof and other elevations remain unchanged.

A third-party approximate locator (44.040144, -123.069258) falls near the source polygon; the [archived 2024 NAIP overview](../data/metadata/naip-2024-main-campus-reference.json) broadly matches its shape and relation to Agate Street. Neither is a registered footprint or current survey. The asset therefore remains labelled **unidentified** and level 1; no building identity change, invented department, or full-campus level claim is made. The modeled generic green entry is an existing estimated marker, not an accurate model of the 2016 entrance. Interior assets and campus layout are unchanged.

## Correcting the earlier review

The [prior candidate report](agate-west-candidate-2026-11-04.md) incorrectly stated the existing marker was invisible. Reinspection of the original before/after images found the 54 × 87 pixel entry crop was identical, including the green door. Instrumented ray checks also found the marker in front of the candidate. This was a visual-review error, not an established scene occlusion. The old JSON and screenshots remain isolated as history. The adopted second version fills the tan wall-color seam behind the marker with a matching cream panel; it does not alter the entry instance.

In the corrected candidate, center rays from the front, both oblique sides and a distant view hit the entrance before the wall; the closest front sample puts the visible door about 0.03 m in front of the overlay. The small depth separation can be sensitive to rendering precision outside the tested views. In a matched 1440 × 1000 front view, the 54 × 87 pixel entrance crop remained byte-for-byte identical, and both facade and entrance were clickable before and after. The 534 entry records were unchanged; the modeled approach and a point on its path stayed unblocked. Three thin facade collision rectangles attach to the original footprint; no modeled navigation graph edge intersects them at a 0.5 m margin. These are model tests, not field-clearance or accessibility certification.
