# Shared illustrative entrance portals — 2026-11-04, local only

All 534 inferred building entrance markers use the same shared component. The existing plain green panel and gold surround now also have a narrow center stile and paired handles on both faces. They are original, generic visual affordances, not photographs, a UO-specific door design or an assertion that the locations are real doors. Existing positions, normals, ray alignment, approach inference, selection, interaction, collision and interior scenes are unchanged. PROP-04 remains level 1. The [campus screenshot](images/portal-campus-2026-11-04.png) shows one unnamed estimated entry from a first-person view; it does not verify physical entrance location or accessibility.

The component uses the existing two instanced meshes (trim and door) rather than per-building objects. Five extra box parts add 60 triangles per instance, up to 32,040 triangles for 534 instances; the two draw objects are unchanged. The existing activity-disabled browser budget is checked at its defined camera and remains <180. A separate first-person screenshot at the unnamed entry measured 190 draw calls and 1,077,066 scene triangles with activity disabled; this view was not compared against a baseline and demonstrates that the budget check is not a global all-camera guarantee. No hardware benchmark is claimed.

The [standalone GLB](../assets/exterior-refinement/entrance-portal-2026-11-04.glb) contains one local-coordinate portal as two meshes, without placement, ray alignment, other building geometry or collision. Frame and door positions, normals, color and identity transforms match their runtime generators on reimport, with source SHA-256 embedded. The independent output is not used by the runtime and must not be loaded in addition to the runtime component at the same location. Regenerate from `apps/web-preview` with local Vite running: `node scripts/export-entrance.mjs ../../assets/exterior-refinement/<new-name>.glb`. The exporter refuses overwrite, checks that source bytes remain stable, and validates reimport. `src/entrances.js` remains the editable source; no `.blend` snapshot was made because Blender was unavailable. Original geometry is CC BY 4.0 and code Apache-2.0.

## Pacific Hall boundary context

The [2025 UO survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/pacifichall2025) describes Pacific Hall as a 3.5-story L-shaped poured-concrete building connected to Lawrence Hall, Columbia Hall and Onyx Bridge. The project `roof-4041562c` is a LiDAR-derived approximate-nearest-roof candidate and its irregular 1,284.5 m² footprint and 20.91 m model height are not a registered architectural plan. The complex is connected and the campus registry contains other disputed science-building matches. The source does not register the surveyed facade to this mesh; no facade, identity or level change was made without that link. No survey image is embedded.
