# Knight Campus information cards

The existing `context-oc-knight-1` and `context-oc-knight-2` entries remain approximate downstream envelopes. Their names, relative placement (Building 2 north of Building 1) and project locators are consistent with [UO's 2023 Building 2 project description](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2023-12/2023-capital-plan-booklet-rdcd-final.pdf) and the Knight Campus addresses. [Knight Campus](https://knightcampus.uoregon.edu/about) states that Building 1 opened in 2020 and Building 2 opened in 2026; its [homepage](https://knightcampus.uoregon.edu/) identifies bioengineering and biomedical data science research groups in Building 2. Checked 2026-11-03.

These are basic cards, not proof of exact envelope dimensions, survey boundaries, current complete occupancy or exterior maturity. Geometry and levels are unchanged.
