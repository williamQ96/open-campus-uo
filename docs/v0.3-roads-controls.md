# v0.3 — Basic roads, Follow zoom and links

The 2 × 2 km scene gained 140 clipped centerline segments: 132 roads and 8 walking/cycling paths. Original names and LINEARID values are preserved. Blender/GLB contain road and path layers; the minimap displays the same network.

Source: U.S. Census Bureau, [2025 TIGER/Line® Lane County All Roads](https://www2.census.gov/geo/tiger/TIGER2025/ROADS/tl_2025_41039_roads.zip). Section 1.2 of the [technical documentation](https://www2.census.gov/geo/pdfs/maps-data/data/tiger/tgrshp2025/TGRSHP2025_TechDoc.pdf) permits reproduction of government material and requests source acknowledgement. About includes that acknowledgement.

`scripts/build-campus-roads.py` verifies the ZIP SHA-256 and NAD83/EPSG:4269 projection, transforms/clips the lines and writes `data/metadata/campus-roads.geojson`. It uses upstream Python plus `pyshp==3.1.6`. The raw ZIP remains ignored.

Display widths are estimated by class, about 3–18 m. Upstream v0.3 unions junctions, removes overlapping surfaces/building coverage, clips the AOI, and tessellates against the original terrain triangles. A 4 cm offset avoids flicker. There are no surveyed curbs, lane markings, bridge decks or routing/access guarantees. Internal campus paths remain incomplete.

Follow wheel zoom spans 2.5–30 m with camera collision taking priority. View changes preserve follow distance; reset restores 7.5 m. About links to the application and upstream repositories; their access may differ.

A 360 × 360 m, seven-segment smoke rebuild passed before advancing the reconstruction pin, followed by a full nine-tile rebuild. Eleven upstream, six movement/jump and five browser tests passed. [Road audit](../data/metadata/roads-audit.json) records source/model hashes and terrain-drape checks. The earlier main-campus audit preserves v0.2 generation evidence.
