# Peterson Hall south frontage

A source-pinned detail layer adds a simplified south-facing brick frontage, entry and three illustrative shrubs without replacing the original business-complex meshes. The [UO Peterson survey and site plan](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/petersonhall2025) identifies its 13th Avenue facade and describes Peterson and Anstett (formerly Gilbert) as paired entry pylons. The layer reuses the existing original Anstett frontage geometry and its [attributed CC BY 4.0 Anstett photo reference](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Anstett_Hall,_University_of_Oregon,_Eugene_-_DPLA_-_7f05aff2c79e236edd0bed0104b6f145.jpg); **that photograph depicts Anstett, not Peterson**. The archived 2024 public-domain NAIP image provides approximate campus placement. UO's survey is linked only; no image is used as a runtime texture.

This is a deliberately simplified approximation, not a surveyed copy: the underlying generic building and its junction with the new frontage remain visible, and dimensions, materials, entry, vegetation and current conditions need further confirmation. No collision, accessibility or route claim is made. The building remains level 1, and interiors are untouched. [Before](images/peterson-south-before.png) / [after](images/peterson-south-after.png).

The building card notes Oregon MBA using the [UO Lillis Business Complex page](https://business.uoregon.edu/about/lillis-complex); it is not a complete tenant list.

## Seam trial (not integrated)

A filled south-wing volume produced roof overlap and visible intersection lines; a subsequent pair of side-wall planes protruded beyond the existing silhouette. Both candidates were rejected and the prior source-pinned layer restored. [Rejected side-wall review](images/peterson-seam-trial.png). A reliable correction requires reconciling the original merged footprint with independently verified south-wing boundaries; the seam remains.
