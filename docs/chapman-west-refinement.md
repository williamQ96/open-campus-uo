# Chapman Hall west entrance and planting

A reversible source-pinned overlay adds a west entrance, three upper arched windows, side windows, stairs and schematic shrubs to the existing `roof-f5cea51d` model. The [UO cultural resource survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/chapmanhall2025) and [historic assessment](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/chapmanhall_historicassessment_2016-01-20.pdf) informed the architecture and identify the west entrance facing the Memorial Quad. Historic images were consulted on 2026-11-04, but are linked only, not redistributed or used as textures. Original procedural geometry is CC BY 4.0.

The original roof footprint is LiDAR-derived and approximate. The overlay uses a thickened front panel to cover original window geometry; its wall thickness is an implementation approximation, not a construction detail. Exact proportions, openings, stair dimensions, plant species and present conditions remain unverified. Other elevations remain generic; trees obscure parts of the entrance in wide campus views. Steps and shrubs were placed against the estimated terrain; no route, collision or accessibility correctness is claimed. [Before](images/chapman-west-before.png) / [after](images/chapman-west-after.png). The original GLB, tree placements and interiors are untouched. The building and shared environment entries remain level 1.

## Multi-elevation follow-up, 2026-11-04

The [UO historic assessment](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/chapmanhall_historicassessment_2016-01-20.pdf), PDF pages 29–36, documents north and south facades, historic and altered entries, terracotta cornices and east egress. It is consulted only, not redistributed or used as a texture; local PDF SHA-256 `dbfa3289ee67b7e5ed5a611931fc306cccf38e04ba01d59e97cdba8328e005c1`. The original 1938 drawings are historical evidence, not proof that their entrance configuration remains current.

The overlay adds schematic north and south roof-edge cornices and repeated terracotta motifs, four illustrative north sash windows and additional pane divisions on existing north/south windows. Local scene ray samples locate the present approximate wall surfaces, and window bars are aligned with the existing glass mesh. The source's flat roof, west entrance, building mesh and landscape remain. [North before](images/chapman-north-before-multiface.png), [north after](images/chapman-north-after-multiface.png), [south after](images/chapman-south-after-multiface.png), [east context](images/chapman-east-after-multiface.png).

Window dimensions, exact cornice detailing, the historical-versus-current openings, north and south entrances, terrain junctions and vegetation relationships remain approximations; south courtyard and accessible route are not reconstructed. New details sit above ground; they do not change the existing collision footprint or navigation graph. Existing west steps have not been verified for collision or accessibility, and visual/ray samples are not a full site clearance survey. The asset stays level 1, the original GLB and interiors are unchanged. The prior overlay is preserved at `assets/exterior-refinement/chapman-before-multiface-2026-11-04.json`.
