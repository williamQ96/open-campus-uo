# Chapman Hall west entrance and planting

A reversible source-pinned overlay adds a west entrance, three upper arched windows, side windows, stairs and schematic shrubs to the existing `roof-f5cea51d` model. The [UO cultural resource survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/chapmanhall2025) and [historic assessment](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/chapmanhall_historicassessment_2016-01-20.pdf) informed the architecture and identify the west entrance facing the Memorial Quad. Historic images were consulted on 2026-11-04, but are linked only, not redistributed or used as textures. Original procedural geometry is CC BY 4.0.

The original roof footprint is LiDAR-derived and approximate. The overlay uses a thickened front panel to cover original window geometry; its wall thickness is an implementation approximation, not a construction detail. Exact proportions, openings, stair dimensions, plant species and present conditions remain unverified. Other elevations remain generic; trees obscure parts of the entrance in wide campus views. Steps and shrubs were placed against the estimated terrain; no route, collision or accessibility correctness is claimed. [Before](images/chapman-west-before.png) / [after](images/chapman-west-after.png). The original GLB, tree placements and interiors are untouched. The building and shared environment entries remain level 1.

## West shallow arch frieze, 2026-11-10

The [UO 2025 survey, page 4](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-01/chapmanhall_2025.pdf) supports a repeated pale shallow arch treatment beneath the west cornice. It is link-only reference, not an archived image or texture. A low-resolution attribution-only photo corroborates the feature but was not admitted to the repository's stricter license whitelist; a focused alternative search excluded Wisconsin-Milwaukee and Mount Union namesakes. No license label or CI rule changed. Details and source checks remain in the [candidate audit](refinement-candidate-audit-2026-11-10-e-chapman.md).

Twelve original simplified arch reliefs now sit on a continuous same-stone backing beneath the existing 10 m west entry cornice. Their 0.75 m rhythm and 0.04 m exposed relief are stylized choices, not measured count, depth or carved detail. The original 144 objects remain identical; 121 primitives are added to the existing merged building mesh. All original windows, roof, stairs, landscape, entrance and collision arrangements remain. The treatment stops at this projection; it does not add the photographed heraldic crest or extend a guessed pattern to other elevations. Level remains 1.

The first primitive candidate failed the existing Euler-range validator. Equivalent angles were normalized into the permitted range without changing the generator or weakening validation. The next candidate passed browser geometry checks but was **visually rejected** because isolated arch outlines resembled horseshoes. A continuous pale backing reduced exposed depth to 4 cm and made the treatment read as a shallow frieze. Its initial close camera cropped the feature and was corrected upward; a tree-obscured oblique camera was moved forward without moving vegetation. A passing automated run was not treated as visual approval. The exact pre-change [source snapshot](../assets/exterior-refinement/chapman-before-west-frieze-2026-11-10.json) is preserved; external review records retain the rejected-run result and reasons.
