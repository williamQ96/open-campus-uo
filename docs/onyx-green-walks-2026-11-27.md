# Onyx Green surface walks and planting islands

The university identifies Onyx Green as the north–south pedestrian connection between East 13th and Franklin Boulevard and places Science Walk along the plaza’s southern edge. The museum guide follows the lower Cascade route past the Dawn redwood and east past the Science Commons toward Willamette Hall. The 2026 campus access map and four dated Lane County orthophotos fit the southern bend, the exposed west spine, the east-side passage and the library’s south approach. The west spine continues to the open plaza south of Onyx Bridge.
