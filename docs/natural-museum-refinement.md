# Museum of Natural and Cultural History exterior study, 2026-11-04

The [UO museum homepage](https://natural-history.uoregon.edu/) shows timber elements and multiple pitched roofs; the [2012 UO site report](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/mnch3reassessreport5-18-12rev1_2.pdf) places the public entrance at the west courtyard (PDF page 14). The report contains proposed future work as well as existing-site diagrams; only its labeled existing plan was used. The photo is linked only, not redistributed or used as a texture. Original schematic geometry and color parameters are CC BY 4.0; no indigenous artworks, sculptures, signage or logos were reproduced.

`roof-e11ec58d` has an approximate curated locator inside a LiDAR-derived outline which can include canopy or omit courtyard structure. A source-pinned color patch changes 128 matching vertical wall vertices to a timber-like tone, leaving the original GLB and roof unchanged. A reversible detail adds illustrative timber posts and glazing on two west segments, three west-facing pitched forms, and a limited south-wall treatment extrapolated from the photo. The source flat roof remains visible behind these forms: this is not a complete roof reconstruction.

Exact roof count, pitch, placement, wall construction, current facade, entrance, accessible approach, courtyard, plantings and east/north elevations remain unverified. The colored panel and windows are schematic and do not create an interactive door. No terrain, trees, sculpture, original GLB, navigation data or interior changed. Detail adds no separate collision. Conservative ENU rectangles around modeled west and south faces were screened against route graph edges with 0.5 m margin and yielded zero intersections; this does not establish physical clearance or accessibility. Keep level 1.

The source-pinned layer is merged into its unique compatible source building mesh after checking identity, vertex count, bounds, transforms and material; the source material and selection identity are retained. This avoids an additional draw call while slightly reducing separate culling within the building. Rebuild preservation checks the independent layer and wall-patch hashes against the source asset.
