# Matthew Knight Arena north facade — local partial refinement, 2026-11-04

The [UO architecture guide](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/matthew-knight-arena) identifies the 2011 arena at 1776 East 13th Avenue. Its [architect](https://tvaarchitects.com/project/matthew-knight-arena/) describes an exterior with transparent materials and a plaza shared with Ford Alumni Center; the [contractor](https://www.hoffmancorp.com/project/matthew-knight-arena/) describes extensive glass curtainwall. A [campus-plan photograph and map](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/campus_plan_2021__0.pdf) show the curved silver and glass exterior. The materials were consulted for broad character, not copied or redistributed.

A source-pinned illustrative layer adds a regular glass-and-metal curtain wall and light upper band across the north side of the existing `context-b0074` placeholder. The underlying source is a 125 by 110 m rectangular envelope, not a reconstruction of the arena's curved form. Actual wall and roof geometry, glass divisions, entrances, plaza, orientation relative to a registered survey and other elevations remain unverified. Glass is opaque stylized material; no interior was modeled. The stadium remains level 1. The existing generic estimated entrance portal remains visible and its computed position, approach and walkability match the baseline, but it does not verify a real entry.

The overlay merges into the 2,168-vertex source mesh, retaining original asset identity and scene layout. Six thin supplemental collision strips border the new north wall; no modeled route edge intersects them with a 0.5 m margin. This does not establish real-world access or clearance. In the matched software-rendered, activity-disabled [before](images/arena-north-before-2026-11-04.png) and [after](images/arena-north-after-v3-2026-11-04.png) first-person views, draw calls stayed at 23 and triangles rose from 629,068 to 630,496. This is one view, not a hardware frame-time test; existing performance limits remain unchanged.

The editable runtime source is `apps/web-preview/public/scene/arena-north-detail.json`, pinned to the existing GLB and referenced by `presentation.json`. The [standalone GLB](../assets/exterior-refinement/arena-north-detail-v3-2026-11-04.glb) contains only the new wall; it omits the base building, entrance portal, collision, roof and other scene objects. Its 4,284 positions, normals and colors matched the generated runtime overlay exactly after export and reimport. No Blender snapshot or whole-building baked GLB was produced. Original overlay geometry and parameters are CC BY 4.0; linked photos retain their own rights. The work remains local and unpublished.

Yasui Hall was also screened. Its [architect](https://rowellbrokaw.com/uo-hamilton-walton-phase-2) describes green and yellow south-facing window trim, but the approximate Yasui envelope overlaps the Cedar envelope and unnamed `roof-1fde9ce4`. Several scene rays find an unnamed foreground mesh at ground height; sampled points along much of the area in front are blocked by existing collision. This is an unresolved project-model relationship, not evidence of a real obstruction. No Yasui geometry or collision was modified; a registered building boundary and ground-level navigation investigation are needed before integrating that facade.
