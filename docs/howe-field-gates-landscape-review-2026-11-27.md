# Howe Field gates and entrance landscape

This site models the restored entrance fittings north of Jane Sanders Stadium's main plaza. The 2014 campus survey documents the concrete wall, ticket opening, memorial plaque and wrought iron. The 2015 design review specifies that the historic leaves would be fixed in place. The 2018 photograph from inside the plaza and the July 2024 University Street view show the three wall portions and the open bays as built. The model includes the two photographed side leaves with decorative iron frames, the grilled opening and shelf, and a bronze tablet on the ticket wall. Wall thicknesses, tablet placement and the iron pattern are estimates. [Historic resource survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/howefieldandgates_2014.pdf) · [Campus Planning Committee annual report](https://scholarsbank.uoregon.edu/server/api/core/bitstreams/322bcb47-9838-4920-9269-1358ff8a17e7/content) · [2018 photograph by Grand'mere Eugene, CC BY-SA 4.0](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Howe_Field_Memorial_Gates,_Jane_Sander_sStadium.jpg) · [July 2024 street view](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=JOWfO6cWDDwVD2oYsGRJog&heading=101.74).

Three ortho dates constrain the two diagonals, the broad plaza and its parking apron. The model adds about 732 m² of concrete paving, 161 m² of asphalt, five representative access-aisle hatches and six planting pockets. It also models the oval Filtration Planter 1 with its concrete rim and representative low plantings. The university's stormwater report identifies that planter in front of the canopy. Grade, plants and markings are estimates. The stadium canopy, seating and playing field are tracked separately. [Lane County orthophotos](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [Stormwater report, PDF pp. 13–14](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/sap2019_stormwater.pdf).

Two exports matched byte for byte. The default desktop and phone previews loaded all three site meshes and all 610 existing exterior IDs. They detected the walls and planter as solids, confirmed both wall openings and the plaza are clear, and returned a 49.36 m modeled route from University Street into the plaza. The five added route edges connect to the existing road graph. Paired views checked the foreground pavement, visible ironwork and the exposed planter soil. The landscape is local on top of `63263a9`.
