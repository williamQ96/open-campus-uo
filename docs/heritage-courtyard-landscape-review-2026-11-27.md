# University Hall and Villard Hall courtyard landscape

The Heritage Project plan and the 2026 reopening photographs locate the paths around University Hall, the courtyard between the two halls and the approach to Villard's south door. The plan supplies the relative alignment of walks, beds and service parking. The completed Villard photograph confirms the five treads, two stair rails, side ramp, planted beds and furnishings. The University Hall photograph confirms the green identification sign and east approach planting. Lane County's 2017 and 2021 aerials register the pre-renovation building and ground edges. Its 2025 aerial shows construction. [Heritage Project site plan, PDF pp. 7 and 17–18](https://www.oregon.gov/biz/aboutus/artscommission/Documents/RFQ_UO_Heritage_2024.pdf) · [Villard south courtyard](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2026-08/img_3020.JPEG) · [University Hall east approach](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2026-08/img_3015.JPEG) · [UO reopening report](https://news.uoregon.edu/content/historic-halls-reopen-modern-amenities-and-more) · [Lane County aerial imagery](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer).

The model extends Hello Walk from the bend south of University Hall along both sides of the hall and through the courtyard, with branches to Villard's east approach and south ramp. It adds 729.81 m² of concrete surfaces, 269.59 m² of marked asphalt and 144.77 m² in eight planted beds. Four representative young trees, three benches, two lamps and the University Hall sign accompany the arched south entry, stair and railings. Two approximate background-tree placements intersecting the new paving are suppressed in the bounds of this site. Widths, grades, planting species and furnishing dimensions are estimates from the sources and modeled terrain.

The 2025 Oregon Arts Commission materials identify the exterior art commission. Installation geometry and permission to reproduce the work remain to be confirmed. [Public art commission materials](https://www.oregon.gov/biz/aboutus/artscommission/resources/commission_meetings/Documents/CommissionMeeting_FY26_July_18_2025_MeetingMaterials.pdf).

Four registered path branches add 30 edges to the navigation graph. Desktop and phone production previews returned 189.36 m from the E 13th anchor to the south stairs, 184.07 m to the ramp foot and 217.86 m to Villard's east approach. The previews checked clear passage on both sides of University Hall, the stair's five rising levels and collision at the rails and sign. Both loaded geometry for all 610 active exterior IDs. The stair and ramp are terrain-fitted model geometry. Their real-world slopes and clearances require field measurements.
