# McArthur Court and Clinical Services source audit

## McArthur Court

The [UO survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-01/mcarthur-court_2025.pdf) identifies a vaulted roof and depicts the connection to the neighboring complex. The project currently uses an approximate rectangular placeholder `context-b0020`; its estimated footprint intersects `roof-a5ff3d5a` (Esslinger Hall) by about 3,230 square metres out of the placeholder's roughly 4,030 square metres. This is a planar comparison of approximate project polygons, not a measured building boundary. The [existing view](images/mcarthur-boundary-existing.png) shows overlapping masses. No roof or facade was added: identify the shared boundary and the respective semantic meshes against further evidence first.

## Clinical Services

The [UO 2006 survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/clinicalservice05_30_07.pdf) describes broad irregular sloping roofs and shows the complex site plan. A staged partial roof wedge was reviewed against the existing model, but appeared disconnected from the flat coarse roof and its exact slope, orientation and extent could not be verified. The candidate was rejected and is not included in the campus. [Existing](images/clinical-roof-existing.png) / [rejected candidate](images/clinical-roof-rejected.png). Establish multiple roof views or more precise massing before another attempt. Survey images are linked only and were not redistributed.

Both entries remain level 1; no building geometry, existing vegetation or interiors were changed by this audit.
