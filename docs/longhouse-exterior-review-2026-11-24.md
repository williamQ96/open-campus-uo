# Many Nations Longhouse exterior review

The local default registers Many Nations Longhouse as UO 167 on the existing scene ID `roof-f736712f`. The old mesh had a gable and a provisional south window. The new 264.09 m² wall envelope gets a single north-falling roof, a shallow overhang, a living roof and its raised hood, vertical timber boards, and the large south window with heavier posts and a short upper row. The east entrance has a modeled door and paved landing. The north and west doors are represented on the exterior. [University history](https://longhouse.uoregon.edu/history-many-nations-longhouse) · [University exterior photo](https://news.uoregon.edu/content/geography-conference-set-many-nations-longhouse) · [University map](https://map.uoregon.edu/).

The UO map outlines the roof, while the first-floor map indicates roughly 270 m² of original floor area and the south glazing's projection. The mapped overlay sits east of the main roof visible in 2024 imagery. The reconstruction shifts the mapped wall diagram approximately 2.15 m west and 0.50 m north to align the walls, the roof and the under-roof platform. The resulting positions, especially the partly covered west eave, are approximate. The 2011 plan's descriptions and historical image document the original building and living roof. Its proposed east and north additions were outside the modeled extent in the 2024 aerial. [2011 conceptual plan, pp. 4–5, 11, 21](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/mnlh_conceptualplanreport02-07-2011_1.pdf) · [OSIP 2024 imagery](https://imagery.oregonexplorer.info/arcgis/rest/services/OSIP_2024/OSIP_2024_SL/ImageServer).

A checkerboard split of the 2017 raster reserved 124 cells for testing. At 123 matched roof hits, the median absolute height difference fell from 1.520 m to 0.151 m. The new roof hits all 124 cells, with a 0.150 m median. Eight paired render views cover the south, east, west and roof. All 7,710 active route edges clear the new envelope by at least 2.81 m. Retained positions, normals and colors of the shared batch match the prior version triangle for triangle, including with the neighboring Kalapuya replacement in place. [2017 surface evidence](campus-surface-2017-2026-11-24.md).

Compared with [Knight Library](knight-library-registration-2026-11-23.md), the complete exterior is recorded at L2 for its roof profile, frontage, material divisions and entry. Roof cover and board colors, roof hood construction, west and north door dimensions and the simulated threshold remain approximate. The east door opens the existing shared interior preview. Sources and images remain in the research worktree under `exterior-l2/east-campus-20261124/`. Only original geometry ships in the project GLB.

The editable [site parameters](../data/metadata/longhouse-site-review.json) and [generator](../scripts/build-longhouse-site-review.py) reproduce the GLB and descriptor byte for byte. Run `python scripts/build-longhouse-site-review.py --audit <audit.json>` from the project root.
