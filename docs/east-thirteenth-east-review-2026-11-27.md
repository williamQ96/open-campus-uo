# East 13th Avenue from University Street to Columbia Street

The central package joins this segment at the same pavement edge immediately east of University Street. Three Lane County orthophotos register the existing road from the EMU turnaround through Willamette, Huestis and Agate. The eastward extension reaches the Columbia Street approach. Summer 2024 street views check the EMU shelters, curb faces, bicycle racks and Agate crossing layout. The model covers 6,595.42 m² of road and 4,274.55 m² of sidewalks, approaches and ramps. Path edges under canopies and curb elevations use estimates. [County orthophotos](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [UO 13th Avenue project](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/13th-avenue-axis-conceptual-design).

The 261.83 m² EMU turnaround uses the visible circular apron, its contrasting inner ring and red outer curb segments. Two photographed gabled bicycle shelters face 13th Avenue. The east segment also represents the shelter near the health center, three rows of open bicycle racks, eleven low planted beds and eight registered trees. The third shelter profile, post dimensions, rack quantities, planting mix and crown extents are approximate. [UO corridor study](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/uo13_final_report.pdf) · [Campus tree inventory](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/trees) · [Editable aerial and street sources](../data/metadata/east-thirteenth-east.json).

The model includes the zebra crossings at the EMU, Willamette and the south access near the health center. It adds the Agate and Columbia approach lines, the south drive crossing, lane markings and STOP lettering on the asphalt. Eight graph branches add 97 edges. The south sidewalk joins the two previously registered Duck Dunes approaches. The west feeder ends outside the court fence, and the east feeder reaches the existing open gate on the support-building route. September 2026 completion photographs establish the court-side setting. Fine dimensions of the adjoining street verge and planting are estimated. [Duck Dunes opening and as-built photographs](https://rowellbrokaw.com/blog/2026/9/23/uo-first-serve-event) · [Great Lawn update](https://news.uoregon.edu/content/get-update-construction-activity-around-university-0) · [Duck Dunes and Great Lawn model](hamilton-great-lawn-duck-dunes-2026-11-26.md).

The Columbia approach uses the pinned Ford/Arena grade patch and L2 foundation, matching the visible runtime terrain. Both independent exports are byte-identical. Six paired views cover the turnaround, bicycle shelters, Willamette, Agate, Duck Dunes and Columbia. Production desktop and phone viewports each loaded triangle geometry for all 610 active exterior IDs. Both returned the same 918.72 m Kincaid–Columbia route and 13.39 m through the Duck Dunes east gate. Nine pavement samples stayed above ground and clear, and seven planter, shelter and bollard samples registered as solid. [GitHub Pages deployment history](https://github.com/williamQ96/open-campus-uo/actions/workflows/pages.yml).

The south leg of the Agate junction continues into the Agate Street L2 model. [Agate Street review](agate-thirteenth-fifteenth-review-2026-11-27.md).
