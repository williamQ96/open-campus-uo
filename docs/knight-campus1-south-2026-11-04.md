# Knight Campus Building 1: partial south facade — 2026-11-04

[Ennead Architects](https://ennead.com/work/uo-knight/) describes two L-shaped volumes around a courtyard and a southern skin of folded glass. Its images were consulted only; their copyrights are separate, and no photographs or third-party geometry are bundled. Original schematic detail is CC BY 4.0.

The project represents `context-oc-knight-1` as a 95 × 50 m approximate rectangular envelope, not a registered footprint. This approximation does not reproduce the two real L-shaped volumes, courtyard, bridge or accurate elevations. The 2D manifest also reports an overlap with unnamed `roof-a79ff330`; the overlay deliberately stops at model x=24 m, while that neighboring footprint begins at x=28 m. Neither object was moved, suppressed or renamed. That model-coordinate separation is not an on-site clearance survey.

A reversible source-hash-pinned layer adds a schematic alternating glass and triangular-line pattern over approximately 61 m of the remaining south face. It is a stylized interpretation of the documented folded glass, not measured panel geometry or a transparent reconstruction. The final 34 m near the conflict, the other elevations, roof, source GLB, ground, trees, bridge and interiors remain unchanged. Exact boundaries, architectural folds, entrance, plantings and present conditions remain unverified; keep level 1.

Three thin collision strips cover the modeled detail, and no current route-graph segment intersects them with a 0.5 m margin. This does not establish real pedestrian clearance or accessibility. The 78 detail boxes merge into the existing selected building only when the exact 2,120-vertex source, identity, transform and material contracts match. A browser test confirms original non-indexed positions, colors and normals are unchanged and the selected building identity is preserved. The original GLB is not edited.
