# Miller Theatre Complex western study - 2026-11-04

The [UO Robinson Theatre historic survey updated 2025](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/robinsontheatre2025) describes a concrete, flat-roofed theatre attached west of Villard Hall, expanded in 2009 by the Miller Theatre Complex. Its site plan and photograph support a west-facing glazed frontage; its tall fly tower is not represented accurately in the existing source. [Hacker Architects](https://www.hackerarchitects.com/cultural/miller-theatre-complex) describe a two-story glass lobby and show pale exterior panels. These images are linked for reference only; no photographs or third-party geometry are redistributed.

The `roof-944fcd46` source identity uses an anchor within its LiDAR footprint but the footprint remains approximate and may combine or omit parts. A source-matched color patch changes 128 vertical wall vertices to an illustrative pale concrete shade. A reversible 13-box source-pinned western study adds a schematic paneled two-storey glass frontage and mullions, masking generic windows within the frontage; it is merged into the compatible 3,364-vertex source building mesh without an extra draw object.
