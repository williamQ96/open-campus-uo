# Science complex and Global Scholars screening — 2026-11-04

The [UO Huestis survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/huestishall2025) describes a three-story rectangular building, an expressed concrete frame, recessed windows, flat roof and brick; it places Willamette beside the Science Walk to its west and Streisinger connected on the north. It also records a 2023 renovation and west elevator addition. The survey was consulted as reference; no third-party image or geometry was bundled. Its plan is not registered to the project's coordinate frame, and the actual present footprint and elevation details were not measured.

The scene identifies Huestis `context-b0040` by an approximate missing-roof envelope. Its 1,344 m² rectangle overlaps the existing `roof-34f891de` Willamette candidate by 746.09 m² (55.51% of Huestis). The same candidate overlaps Klamath by 1,556.33 m² and Streisinger by 599.25 m². These numbers describe overlap of *project 2D footprints*, not actual buildings or verified mesh intersections. The Willamette candidate is an irregular LiDAR-derived roof mask that may include canopy or merged structures; both Klamath and Streisinger are approximate envelopes. These are reasons to defer a site-specific exterior overlay and investigate registered footprints, not proof of which candidate is wrong. The Huestis and Willamette scene, collisions and grades were not changed.

For Global Scholars Hall, `context-b0158` is a 5,100 m² rectangular approximate context envelope. [UO's building guide](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/global-scholars-hall) and [UO Housing](https://housing.uoregon.edu/global%20scholars) identify the building; a [UO article](https://news.uoregon.edu/oq/thinking-about-space) describes two enclosed courtyards. An exterior photo on [UO's student-success site](https://uess.uoregon.edu/uo-neuroscience-major-named-rhodes-scholarship-finalist) was reviewed for broad visual character; image rights are not granted for redistribution, and the photo is not bundled. The solid rectangular placeholder cannot represent those courtyard volumes, and the photograph does not register facade segments to the model. No full-building facade was attached, since it would make the incorrect massing more persuasive. The building remains level 1. A registered plan or other spatially anchored footprint and multi-view references are needed before correction.
