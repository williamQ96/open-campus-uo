# East 15th Avenue and rebuilt Powell Plaza

The route follows East 15th from University Street and Straub Hall Green through Onyx and the Student Recreation Center frontage to Powell Plaza and Agate. The campus plan distinguishes the western street from the pedestrian and authorized-vehicle zone east of Onyx. Three dated County orthophotos track the reconstruction. The current venue page, campus museum guide and photographed landscape project establish the post-2020 plaza, the planted islands, gray paving, yellow-ended benches, three main flagpoles and dark gate panels. The western connection includes the traditional asphalt street, both walks and the paired-line crossings near the Straub approach and Onyx. From Onyx east, staggered planted islands divide the wider paver promenade. The paving continues to the existing stadium approach, with three tall flagpoles, a low curved base, information boards and dark entrance fencing. The replacement paving meets the existing plaza near the stadium shell, the Field 1 and SRC approaches, and the Agate crossings.
