# Erb Memorial Union and EMU Green exterior review

The local default now loads the unified Erb Memorial Union, circular lawn and five modeled approaches. `/?exterior-review=emu-baseline` restores the historical comparison in the development viewer. The building exterior is recorded at L2 after the November 24 review. EMU Green is part of a broader campus landscape group that remains at L1.

## Evidence and reconstruction

- The 2024 Oregon image shows that the named model and the unnamed southwestern model `roof-39f81f68` cover parts of a single continuous building. The named model covers 31.75% of campus outline 033, the two together cover 63.95%, and the authored exterior covers 99.59%. Footprint intersection-over-union rises from 0.5760 for the old union to 0.9937. The map and aerial guided the placement. [UO campus map](https://map.uoregon.edu/), [2024 Oregon orthophoto](https://imagery.oregonexplorer.info/arcgis/rest/services/OSIP_2024/OSIP_2024_SL/ImageServer).
- The 2016 project replaced the former eastern addition. SERA's completed-project photographs show the brick north elevation and large glazed bay, and the alternating facade panels and projecting stone entrance at the Hearth. These guided the original modeled facade elements. The dated UO roof plans and elevations were consulted as design references. The southwest ballroom and connector have retained historical first-return height bands. The new volumes above the north and east wings and the exact threshold elevations remain approximate. [UO project history](https://emu.uoregon.edu/index.php/about/history), [SERA completed project](https://www.seradesign.com/projects/university-of-oregon-erb-memorial-union/), [UO 2014 design archive](https://pages.uoregon.edu/moxoby/EMU/EMUdocs.html), [NOAA source](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/57407).
- The model includes separate roof heights for the ballroom, tower, Fishbowl, Craft Center and northern addition, with simple solar panel rows, a mechanical screen and two skylight accents. The indoor portal still uses the project's illustrative shared classroom. The west-side entry and terrace details need a further exterior check.
- In the earlier model, building geometry projected over about 746 m² of the aerial-traced green. The new exterior has zero plan overlap with the lawn and its ring. The lawn and pavement follow the existing heightfield with a constant 0.12 m render offset to clear the prior grass and paving layers. Walking height remains tied to the original ground. The new preview also revealed a remaining procedural shrub paired with previously suppressed tree 290. That shrub was removed through the already approved tree span recipe, bringing the paired shrub count to three. [Original EMU Green planting audit](pilot-tree-clearance-2026-11-23.md), [campus plan](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2023-12/principle-12-design-area-special-conditions_2022_23_.pdf).

## Collision and identity

The viewer validates its source and geometry hashes before removing both old source nodes. The southwestern mesh resolves as part of EMU and uses the unified collision outline. Its source IDs remain in the original manifest and inventory with a note on the 033 relationship. The corrupt-package fallback and development baseline use the previous two records.

The 1,222 perimeter samples found at least 0.332 m of footing cover. No route edge gained a footprint conflict at 0.42 m in the navigation screen. Browser raycasts and collision checks exercised both the open lawn and the retained ballroom. Both modeled entrances have walkable approaches. The Hearth portal was exercised through the shared classroom and back to the same exterior review. Four matched viewpoints and a PBR close view capture the result.

Edit [the model dimensions](../data/metadata/emu-green-site-review.json) and run `python scripts/build-emu-green-review.py <audit.json>` to regenerate the authored GLB and [runtime descriptor](../apps/web-preview/public/scene/emu-green-review/site.json). The source imagery and drawings are research references held outside the repository at `D:/github/opencampus-interior-research-20261116/exterior-l2/emu-green-20261123/`. The authored GLB has no reference textures. Run `render.mjs` from that folder with the development viewer active to repeat the browser comparisons.

## Default adoption and exterior review, 2026-11-24

The west entrance work combines the 2024 campus door announcement with the current visitor plan and the 2014 architectural sheets A051, A310 and A311. The announcement image and the orthophoto register with 52 matched points and a 0.19 m median residual. The lower west doorway uses the 440 ft design level. The Fishbowl and historic entry use the 453 ft design level. The north wing roof follows the 487 ft drawing elevation after transferring the datum to the local terrain. The separate mechanical screen and the reconstructed stairs are approximate. [Campus door notice and photo](https://blogs.uoregon.edu/cpfmnotifications/2024/12/), [2026 public visitor plan](https://urds.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2026-04/2026-unergraduate-research-symposium-program.pdf), [architectural design archive](https://pages.uoregon.edu/moxoby/EMU/EMUdocs.html).

The Fishbowl curve now follows the imagery-aligned control points with individual glass bays, vertical mullions, a low brick base and a narrow canopy. The historic entrance has trim, a shallow canopy and a modeled approach. These parts were compared with archived photos. The north glazed bay, east Hearth, south wing and prominent roof steps were compared with the completed-project photos and drawings. This combination of silhouette, identifiable façades, editable assets, and checked scene placement supports L2 for this exterior against the existing Knight Library example. Window counts and secondary back-of-house fittings remain simplified, and the two drawing elevations at the west doors remain estimates in the scene. [UO historic assessment, PDF pp. 17 and 32](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/emu_historicassessment_2016-01-20.pdf#page=17), [SERA completed project](https://www.seradesign.com/projects/university-of-oregon-erb-memorial-union/).

The default exterior covers 99.66% of the UO outline with 99.44% intersection-over-union. The circular lawn has zero plan overlap with the building. Screening found zero new conflicts on 7,705 route edges, and 1,223 perimeter terrain samples cover the foundation by at least 0.332 m. The latest planting fits the southeast elm and birch to campus records 6354 and 6370 and removes one inaccurate stem from the lawn edge. An east-side tree remains in contact with the building mesh and its stem match is unresolved. [Public tree inventory](https://fass-ig-web1.uoregon.edu/arcgis/rest/services/DistributedEdit/CampusTrees/FeatureServer).

Independent hash checks allow EMU and Knight Library to load or revert separately. The EMU's southwest source ID `roof-39f81f68` remains archived and covered by the canonical `roof-266f13fd` in the default scene. Both source objects and the former collision return if the EMU package fails. The five modeled approaches are reachable in the browser scene. The Hearth and Fishbowl traversals exercise the generic room and return to the default outdoor viewer. Terrain contact, runtime reports and side-by-side images are in the local research folder.
