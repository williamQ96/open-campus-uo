# Oregon Hall southeast landscape evidence

The 2012 Northeast Campus Diagnosis labels the Oregon Hall map station as J, just east of the south raised lawn. Six corresponding roof corners give a local affine registration. The center of J projects to east 42.03 m and north 16.43 m in the scene frame. The six fitted controls have 0.236 m RMS residual. The map symbol's footprint, roof overhangs and the survey scale add uncertainty beyond that fit. The result identifies the historical search area. [Northeast Campus Diagnosis, printed p. 15](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/nediagnosis_sept_17_2012rdcd2.pdf#page=19) · [Existing Oregon Hall registration](oregon-jaqua-exterior-review-2026-11-24.md) · [Registration and source hashes](../data/metadata/oregon-hall-landscape-screen-2026-11-27.json).

Four Lane County orthophotos show the main walk and south planting edge. Views from the south from 2020 and 2024 confirm the deciduous canopy, foundation shrubs and small curb-lawn sign. The 2020 view also shows a bench and receptacle by the east end. Both views from Agate show a separate green lamp and an octagonal stop sign with an ALL WAY plate. The four projectively screened views leave the map panel and its supports unresolved around historical J. [Lane County aerials](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [June 2024 south view](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=E49Lw15bT54_HqjYM59y5Q&heading=16.19) · [July 2024 east view](https://www.google.com/maps/@?api=1&map_action=pano&pano=pGAjGJZRyDKa-pxn2DaswA&heading=253.63).

The 2025 UO survey records the south retaining-wall planter. The dated views show the separate planting strip at the southeastern foundation. The existing Oregon Hall asset represents the raised planter and walks to the south entries. The next scenery pass should register the foundation bed to the wall and walk, locate the stop sign and lamp independently, and check clearance against the adjacent East 13th and Agate walking network. A clear view of the J search area from the walkway would resolve the remaining map-station question. [UO building survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2025-01/oregonhall_2025_0.pdf) · [East 13th corridor review](east-thirteenth-east-review-2026-11-27.md).

The research record contains four aerial receipts, four dated reference bearings and the historical map registration. Geometry and field measurements remain pending.
