# Living-Learning Center North exterior study, 2026-11-04

The [UO architecture history](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/living-learning-center) identifies the 2006 complex. A [2011 photograph of the north building](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Living-Learning_Center_North_(LLC_North).jpg) by Ckere, licensed [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/), was consulted for the courtyard brick, pale top story and window vocabulary. It is linked only; no photo pixels are redistributed or used as textures. Original schematic geometry and color parameters are CC BY 4.0.

A source-pinned patch recolors 76 matched vertical wall vertices to schematic brick, preserving roof and existing window colors and the original GLB. A reversible layer adds four-story windows, pale upper cladding and roof-edge trim on selected west and east outside spans. [West before](images/llc-west-before.png) / [after](images/llc-west-after.png); [east before](images/llc-east-before.png) / [after](images/llc-east-after.png). Its existing flat roof is retained. `roof-d2f88f9e` uses an approximate anchor within a LiDAR-derived outline, which can include canopy or omit courtyard structures.

The reference photograph shows a courtyard; applying its material and window vocabulary to the selected external faces is an inference. Exact facade orientation, bay sizes, color, entry, courtyard, present appearance and roof fixtures are unverified. Neither the entrance nor landscaped courtyard is reconstructed. Terrain, trees, interiors, source GLB and navigation data remain unchanged. The facade adds no collision; an ENU graph screening using conservative rectangles and 0.5 m margin found no route edges crossing them. This is not a clearance or accessibility survey. Keep level 1.

## Building mesh consolidation

This opt-in layer is merged into its unique source building mesh after checking the source pin, identity, vertex count, bounds, transforms and material contract. Geometry and colors are preserved in a non-indexed copy, while the original source mesh material and building identity remain. This avoids an additional draw call and reduces culling granularity only within that building. The overlay uses the source material after merging (roughness 0.9 and double-sided) rather than the separate detail material (roughness 0.87 and front-sided); fixed 1440×1000 views differed at 1,857 pixels in Toon and 12,553 pixels in standard rendering, including material and rasterization differences; building selection was tested. Rebuild preservation still checks the independently recorded layer and source hashes.
