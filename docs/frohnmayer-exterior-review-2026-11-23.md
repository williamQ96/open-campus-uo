## Evidence and model scope

- Compared the UO 025 outline with Oregon footprint feature 1852729, the 2024 orthophoto, and all three old scene meshes. The model uses the generalized state outline with its interior courtyard ring. Coverage of the UO outline rose from 74.43% to 93.41%. The modeled enclosed footprint is 5,295.45 m². The 909.95 m² model courtyard is open and includes draped paths and lawn. Old models occupied 18.01 m² of this court. [UO campus map](https://map.uoregon.edu/), [Oregon footprint inventory](https://www.oregon.gov/dogami/pubs/pages/dds/p-sbfo-1.1.aspx), [2024 Oregon orthophoto](https://imagery.oregonexplorer.info/arcgis/rest/services/OSIP_2024/OSIP_2024_SL/ImageServer).
- Built sixteen roof regions with lower connections, a hipped Beall roof and west pediment, arched entry bays, original west school siding, the 1948 wing's red-brick and awning frontage, the north rehearsal volume and clerestory, and the east academic wing with lower garden roofs and glazing. Historic photos and the architect's project description establish the main visible features. Window spacing on obscured faces, the terrain under roof overhangs and minor roof equipment are approximate. [Historic resource survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/frohnmayermusicbuilding06_22_07.pdf), [UO School of Music and Dance facilities and exterior photo](https://musicanddance.uoregon.edu/about/facilities), [Bora project description](https://bora.co/project/school-of-music-dance/), [UO expansion opening](https://pages.uoregon.edu/digital/uonews-archive/newly-expanded-music-building-opens-march-6.html).
- The median absolute height difference on 3,909 shared 1 m roof cells fell from 1.557 m to 0.121 m against the historical returns used to fit the roof sections. The figures describe agreement with this dated surface. A separate field survey of current heights, site paving and entry thresholds has not been conducted. [NOAA Lane County lidar](https://www.fisheries.noaa.gov/inport/item/57407).

The local default uses the unified exterior and open courtyard. The 2010 UO first-floor sheet shows a narrow central flight and two side flights at the Beall forecourt. The steps and landing now use that arrangement. The model uses approximate heights and tread dimensions, and player height follows the scene terrain grid at the forecourt. The school facilities photo supports the south wing's brick, cream trim and red awnings. [UO first-floor plan](https://pages.uoregon.edu/music/downloads/floorplan1.pdf) and [School of Music and Dance facilities](https://musicanddance.uoregon.edu/about/facilities).

The west pediment and arched entrance bays, 1948 wing, differentiated 2009 volumes, and sixteen roof regions reproduce the primary visible portions at a modeling maturity comparable to the Knight Library example.
