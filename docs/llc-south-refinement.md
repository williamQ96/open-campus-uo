# Living-Learning Center South exterior study, 2026-11-04

[UO architecture history](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/living-learning-center) identifies the 2006 complex. A photograph explicitly labeled LLC South in the [UO housing study, PDF page 171](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/uo_rhfmdstudy_101211_0.pdf), dated 2011, was consulted for its four levels, brick, pale upper surfaces and low roofline. The image is linked only, not redistributed or used as a texture. Original procedural geometry and color parameters are CC BY 4.0.

The existing `roof-453966d5` has a curated approximate anchor inside a LiDAR-derived footprint. It modeled three window levels and an exaggerated high pitched roof. The source-pinned, reversible layer adds four-level window rhythms on selected west, east, north and inner south spans, a pale upper perimeter and an illustrative low, flat roof over the existing footprint. A separate patch recolors 128 matching vertical wall vertices to schematic brick. [South before](images/llcs-south-before.png) / [after](images/llcs-south-after.png); [north before](images/llcs-north-before.png) / [after](images/llcs-north-after.png).

The photo supports a lower roofline, not exact roof geometry or dimensions. The flat approximation, bay counts, white band, wall placement and present conditions are not surveyed. Some short end faces retain generic lower windows and no specific upper windows; entrances, courtyard landscaping and accessibility are not reconstructed. No terrain, trees, interiors, base GLB or navigation data changed. The overlay adds no separate collision. Conservative ENU rectangles around the ground-level face projections screened with a 0.5 m margin found no route-graph intersections; this is not a clearance or accessibility survey. Keep level 1.

The load-time roof replacement is limited to the uniquely identified source mesh, pinned base hash, 1,664 source vertices, validated material and transforms, height threshold and exactly 162 source triangles. A mismatch aborts before replacing the source geometry. The independently recorded layer remains removable, and existing rebuild checks refuse a changed asset hash. The replacement is merged into the same building mesh and uses its material, preserving selection and the original draw-call budget; it slightly reduces separate culling granularity within the building.
