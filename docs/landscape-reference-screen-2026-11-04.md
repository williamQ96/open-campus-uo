# Landscape references for later spatial registration, 2026-11-04

Two primary UO references identify additional landscape areas outside the recent sky and furniture work. They were consulted as references only; no source image or third-party geometry was copied.

* [Gerlinger Field Green survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/gerlinger_field_green_05_10_07.pdf), 2007: describes a field bounded by Gerlinger Hall and Annex, a concrete path at the Annex, and conifers and an ivy-covered embankment beside Pioneer Cemetery. Its historical tree counts and condition should not be assumed current. The report does not register individual plant locations or paths in this project's coordinate frame. The 2024 aerial can inform broad placement, but shadows and resolution alone do not establish exact plant locations, and a field survey or registered data would be required before assigning species to modeled trees.
* [UO campus artwork tour](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/art_tour_april_2021.pdf), updated April 2021, describes the Science Walk between Cascade Hall and Deschutes Hall as a designed landscape with inlaid stone and tile beginning at Cascade Charley. The overview does not provide sufficient registered geometry, full layout, dimensions, rights to reproduce the artwork, or current condition to reconstruct its individual features. Existing science-complex footprints have known unresolved overlaps; see [science complex audit](science-and-global-footprint-audit-2026-11-04.md).

These references are leads rather than evidence of a completed scene change. No landscaping, artwork, building, collision or navigation was changed and no inventory maturity was promoted. Further visual work requires an appropriately licensed and spatially registered reference for the specific area, followed by scene and performance validation.
