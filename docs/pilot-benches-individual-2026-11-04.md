# Individual editable pilot benches, 2026-11-04

The [individual GLB](../assets/exterior-refinement/pilot-benches-individual-2026-11-04.glb) provides nine separately selectable bench groups, each containing wood and its original metal supports. It retains the same campus-space locations and pivoted transforms as the [complete combined export](pilot-benches-complete-2026-11-04.md); it does not change the runtime scene or improve its appearance. PROP-01 remains level 1. Locations and current real-world furniture, clearances, ground contact and conditions remain unverified. This does not provide a new Blender snapshot or independently editable individual boards.

The original asset and generator are CC BY 4.0 and Apache-2.0 respectively; references and attribution are in the [earlier complete-export record](pilot-benches-complete-2026-11-04.md). No third-party photo is embedded. The exporter pins the campus base, source overlay and suppression patches, checks support bounds and indices, prevents overwrites and reimports its output. All 18 meshes matched positions, normals and indices on roundtrip, and reconstructed world-space vertex coordinates matched their source exactly. SHA-256: `260bd052f3f7701963ef16a5229a1828cf73c721a06833615fdbe43126576017`.

Reproduce from `apps/web-preview` with the local Vite server on port 5173: `node scripts/export-pilot-benches-individual.mjs ../../assets/exterior-refinement/<new-file>.glb`. An automated browser comparison found an exact world-space position/normal/color vertex multiset match to the previously verified combined export and verified the nine groups and eighteen meshes. A matched visual comparison looked the same; rasterization is not bit-identical (maximum channel difference 44 on the checked rendering), so the new comparison permits small edge differences. These are export checks, not an on-site survey. The GLB is for editing and reuse; loading its separate meshes directly in the campus runtime would change draw calls and was not benchmarked.
