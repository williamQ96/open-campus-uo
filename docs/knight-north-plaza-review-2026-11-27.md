# Knight Library north forecourt and Bailey fountain

The site covers the concrete north plaza, historic fountain and planting terraces at the south end of Memorial Quadrangle. Lane County orthophotos from 2017, 2021 and 2025 locate the fountain, seating and side approaches. The university assessment identifies Frederick A. Cuthbert's terrace, cast-stone seating and fountain. It also identifies the Robert Chase Bailey memorial plaque above the basin. The university's August 2026 construction photograph shows the basin lip, three bronze panels, side piers and the low brick walls. [Historic assessment, PDF pp. 45–46, 62 and 68](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/knight_library_prelim_hp_assessment.pdf) · [Lane County imagery](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [August 2026 project update](https://news.uoregon.edu/content/get-update-construction-activity-around-university-0).

The package adds 875.78 m² of concrete, three edged planting areas and four cast-stone seats. The fountain has a shallow rectangular basin, rear wall, three patinated panels, spouts and a small Bailey nameplate. Joint spacing, hardware profiles, plaque lettering and the plant forms are representative. The basin uses a visible solid floor and the current operating state of the fountain still needs field evidence.

The university architect confirmed four cherry removals and new stair lighting in October. The tree register lists six older Shogetsu positions around the terrace, with last edits on August 17, 2026 and pruning observations from 2018. Those entries establish the historical row. The identities of the four removed trees and the layout of the replacement saplings remain unresolved. The model removes the existing inferred tree rooted in the modeled east path after screening that position against the source imagery and tree records. It retains the existing library stair flights and historic entry lanterns. The appearance and mounting locations of the new lights and finished stair alterations await dated close views. ## Local verification

Three branches add 14 graph edges around the basin and planted terraces to reach both existing stair approaches. The basin collision envelope excludes the old center link. The east–west apron and the side routes follow the registered concrete. The asset and route coverage reproduce byte for byte. The downloadable geometry contains original meshes and vertex colors.

In the research preview, desktop and phone each loaded 611 exterior IDs, 376 tracked resources and all 13 added waypoints. The west approach measures 18.43 m, east 18.12 m, stair-to-stair 62.95 m and north apron to middle terrace 34.40 m. All four routes stay within 0.13 m of registered paving and clear the new solids by at least 0.98 m. Six surface probes and four solid checks pass on both profiles. The geometry and descriptor match the staged export, and 50 served-file hashes match the local production build.
