# Knight Campus Buildings 1 and 2 exterior review

Both models meet the exterior L2 comparison used for [Knight Library](knight-library-registration-2026-11-23.md). The editable geometry uses campus-map records 402 and 403, the 2024 OSIP aerial, public architectural references and the campus terrain. The two existing selection IDs and building cards carry through to the new geometry. [UO campus map](https://map.uoregon.edu/) · [OSIP 2024](https://imagery.oregonexplorer.info/arcgis/rest/services/OSIP_2024/OSIP_2024_SL/ImageServer).

Building 1 has two tall wings, a glazed center volume, a recessed ground level, a low north pavilion and a canopy over the millrace court. Faceted screen glass covers the prominent outward elevations. The simple curtain wall faces the millrace. The Tschache–Keana crossing uses the published 190 ft span, with a 57.92 m modeled centerline, splayed arches, hangers and two ground contacts. The route under it remains open in the model. Its southern end reaches the campus-map bridge endpoint. The adjacent Lewis exterior has no corresponding existing selectable model in the scene. Building 1 roof and screen heights were estimated from photographs, and the bridge deck and arch levels use the university reference and scene grade. [Ennead project photographs](https://ennead.com/work/uo-knight/) · [Bora architecture description](https://bora.co/project/university-of-oregon-knight-campus-for-accelerating-scientific-impact/) · [UO bridge dimensions](https://knightcampus.uoregon.edu/building).

Building 2 uses the 2024 perimeter as registration evidence and the 2026 photographs for the finished exterior. It has a stepped west edge with terraces and railings, pale faceted metal shades, the long east penthouse and the separate glazed millrace bridge on a Y-shaped support. The modeled ground extents are 41.59 by 109.69 m in the east–north frame. The project reports nominal dimensions of 128 by 353 ft, or 39.01 by 107.59 m. The modeled roof and penthouse heights are 20.12 and 25.61 m above the first-level datum. These use the later stated 66 and 84 ft values. The earlier design elevations show a higher pair of roof levels and help place the window bands. Terrace slab dimensions, upper roof finishes and the modeled bridge levels are approximate. [Municipal exterior elevation A3.01](https://www.eugene-or.gov/AgendaCenter/ViewFile/Agenda/_08312022-1154#page=30) · [UO construction dimensions](https://knightcampus.uoregon.edu/building-2-update) · [UO 2026 photographs](https://knightcampus.uoregon.edu/building-2/about) · [ZGF exterior and terrace description](https://www.zgf.com/work/6689-university-of-oregon-phil-and-penny-knight-campus-for-accelerating-scientific-impact-building-2).

The campus surface raster from 2017 predates both buildings. The checked 2023 Autzen point-cloud clip ends north of the site. The comparison therefore uses registered surface outlines, published design dimensions and dated photos. The short draped millrace reach follows visible banks from the 2024 aerial. Water level and bank geometry are illustrative. [2017 surface](campus-surface-2017-2026-11-24.md) · [2023 clip metadata](https://pointcloud.org/datasets/autzen-2023/).

The 257.5 m² older candidate `roof-a79ff330` falls 93.05% within the present Building 1 envelope. The site retires it and removes the 222 anchored triangles from `context-campus-2-2`. The previous outlines, mesh and partial façade studies remain available through fallback. The shared batch retains the same vertex attributes and exact remaining index sequence.

Reviewed 23 paired toon, PBR and context views. Four upward rays hit the two modeled bridge soffits at scene elevations 9.88 m and 4.07 m. The ground-contact screen covers 7,710 route edges, with 6.942 m minimum distance to a checked route. The three Franklin road edges that intersect the bridge projection remain clear at ground level. Desktop and mobile production checks cover six walkable approaches, eight collision points, both selections and the three rollback cases. The scene census is 517 selectable models, with 443 in source batches and 139 road segments. Three separate re-exports produced the same GLB and descriptor bytes.
