# Central campus greens landscape review

Lane County orthophotos from 2017, 2021 and 2025, the campus plan and dated ground views register the Women's Memorial Quad, Gerlinger Entrance Green and Straub Hall Green at University Street. The 2026 Susan Campbell assessment confirms that the curved Quad paths were rebuilt in 2021–22 and records the surviving stone benches and terrace wall. [Lane County imagery](https://lcmaps.lanecounty.org/arcgis/rest/services/AirPhotos/AirPhotos/MapServer) · [2022 campus plan](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/principle_12_design_area_special_conditions_2022.pdf) · [Susan Campbell assessment, PDF pp. 6, 8 and 39–40](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2026-02/susan-campbell-hall_preliminary-hp-assessment_2026_sm.pdf).

| Site | Local geometry |
| --- | --- |
| Women’s Memorial Quad | Thirteen walk traces reproduce the post-2022 curved pavement, the two diagonals and the Knight Library Axis link. The terrace has about 33 m of brick and cast stone wall, a bowed central section, two three-tread flights and six cast stone benches. Step dimensions and individual bench positions are estimates. |
| Gerlinger Entrance Green | Registered the oval lawn and curb, horseshoe drive, four walking segments, planting crescent and observed parking marks. The chain and posts use the historic record and an estimated spacing. |
| Straub Hall Green | Registered nine walks, diagonal crossings, planting pockets, a bicycle rack pad and eight hoops, an entrance sign, a low memorial plaque and a representative senior bench. The Class of 1910 bench and Stafford plaque use the historic survey with estimated dimensions and locations. |

The package adds 30 trees at campus inventory coordinates. Crown heights and shapes are estimates informed by the recorded heights and available lidar. It suppresses 54 procedural trees and four shrubs where the new walks, lawn or registered trees occupy their positions. The underlying building entries and geometry are retained. [UO tree inventory](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/trees) · [Gerlinger Entrance Green survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/gerlinger_entrance_green_05_07_07.pdf) · [Straub Hall Green survey](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/straub_hall_green_06_12_07.pdf).

The University Street crossing adds the observed marking bars, local asphalt and two approximate curb ramps between the Knight Library Axis and Straub's northern approach. The added navigation graph contains 63 edges. In the paired geometry review, the modeled route from the Quad to the Straub west approach is 248.58 m, compared with 310.76 m in the prior graph. Both flights have three increasing standing heights. The route test checks the registered connections and clearance around the modeled site barriers.

The mapped Straub sculpture site contains a 25 m² platform and a simplified interpretive plate. The sculpture geometry is pending a reusable reference with permission to reproduce it. The 2026 assessment records removal of the Pioneer Mother figure in 2020, and the present-day Quad model represents the terrace wall and plantings. [Akbar’s Garden record](https://expo.uoregon.edu/spotlight/history-uo-architecture/feature/akbar-s-garden) · [2023 site confirmation](https://cpfm.uoregon.edu/sites/default/files/2023-08/record_07_28_23.pdf).

Independent exports matched byte for byte. Before release, the local production browser loaded the site with 610 modeled exterior IDs, 457 entrances and 139 registered corridor records. Research views and source receipts are retained under `exterior-l2/central-axes-landscape-20261127/`. [Release history](../DEVELOPMENT_LOG.md#2026-11-27---release-expanded-outdoor-l2-landscapes).
