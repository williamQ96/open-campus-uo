# Open Courtyard

Space leads. A heavy geometric enclosure leaves one deliberate opening: a threshold rather than a sealed perimeter. The empty interior carries as much weight as the ink around it. This is a meticulously crafted balance of presence and permission, with every radius and interruption optically adjusted.

The palette is architectural: forest ink against mineral grey and paper. Colour describes structure, never decoration. Small changes in material value make the central void feel accessible, while the dark perimeter holds the composition with quiet confidence.

The rhythm comes from a repeated unit, interrupted once. A detached square acts as a coordinate marker and a promise of continuation. The geometry suggests a courtyard, a floor plan and a letterform without illustrating a particular institution. The reference should be felt before it is identified.

Type is sparse and direct. The name alone sits beside the mark, with painstaking attention to spacing and baseline. The result should feel like the product of deep expertise: exceptionally clean at a small scale, composed enough to occupy an entire page, and independent of any slogan.

Refinement removes. Edges, clear space and proportion are revisited until nothing feels accidental. Master-level execution resides in those small relationships, allowing the same identity to remain legible on a browser tab, a map and a publication.
